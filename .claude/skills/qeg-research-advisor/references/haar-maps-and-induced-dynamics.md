# Haar maps, induced dynamics and reserved observations

Use these lessons when a static section, gauge choice, conditional expectation or change of variables is proposed as a physical dynamic reduction. They are demonstrated by Yang–Mills Workbench Round22 P1/P2 and apply only after checking the new problem's hypotheses.

## Separate the map obligations

State the actual source and target measures, invariant sectors, cores, form/operator domains and physical observables. A pointwise identity on smooth functions is not an L2 bound. Prove the measure transformation or an isometry estimate before extending a map to equivalence classes. Do not evaluate arbitrary L2 representatives on a null section.

A genuine tree gauge fixing retains all independent chord variables. In the tested18-vertex33-link graph, a spanning tree fixes17 links and leaves16 chords. Fixing30 links additionally constrained13 physical cycles. Source orbit formulas for a genuine tree did not justify that larger restriction. For the proper map, fixing tree coordinates makes each independent chord change a left/right Haar translation; Fubini and invariant density establish the unitary identification.

Test stronger topology claims at their actual scale. Normalized characters on one fixed loop gave L2 nonclosability. Three edge-disjoint character loops gave an electric graph-norm null sequence at fixed alpha/E_star while the restricted image stayed one. Edge disjointness, Casimir growth and normalization were essential; two factors did not give the claimed graph-null sequence. Do not extend this obstruction to every possible reduction or stronger Sobolev topology.

## Transport all dynamics and close the domains

Differentiate the original generator before imposing coordinates. Under a tree map, every tree-link derivative acts on all relevant chord endpoint paths. A chord internal to the cut can acquire a conjugation derivative even though it does not cross the cut. Squares of sums contain mixed derivatives; dropping them changes the operator. Translate source path/gauge conventions explicitly and verify them with noncommuting fixtures.

An isometry alone supplies neither an intertwiner nor a reducing image. Prove a commuting semigroup/spectral projection or a closed-form decomposition, then identify the operator domain. On the tested electric endpoint, averaging unused original chord links commuted with every link heat semigroup. Positive finite Peter–Weyl blocks, compared to the product Casimir, gave H1 form and H2 operator domains. This supported exact graph-norm and common-clock intertwining with the induced generator.

State exactly which Hamiltonian closes. Reduction for H0 does not establish reduction after a new interaction is added. Check the off-diagonal block of the new operator; preserve the original state/domain questions. No magnetic, homogeneous, representation or continuum transfer follows merely from matching coordinate names.

## Preserve the physical completion and the reserve

Two functions with identical section values can be different physical observables. The tested old four-link face and new six-link tree loop both restricted to x, but their electric energies differed. The old clock coefficient therefore could not be reused. Freeze the full pullback observable, common alpha/E_star and hbar, training data and held-out data together.

An exact map to an induced generator is distinct from matching a chosen conditional family. In P2, one training slope fixed c while leaving the affine mobility parameter unresolved. Reserved curvature narrowed that parameter, and the reserved full-time channel still rejected the family. Do not refit the training coefficient from reserved information. Distinguish compatibility testing from measured physical calibration, and finite-family rejection from unrestricted model rejection.

Retain the divergence drift, both complex means, adjoint convention and ground-compatible scalar shifts. A scalar correction cannot repair a mismatch merely by being renamed a ground energy.

## Demand a discriminating control

Execute the tempting wrong model and show a nonzero difference on a valid channel. A single trace failed to distinguish an omitted internal-chord term in the tested fixture; an invariant product channel did distinguish it. Preserve the failed candidate and the replacement. A pre-freeze independent improvement is not a post-freeze shared correction; neither the failed control nor a replay adds a research loop.
