# Reading ledger template

Copy an entry for each primary work or paper. Keep “not yet checked” visible rather than filling a gap from memory.

## Document identity

- Title, authors, collaborators:
- Archive or publisher:
- Stable identifier, DOI, patent number, or arXiv version:
- Date of composition, submission, publication, and later revision:
- Edition, translator, language, page or folio:
- URL and date accessed:
- Readable scan, transcription, translation, catalogue-only, or unavailable:
- Rights/access note:

## What the source establishes

- Research question or historical purpose:
- Definitions and assumptions:
- Central argument or calculation:
- Method and data provenance:
- Exact figure/table/proposition supporting the conclusion:
- Measurement, simulation, theorem, proposal, patent claim, or interpretation:
- Uncertainty, controls, convergence, and model limitations:
- Strongest conclusion supported by this evidence:
- What is often claimed beyond that evidence:
- Conflicting or follow-up sources:

## My work

- Prerequisites I need to repair:
- Derivation reproduced independently:
- One figure or result I can reproduce:
- Software, versions, data, and hardware required:
- Observed discrepancy or failed attempt:
- A test that could change my conclusion:
- Next three documents to read:
- Review date and reviewer feedback:

For a historical source, explicitly separate original wording, translator choices, editorial interpretation, and the modern scientific assessment. For a recent paper, distinguish first submission, revised preprint, journal acceptance, publication, and corrections.
