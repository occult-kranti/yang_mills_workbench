# Sources and open-source reuse

The site includes original roadmap annotations and small formula-based browser calculations. It does not package complete third-party books, videos, papers, archive scans, or scientific software distributions.

Each chapter and resource links to its source. External full texts remain subject to their publishers’ and custodians’ access and reuse terms. “Free to read” does not automatically mean permission to redistribute. In particular, the linked Caltech Feynman Lectures edition has its own usage restrictions. Project Gutenberg availability is jurisdiction-dependent. Archive catalogues may expose metadata without the complete underlying text.

The optional examples import NumPy, SciPy, and Matplotlib from the user’s environment. Project briefs direct readers to official tutorials for QuTiP, Qiskit, Meep, FEniCSx, FiPy, LAMMPS, Quantum ESPRESSO, Kwant, OpenMC, REBOUND, Astropy, PlasmaPy, Scikit-HEP, and related tools. Their software and dependency licenses remain with their respective projects. Check the exact release’s installation, citation, and contribution instructions.

The paper refresher uses the public arXiv API for metadata, with a bounded query size, request spacing, caching, and no background polling. Original papers are linked at arXiv and, when verified, their publisher DOI pages. Metadata and scientific conclusions remain attributable to the listed authors and sources.

The hosted paper service vendors the unmodified SAX 1.6.1 parser. Its Blue Oak Model License 1.0.0 is preserved in worker/vendor/SAX-LICENSE and bundled into the deployment.
