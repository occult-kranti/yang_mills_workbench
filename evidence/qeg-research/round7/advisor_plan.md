## Four technical work packages

1. **Classify every freedom.** N and K are regulator labels; nK is quadrature resolution; mu_RG is a subtraction scale; phi is a new degree of freedom. Give each its equation or matching prescription. Rename the interpolation index used in the renormalization notes if implemented: its nu is not the finite scalar mass ratio nu.
2. **Close energy before coupling geometry.** Derive both scalar force and Maxwell exchange from the same action. Preserve the fixed magnetic energy's scalar dependence even when the magnetic field is held constant. Test zero frequency and the invariant zero-scalar branch separately from nonzero exchange.
3. **Close the classical stress independently.** Evolve both spatial Einstein equations without resetting the Hamiltonian constraint. Compare conserved-flux recovery and a separately written skeptical formulation. Distinguish shared-RHS comparisons from independent equations.
4. **Regress the full target into missing calculations.** Ask what quantum functional, state, subtraction, matching and estimates would suffice. Use the finite reviewed graph to find which obligations are actually met. Keep proposed connecting edges out of executable proof rules until justified.

## Prioritized next experiments

### P1. Common causal quantum stress in prescribed axial geometry

Start with a prescribed smooth axial metric and one specified positive gauge-kinetic function. Derive the tetrad Dirac equation, evolving physical longitudinal momentum and magnetic/Landau basis. Specify an admissible initial two-point state. Compute the quantum current and both directional stresses from the same regulated mode representation and allowed local subtraction.

Forward input: the present exchange signs and independently tested geometric constraint identity. Backward requirement: a quantum force Ward residual calculated from independently evaluated current and pressures. The bridge is accepted only if this residual tends to zero with independently varied time, quadrature and subtraction errors. Do not define a pressure by solving the Ward identity being tested.

Reject when the state is unspecified, a fixed-b basis is reused in changing B without its extra terms, or the current and stress use incompatible finite counterterms. Deliver the operator equations and regulator contract before writing a coupled Einstein integrator.

### P2. Reference subtraction at fixed physical normalization

Use one physical normalization condition at a fixed reference field, state and momentum prescription. Derive all corresponding action variations. Study the combined current S-C xprime+D x^2, energy and pressures, not just the separated C coefficient. Vary N and K along multiple cofinal paths and separately converge nK.

Reject a convergence claim if changing N,K also retunes scalar initial data, changes the measured charge, or changes the physical source. An unbounded bare coefficient is not automatically forbidden, but the finite renormalized observables and Ward identities must be established.

### P3. Weak-field covariant overlap benchmark

Use the local Euler-Heisenberg expansion only where field amplitudes, derivatives and curvature satisfy its low-energy truncation assumptions. Vary the action before inserting its anisotropic stress. Compare with the quantum calculation in that overlap. A failure tests the normalization or truncation; success does not validate critical electric fields or ultrastrong-field pair production.

### P4. Self-consistent semiclassical evolution

Only after P1-P3, couple the common observables to geometry. Determine a controlled treatment of higher derivatives, constraints, renormalization freedom and state/noise effects. Monitor all independent conservation residuals, regulator errors and approximation estimates. A PINN may later approximate this specified system; it cannot supply missing constitutive equations.
