# Independent review of the finite proof planner

Review date: 9 September 2026. Scope: the actual Horn-rule search, its certificate, the selected-regulator dependency chain and the limits of the software's claims. The mathematical advisor and critic separately review the analytical Maxwell–Dirac and Bianchi-I proofs.

## Decision and trusted boundary

**Accepted within the stated finite-library scope: all 19 independent gates and the re-executed 24 developer tests passed.** The revised implementation alternates real forward fact-set and backward goal-regression frontiers, requires every conjunctive premise, reconstructs a meeting into a forward rule sequence, and independently certifies least rule cost by uniform-cost search. The final executed gate status and exact source hashes are recorded in `search_independent_review.json`.

The software verifies a **derivation from admitted rules**. It does not parse or prove the mathematics in `proof_ref`, and a reviewer label is trusted metadata. The proof's mathematical content rests on the full equations, inequalities and ODE theorem applications in `theorem_advisor.md`, together with `proof_critique.md`. No Lean kernel certificate or continuum quantum-gravity theorem has been produced.

## Findings that changed the delivered implementation

The original selected-theorem path assumed `positive_margin`, while a separate scenario derived that atom and then stopped. This did not connect the selected constants to the final theorem. The combined `full_selected_regulator` scenario now removes the margin seed and includes `PM01 → PM02 → R01 → ... → selected_theorem`. Its proof costs fourteen rule applications in the frozen library; the generic conditional theorem route costs twelve, and the standalone margin route costs two. These are different queries, not three estimates of one physical quantity.

Three adversarial probes found certificate-integrity defects: a seeded theorem could hide an unretained conjectural prerequisite in `assumption_ids`; a theorem seed marked `unverified` was accepted; and a loaded library's rule dictionary could be mutated while retaining the old hash. The revised loader rejects the two seed cases. Immutable mappings and digest verification protect the validated rule library during replay. The earlier observations are retained in `search_independent_initial_findings.json`, including the limitation that the initial inline probe did not record a source hash. The later hash-stamped acceptance runs occurred after repair.

The final metadata review also corrects an ambiguous regulator label: the selected Landau cutoff is **n=0,...,4 inclusive**, meaning five Landau levels, with a separate finite longitudinal quadrature. The derived positive-margin atom is a theorem in the combined scenario, rather than an unexplained input assumption. Its source points to the actual analytic certificate.

The causal-response node must retain its preparation conditions. Identical initial data and source histories imply identical solutions until their histories differ. A delayed source perturbation gives zero earlier tangent response when the initial tangent is zero. Arbitrary parameter-dependent initial data do not satisfy that latter condition automatically. The node states this conditional consequence explicitly.

## What was independently executed

The independent script re-executes the developer's unit suite and then runs its own acceptance cases without importing those test helpers. The main fixture has an actual intermediate meeting: forward cost seven and backward cost five. Its backward obligations are a subset of the forward facts, and both sides contribute nonempty derivation segments. This is not a forward solution with a backward label added afterwards.

The cost-nine direct route versus cost-two two-step route test supplies a real nonoptimal first meeting. The final certificate returns two. A shared-lemma example charges its common premise once, yielding total cost four. An independent subset-state dynamic program, which uses no priority queue or production search routine, agrees with the planner on sixty-four deterministic small Horn libraries, including multiple goals and unsupported cycles.

Other gates attack missing conjunctions, reversed implications, undisclosed assumptions, unverified seeds, post-validation mutation, and stale saved results. Reaching the forward pop budget returns `incomplete`, not non-derivability. Exhausting the backward candidate budget can still leave a valid independently obtained forward certificate; the result preserves the candidate-search status. Budget parameters bound popped states per pass, not total memory, generated nodes or wall-clock runtime.

The final run passed nineteen acceptance gates, including a fresh execution of all twenty-four developer tests. Source hashes and executed output are in the JSON companion. The acceptance script is `verify_proof_search_independent.py` and can be rerun with the Python standard library.

## Remaining limits relevant to interpretation

The all-potential margin is an analytical finite-regulator statement. The search atom does not turn floating-point trajectory errors into interval bounds or prove the regulator can be removed. The exact quadrature-family certificate and the separate rounded-coefficient certificate must retain their respective definitions.

The `physical_infinite_quantumgravity` target remains explicitly blocked. Its status reflects the supplied research library's missing accepted bridge, not a proof that the target is impossible. The planner's least-cost result ranges only over the admitted finite ground rules and their declared costs. It has no MM midpoint theorem, no NBS expansion guarantee and no optimality claim over all possible mathematical proofs. Every current result still carries the declared model hypotheses shown in the query, even when no conjectural hypothesis is needed.
