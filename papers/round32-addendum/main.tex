\begin{abstract}
This interim edition covers sub-round~1 (AV1, AV2) of a ten-investigation
cycle; later sections are added only as their reviewed gates land. The model is
the zero-selected, nonuniform AQ family of SU(2) Kogut--Susskind form at fixed
lattice spacing, with its infinite-volume subsequential states and physical
energy generator, at the original coupling cap $|\tau|\le10^{-8}$. AV1 replaces
the Round31 square-root local state bound by one linear in $\tau$. Ordering the
commuting creation-operator expansion of each finite-volume ground vector so
that creations meeting the 48-link Wilson cover act last makes the
volume-dependent outside normalization cancel exactly. Two separately proved
inequalities then bound the trace distance between the reduced density on the
cover and its Haar reference, uniformly in the volume, after removal of the
on-site cutoff and for every AQ subsequential limit. The exact-first-order tier
gives $D\approx1.3612\times10^{-8}$ at both signs, about $5.9\times10^{4}$
times below the Round31 bound; the crude tier, $D\approx2.368\times10^{-5}$,
misses its preregistered target and is retained. AV2 replaces the Poisson
kernel by a frozen $C^2$ window that equals $e^{-sx}$ on the nonnegative
spectrum and whose Fourier transform has finite first moment $4s/\pi$, so no
real-time cutoff or tail remains. At the unchanged cap and $s=1$ the centered
Euclidean Wilson correlation lies within $1.832\times10^{-7}$ of an exact
rational datum. This meets the $10^{-6}$ point-error target that Round31 missed
at this coupling. The Poisson route remains insufficient at every cutoff. The
free value $e^{-3}/4$ lies inside the certified interval, so no
interaction-induced shift is resolved. These are analytic upper certificates
for one patterned fixed-spacing model and one observable. They establish
neither uniform Wilson theory nor a continuum Yang--Mills construction.
\end{abstract}
