\begin{abstract}
This edition records the complete Round32 cycle: ten prospectively contracted
investigations in five sub-rounds of two (AV1--AZ2), all accepted within
their stated model scope. Each result is a fixed-spacing statement about a
named SU(2) Kogut--Susskind model at the original coupling cap
$|\tau|\le10^{-8}$, or about a named finite graph. In the zero-selected,
nonuniform AQ family, AV1 replaces the Round31 square-root local state bound
by one linear in $\tau$: the trace distance on the 48-link Wilson cover is at
most $D\approx1.3612\times10^{-8}$ in every volume and for every AQ
subsequential limit. AV2 replaces the Poisson kernel by a $C^2$ window, and
the centered Euclidean Wilson correlation at $s=1$ then lies within about
$1.832\times10^{-7}$ of an exact datum, meeting the $10^{-6}$ target that
Round31 missed at the cap. AW1 proves Peter--Weyl parity and a link-flip lemma
and finds the static Wilson mean $+\tau/144$ up to $K_2^+\tau^2$,
$K_2^+\approx3354.80$. AW2 encloses that mean at the rule-fixed coupling
$\tau=10^{-8}$ in about $[6.911,\,6.978]\times10^{-11}$, mirrored at
$-10^{-8}$, so its sign is certified. AX1 re-derives the admitted chain by
route~B for the uniform Kogut--Susskind Hamiltonian at fixed spacing and
strong bare coupling, $g^4=9.6\times10^9$ at the cap, and AX2 certifies its
Euclidean node with radius about $1.912\times10^{-7}$. AY1 shows that any two
subsequential limits of two named construction families lie within
$2D\approx2.72\times10^{-8}$ of each other on the cover, share one explicit
first-order density and differ beyond it by at most $2K_2'\tau^2$,
$K_2'\approx13418$. AY2 lists six unproved obligations and certifies a static
two-sided distance of every limit from the Haar product on the cover, about
$4.38$ to $4.41\times10^{-10}$. AZ1 states that along a trajectory with
$g_n\to0$ the family supplies one necessary estimate, the volume-uniform gap
$g^2/(32a)$ at fixed $a$ for $g^4\ge9.6\times10^9$, whose lattice-unit floor
$a\Delta\ge1250\sqrt6\approx3062$ excludes a finite-mass limit inside the
admitted regime, and none uniform along $a_n\to0$. AZ2, on the Round11
two-plaquette graph with both faces coupled, a different finite model, finds
the Wilson derivative $1/6$ exactly, a zero second-order coefficient and the
third-order coefficient $-187/33696$, with sign-certified enclosures; under
the dictionary $\tau_{\rm FG}=\tau/24$ the value $1/6$ is consistent with
$1/144$, a consistency check only. The free reference lies inside both
Euclidean intervals, so no shift of the centered correlation is resolved.
These are analytic certificates for fixed-spacing models and one finite
graph. They establish no state uniqueness, no estimate uniform in the lattice
spacing and no continuum construction. The four-dimensional continuum
Yang--Mills problem remains open.
\end{abstract}
