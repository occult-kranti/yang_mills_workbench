\paragraph{Process contributions.}
The cycle also produced four process records. They are methods of working,
not scientific results, and they count as zero investigations.
\begin{itemize}\raggedright
\item \emph{The pre-registration block} (\recid{hnm-r32-prereg-v1}), frozen
 and hashed in every contract before any outcome: the model identifier,
 selected triple, coupling and signs, state provenance, observable with its
 centering and reference route, nodes, itemized error terms, target, outcome
 types, allowed sub-labels, claim exclusions and required control ids, with a
 control list that must equal the contract's (Section~\ref{sec:plan}).
\item \emph{The sentence template.} From sub-round~4 on, a contract may fix a
 \recid{mandatory_sentence_template}, the gate fields it requires and, for
 AZ1 and AZ2, forbidden phrasings; the producer quotes the template verbatim
 and the gate fills it with reviewed constants
 (Sections~\ref{sec:az1-sentence} and~\ref{sec:az2-sentence}).
\item \emph{The two vocabulary extensions} recorded in the plan after the
 Jung assistant-2 and assistant-4 audits (Section~\ref{sec:history-loops}),
 with AX1, AY1 and AY2 grandfathered and their contracts unamended.
\item \emph{The tools} of Section~\ref{sec:history-tools}: the freezing tool,
 the contract freezer, the gate recorder and the Arb cross-check, together
 with the skeptic's replay tools \sourcepath{replay_loop.py} and
 \sourcepath{replay_declared.py}. None of them computes a scientific result.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Evidence records.}
The immutable decision records are the ten gates listed above. Each binds its
contract, the frozen producer reports, checkers, input snapshots and outputs,
and the skeptic's review evidence. Fresh normal and optimized replays
reproduce every producer output byte for byte, and gate-specific method
snapshots preserve the instructions actually used, so later guidance cannot
rewrite an earlier admission. This addendum's builder refuses to run until
all ten gates exist and verifies every gate binding. Its build receipt binds
the scientific and authoring sources to the PDF, and a separate QA record
binds the page geometry, the reference resolution and the review of every
rendered page to the PDF's digest. From the repository root:
\begin{quote}\small\ttfamily
python -B papers/round32-addendum/build.py -{}-render\\
python -B papers/round32-addendum/build.py -{}-check
\end{quote}
A hash or a passing program does not replace the stated mathematical
hypotheses, and the release checks are distinct from the page review.

Exactly ten investigations are closed. The deliberation loops, the assistant
packages, the lens updates, repairs, replays, this manuscript and publication
add no research loop. Draft03, the Round31 addendum and every earlier
scientific verdict are preserved.
