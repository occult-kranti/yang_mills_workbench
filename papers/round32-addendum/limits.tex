\section{What the Round32 record does and does not establish}
\label{sec:limits}

All ten Round32 gates record the verdict \recid{accepted_within_scope}, and
each acceptance holds only inside the model its gate names. The record uses
three models, and they are kept apart:
\begin{itemize}
\item the zero-selected, nonuniform AQ family at fixed spacing (AV1, AV2, AW1,
 AW2, AY1 and AY2), with the selected triple exactly $(0,0,0)$, the Haar
 reference and the cover $R=\{0,e_z\}$;
\item the uniform Kogut--Susskind SU(2) model at fixed spacing and strong bare
 coupling, $g^4\ge9.6\times10^9$ at the cap, handled by route~B (AX1, AX2 and
 AZ1);
\item the Round11 two-plaquette graph with both faces coupled (AZ2), a
 different finite model.
\end{itemize}
A result proved in one model is not a claim about another. AX1 re-derived
the admitted chain for the uniform model; it did not import it. AZ2 transfers
nothing to either lattice model. Table~\ref{tab:limits-gates} lists, for each
gate, its model, its sub-labels and the claims its frozen contract excludes.
Every contract also carries the seven preregistered exclusions: no
interaction claim when the free reference lies inside an enclosure; no
uniqueness of the AQ state, or of any subsequential limit in the wording of
AZ1 and AZ2; no whole-sequence convergence or rate in $N$; no continuum or
weak coupling; no transfer from a finite graph; no static shift relabelled
as dynamical; and no scientific priority.

\paragraph{No shift of the centered correlation.}
AV2 and AX2 certify the centered Euclidean Wilson correlation at the single
node $s=1$, with radii about $1.832\times10^{-7}$ and $1.912\times10^{-7}$
against the target $10^{-6}$. In both, the free value $e^{-3}/4$ lies inside
the interval, so no shift, sign or coefficient of $C(s)$ is resolved, and
AW1 proves that its first-order shift vanishes. The only interaction effect
certified in the $\mathbb Z^3$ models is the sign of the uncentered, static
Wilson mean at $\tau=\pm10^{-8}$ in the zero-selected family (AW2). It is an
equal-time property of the states, not a dynamical correction, mass shift,
susceptibility or correlation shift, and it is not certified in the uniform
model: AX1 transfers only the first-order coefficient $+\tau/144$, not a
remainder constant. The AY2 two-sided tier, which puts
$\norm{\rho_R-P_R}_1$ between about $4.379\times10^{-10}$ and
$4.405\times10^{-10}$ for every limit, is also static.

\paragraph{No uniqueness.}
AY1 proves that any two subsequential limits of the two named construction
families, at the same coupling, lie within $2D\approx2.72\times10^{-8}$ of
each other in trace norm on $R$, share one explicit first-order density, and
differ by at most $2K_2'\tau^2\approx2.7\times10^{-12}$ beyond it, with
$K_2'\approx13418$. That is closeness on one fixed region at one coupling. It
is not uniqueness, whole-sequence convergence, translation invariance, a rate
in $N$ or boundary independence of the dynamics. AY2 lists what remains
unproved, and Table~\ref{tab:limits-obligations} summarizes its obligations
table (Table~\ref{tab:ay2-obligations} gives the full rows). AY2 also records
a falsifying scenario that the present bounds cannot exclude: two admissible
densities that agree to first order and differ at second.

\paragraph{Nothing uniform in \texorpdfstring{$a$}{a}.}
Every lattice result holds at one fixed spacing. AZ1 names the one necessary
estimate that the fixed-spacing family supplies along a trajectory: the
volume-uniform gap $\alpha/16=g^2/(32a)$ at fixed $a$, for
$g^4\ge9.6\times10^9$. Every trajectory with $g_n\to0$ leaves that regime
after finitely many steps, so the family supplies no estimate uniform along
$a_n\to0$ on such a trajectory. Inside the regime the lattice-unit floor
$a\Delta\ge g^2/32\ge1250\sqrt6\approx3061.86$ holds, so the certified gap is
a cutoff-scale quantity, and a finite physical mass cannot be approached
inside the admitted regime. That is the failure of a sufficient certificate,
not a statement that the gap closes. The requirements of a continuum
construction are all missing (Table~\ref{tab:az1-requirements}): estimates
uniform in $a$, reconstruction hypotheses and physical scale control,
together with the AY2 obligations and nontriviality. A rate in $N$ would not
be a rate in $a$.

\paragraph{Nothing transferred from the finite graph.}
AZ2's exact coefficients belong to the two-face model on the Round11 graph.
The derivative $1/6$ corresponds to $1/144$ under $\tau_{\mathrm{FG}}=\tau/24$, which is a
consistency check expected from the shared normalization and nothing more.
The zero second-order coefficient is a finite-graph centre-flip fact. Nothing
about the AQ model, its subsequential limits, $K_2$, $K_2'$ or a $\mathbb Z^3$
shift follows from the graph, and roadmap goal~2 is not resolved by it. The
one-face variant, which the contract text also admits, is a different finite
model; it is not admitted, and it differs at third order.

\paragraph{Standing of the evidence.}
Every admission uses exact rational arithmetic with directed rounding; the
decimals in this addendum are previews, and the Arb and mpmath comparisons
never admit a value. The producers, the skeptic, the advisor, the lenses and
the assistants are model agents with correlated ancestry. Their agreement is
separate model-agent work, never external human peer review or formal
verification, and in several loops the premises already printed the headline
values. Contract wording defects were recorded as non-blocking by the gates
and did not change any frozen text. Inherited premises (I1, AL1,
AM2, AQ1, AQ2, AT4 and the Round11 graph) are cited, not re-proved, and the
standard theorems of \cite{standard} are cited, not machine-checked.

\paragraph{Attribution and priority.}
No scientific priority is claimed or verified for any Round32 result. The
attribution sentence fixed by the skeptic's Gauvin version-diff record and
adopted in the plan freeze stands unchanged: the AM2 creation-expansion gap
and the AQ construction follow strategies present in Gauvin
arXiv:2503.15539 \cite{gauvin} from v2 (2026-09-11; absent from v1 of
2025-03), which itself credits the creation-operator method to Bravyi,
DiVincenzo and Loss (2008) \cite{bdl}; the SU(2) selected-reference
application and its explicit constants are this project's record; scientific
priority is unverified and no independence is claimed. The claimed
reflection-positive SU($N$) construction of Faizal and Shabir \cite{faizal}
remains an unaudited lead, read at abstract depth only; nothing in Round32
depends on it.
