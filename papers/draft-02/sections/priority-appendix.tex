It does not rank scientific priority, assign discovery scores or estimate how much of continuum Yang--Mills has been solved. All entries are scoped to their stated model. New Round29 findings are recorded separately with their gate verdicts; a recently executed loop does not outrank a prior result merely by recency. Limitations describe each named result at its own gate; separately gated current extensions may close a listed obligation only under their own model and state assumptions.
