\begin{abstract}
This addendum records the Round33 cycle: eight prospectively contracted
investigations in three research sub-rounds and an applications stage, all
accepted within their stated model scope. They are the paired BA1, BA2, BB1,
BB2 and BD1, the statement investigation BC1 and the single-direction
investigations BC2 and BD2. The first five
concern the zero-selected patterned family of the SU(2)
Kogut--Susskind form at fixed spacing and the original coupling cap
$|\tau|\le10^{-8}$, with the two named construction families of Round32:
F1, AQ1's centered whole-star boxes, and F2, I1's all-contained-face boxes
with padding, both on $\Lambda_N=[-N,N]^3$, $N\ge2$. BA1 shows that
replacing one box of the named constructions by another moves the AM2
creation coefficients on supports meeting the Wilson cover $R=\{0,e_z\}$ by at
most $Kq^{N-1}$ in the anchored norm, where $N$ is the smaller box size, in
each on-site cutoff space and uniformly in the cutoff: $K=49/111790368\approx
4.383\times10^{-7}$ at $q=1/64$, and $K=49/2018304\approx2.428\times10^{-5}$
at the per-step ball ratio $q=37888|\tau|=148/390625$, for every comparison of
the contract and both signs. It is proved by two routes, a weighted-norm
contraction and analyticity on a complex-coupling disc. This is decay of coefficients
at a rate in $N$, not decay of the reduced density. BA2 shows that the
finite-box Heisenberg evolutions of F1 and F2 of every $A\in B(\mathcal H_R)$
differ in norm by at most $K_{\rm cmp}(5N+1)N^{-3}\norm A$, with
$K_{\rm cmp}=3969/155720800000000\approx2.549\times10^{-11}$, for
$|\theta|\le8$ in $\theta=\alpha t/\hbar$. The evolutions of each family are
Cauchy in $N$ at the rate $1/(N-1)$, with constants about
$5.947\times10^{-11}$ (F1) and $9.452\times10^{-11}$ (F2), and the F2 limit
dynamics equals the AQ1 limit dynamics. This closes two obligations left
open by Round32, boundary independence of the dynamics on compact windows and
the dynamics of the padded family, within that scope. Both BA2 constants are
quadratic in $\tau$. The BA2 statements concern the algebraic dynamics of the
named constructions on a compact window. They assert no equality of GNS
dynamics or of correlation functions of different states and nothing uniform
in time. BB1 turns coefficient decay into locality of the reduced densities.
For each of five comparisons of the named constructions, among them the
fixed-$N$ change of boundary prescription and two volumes of one
prescription, the reduced densities of the finite-box ground vectors on $R$
differ in trace norm by at most $Cq^{N-1}$ with $q=1/64$ and
$C\approx8.905\times10^{-7}$. On every finite region $Y\subset\Lambda_N$ they
differ by at most $c_{\rm site}|Y|e^{|Y|/10^8}q^{d_Y}$ with
$c_{\rm site}\approx8.768\times10^{-7}$. The bounds hold in each on-site
cutoff space and, at fixed $N$, for the untruncated ground vectors. They are
proved by a hard-core polymer expansion with a Kotecký--Preiss condition,
whose cited criterion was checked after the freeze against a committed
excerpt, and by an iterated product-ordering split. BB1 is static and per
comparison. BB2 uses BB1's frozen targets as hypotheses, which the BB1 gate
discharges item by item. The reduced densities of F1 and F2 then converge as
whole sequences, without compactness, on every finite region: the supremum
over $M>N$ of the difference on $R$ is at most $C'q^{N-1}$ with
$C'=4/984375\approx4.06\times10^{-6}$. They converge to one common limit, the
limit of the named constructions. That limit equals every AQ1 and every F2
subsequential limit, inherits exactly their admitted properties, and is
invariant under coarse translations. Its correlation functions on
$|\theta|\le8$ are the limits of the finite-box ones. The correlation bound
carries an explicit rate only for $5\le N\le14000$ and is vacuous from
$N=14419$. BC1 restates two Round32 certificates for that one state with
every constant unchanged: the node certificate for the centered Euclidean
Wilson correlation at $s=1$, whose interval still contains the free
reference $e^{-3}/4$, and the enclosure
$[\tau/144-K_2^+\tau^2,\tau/144+K_2^+\tau^2]$ of the static Wilson mean. The
whole-sequence bound then certifies the sign of the static Wilson mean of the
untruncated padded boxes for every $N\ge N_{\rm sign}=4$, at both signs of
$\tau$; the boxes with $N=2,3$ stay open, and no finite-box node is claimed.
BC1 also splits the Round32 obligation ``uniqueness of the limit'': the
coincidence of all subsequential limits of the two named families is closed
within the BB2 scope, and uniqueness of any ground state stays open. BC2
concerns the uniform Kogut--Susskind SU(2) model at fixed spacing and strong
bare coupling, in which every face carries the coupling, handled by
Round32's route~B. It re-derives the chain of BA1, BB1 and BB2 with the
route-B constants (per-site sum $29|\tau|$, 52 first-order faces and 5
interaction groups per site): coefficient decay with
$K_B=13/27941256\approx4.653\times10^{-7}$, locality with
$C_B\approx9.452\times10^{-7}$ and $c_{{\rm site},B}\approx9.306\times10^{-7}$,
and whole-sequence convergence of that model's one named construction for
every $N\ge2$. The limit is also reached along every exhausting sequence of
the same prescription, is identified with every AQ1-type subsequential state
of that construction, is mirrored pointwise between the two signs of $\tau$
and is invariant under coarse translations. It satisfies the Round32 node
certificate of that model at $s=1$ with unchanged constants. None of these
investigations establishes uniqueness of any ground state, a statement about
states outside the named constructions or about other boundary
prescriptions, equality of GNS dynamics of different states, route-B
dynamics, an interaction shift of the Euclidean correlation, an estimate
uniform in the lattice spacing or a continuum statement. The applications
stage records what transfers and what does not. BD1 carries the Round32
link-flip lemma and first-order parity theorem to other gauge groups on
one-plaquette and box models under a frozen convention. The flip transfers
to SU(2), SU(4), U(1) and Z2, whose centres contain an element acting as $-1$
on the Wilson representation, and the parity to SU(2), SU(4), SU(5), U(1) and
Z2, whose third Haar moment of the Wilson variable vanishes. SU(3) and SO(3)
are obstructions with exact nonzero coefficients ($1/589824$ and $1/331776$
at second order), and SU(5) is a flip obstruction with the fourth-order
coefficient $1/(2^{30}3^{10})$. For SU(2) the Wilson-loop mean is odd or even
in $\tau$ with the parity of the area of any spanning surface, in every box
and, through BB2, for the limit. BD2 turns to new SU(2) observables and a new
dimension. On the two-plaquette graph with independent couplings it gives
exact coefficients and a certified positive 1x2 loop, with relative width at
most $1.79645\times10^{-17}$. In the zero-selected family the 1x2 Wilson mean
vanishes at first order and is bounded by $K_2'\tau^2$, with only a formal
second-order coefficient, and the electric energy on the cover lies in the
certified band $[\tfrac32L^2,98|\tau|]$. In 2+1 dimensions only the AM2 constants
are recounted, with no theorem. The addendum closes with the process history,
the reviewed state of the 21 obligations, what the applications did not
transfer, and a ranked roadmap of unexecuted goals. The limit throughout is the
limit of the named constructions, nothing is uniform in the lattice spacing,
and investigation counts are never a fraction of the four-dimensional
continuum Yang--Mills problem, which remains open.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Status and reading guide}
This is a separate scientific addendum; Draft03 \cite{draft03} and the Round31
and Round32 addenda \cite{r31add,r32add} are unchanged. The Round33 cycle consists of eight prospectively
contracted investigations in four sub-rounds of two. Three are research
sub-rounds: locality (BA1, BA2), convergence of the named constructions (BB1,
BB2) and consequences for the admitted certificates (BC1, BC2). The fourth is
an applications stage (BD1, BD2). It applies admitted Round32 and Round33
equations to related problems and records an explicit obstruction where an
equation does not transfer. An expert panel fixed the goals, directions and
contract rules in two deliberation loops before any production. The panel
consists of three methodological lenses (Newton/Tesla, Jung/Pauli and
Penrose/Feynman), an advisor and a skeptic. After each sub-round one research
assistant per lens runs the tests named in the lens updates, and the advisor
may reorder, replace or retitle the next pair. Assistant work counts as zero
investigations.

This is the complete edition. It records all four sub-rounds, and all eight
investigations are gated and accepted within their stated model scope. It is
authored from the eight reviewed gates, and its builder refuses to run before
all eight exist and verifies every gate binding.

BA1, BA2, BB1, BB2 and BC1 concern one model, the zero-selected patterned
family of Round32, and the two named construction families of
Section~\ref{sec:families}. BC2 concerns the uniform model handled by
route~B and its one named construction (Section~\ref{sec:bc2-model}). BD1
concerns one-plaquette and box models of seven gauge groups and, for SU(2),
the zero-selected family; BD2 concerns the Round11 two-plaquette graph, the
zero-selected family and a declared 2+1-dimensional model. BA1, BA2, BB1 and BB2 are paired: a forward and a
reverse producer proved the same frozen targets by different routes, the
reverse under premise isolation. The skeptic derived the constants in a
pre-comparison package, committed before both producer commits for BA1, BA2
and BB1 and between the two for BB2, and replayed and mutated both producers
after the comparison. BC1 is a statement investigation with one forward
producer; the skeptic's pre-comparison replay was committed 3~min 32~s after
the forward commit, with its values final before the forward checker was last
written. BC2 is a single-direction investigation with one forward producer;
the skeptic's pre-comparison replay from the contract was committed 3~min
44~s before the forward commit. BD1 is paired, with a characters route and a
Weyl-integration route, and BD2 is single-direction; for both, the skeptic's
pre-comparison package was committed after the producer commits, with its
values final before them. BA1 carries the sub-label
\recid{boundary_decay_rate_only} with the secondary label
\recid{static_not_dynamic}: it is a statement about creation coefficients, not
about states or dynamics. BA2 carries \recid{dynamics_on_compact_windows}: it
is a statement about the algebraic Heisenberg dynamics on the window
$|\theta|\le8$, not about the states. BB1 carries
\recid{boundary_decay_rate_only} with \recid{static_not_dynamic}: it is a
statement about the reduced densities of finite-box ground vectors, one
comparison at a time, and forms no limit object. BB2 carries
\recid{convergence_of_named_constructions} with
\recid{common_limit_of_named_constructions}: it is a statement about the limit
of the named constructions only. BC1 carries
\recid{certificate_restated_for_limit} with \recid{reference_unresolved} for
the node and \recid{static_not_dynamic} for the static mean: it restates
admitted certificates for that one limit and adds a finite-box corollary.
BC2 carries \recid{convergence_of_named_constructions} with
\recid{certificate_restated_for_limit}, \recid{reference_unresolved} and
\recid{static_not_dynamic}: it concerns the one named construction of the
route-B model, its limit and the restated node, and no route-B real-time
dynamics. BD1 carries \recid{transfer_to_named_model} with
\recid{obstruction_recorded}, and BD2 carries
\recid{sign_certified_finite_graph} with \recid{transfer_to_named_model},
\recid{obstruction_recorded} and \recid{static_not_dynamic}: they are
statements about named models and a finite graph.

The headline results, each within the scope its gate records, are these.
\begin{itemize}
\item Boundary decay of the AM2 creation coefficients (BA1):
 $\sum_{I\ni u}\norm{c^{\rm box\,1}_I-c^{\rm box\,2}_I}\le Kq^{N-1}$ for $u\in R$,
 with $K\approx4.383\times10^{-7}$ at $q=1/64$ (margin about $1.14$, recorded
 as thin) and $K\approx2.428\times10^{-5}$ at $q=37888|\tau|$, for every
 comparison of two named boxes, both signs and every on-site cutoff; the
 crude tier misses the headline target and is retained.
\item Comparison of the dynamics (BA2):
 $\norm{T^{F2,N}_\theta(A)-T^{F1,N}_\theta(A)}\le2.549\times10^{-11}(5N+1)N^{-3}$
 for $\norm A\le1$ and $|\theta|\le8$, with the first order in $\tau$
 vanishing exactly.
\item Cauchy estimates and the F2 limit dynamics (BA2): rate $1/(N-1)$ in $N$
 for both families, whole-sequence convergence of the evolutions, the F2
 limit dynamics identified with AQ1's, and AQ1~\S\S4--5 rerun for every F2
 subsequential limit state. This closes the Round32 obligations O5 and O6
 within that scope.
\item Locality of the reduced densities (BB1):
 $\norm{\rho^{\rm box\,1}_R-\rho^{\rm box\,2}_R}_1\le Cq^{N-1}$ with
 $C\approx8.905\times10^{-7}$ at $q=1/64$ (margin about $4.49$), and a region
 form with $c_{\rm site}\approx8.768\times10^{-7}$ (margin about $2.28$, the
 binding one), for every comparison, both signs, each cutoff space and the
 untruncated vectors at fixed $N$; the crude tier fails and is retained.
\item Convergence of the named constructions (BB2), with every BB1 hypothesis
 discharged: whole-sequence Cauchy bounds with $C'=4/984375$ and
 $c'_{\rm site}=2/984375$ (margin $315/128$), one common limit identified with
 every AQ1 and F2 subsequential limit, coarse translation invariance with
 $\norm{\rho^{\Lambda_N+v}_R-\rho^{\Lambda_N}_R}_1\le C_hq^{N-|v|_\infty-1}$,
 and convergence of the correlation functions on $|\theta|\le8$ with
 $C_{\rm dyn}\approx1.2532\times10^{-10}$, at a rate $K_5/N$ on
 $5\le N\le14000$ only. This closes the Round32 obligations O2, O3 and O4 for
 the named constructions within that scope.
\item Certificates for the limit (BC1): the node certificate $|C(1)-d|\le R$
 with $R\approx1.832\times10^{-7}$ and the free reference $e^{-3}/4$ inside the
 interval, and the static enclosure of $\omega_\infty(W)$ in about
 $[6.91090\times10^{-11},6.97799\times10^{-11}]$, both restated for the limit
 of the named constructions with unchanged constants; the sign of the static
 Wilson mean of the untruncated padded boxes certified for every $N\ge4$ at
 both signs, with the boxes $N=2,3$ open and no finite-box node; and a
 reviewed obligations table of 21 rows, in which O1 is split into O1a, closed
 within the BB2 scope, and O1b, open.
\item Convergence for the route-B uniform model (BC2): every-site
 coefficient decay with $K_B=13/27941256$ (margin about $2.149$) on the disc
 $|z|\le64|\tau|$, admissible under $J'=29|\tau|$ while the route-A extremes
 fail the self-map by the factor $29/28$; locality by the iterated split with
 $C_B\approx9.452\times10^{-7}$ and $c_{{\rm site},B}\approx9.306\times10^{-7}$
 (margin about $2.149$); whole-sequence convergence by one direct comparison
 for every $N\ge2$, exhaustion within the prescription, the pointwise sign
 mirror, identification before inheritance and coarse translation
 invariance; and the AX2 node restated for the limit, with the free
 reference still inside the interval.
\item Centre symmetry across gauge groups (BD1): exact Haar moments and
 first-order Wilson coefficients for SU(2), SU(3), SU(4), SU(5), U(1), Z2 and
 SO(3) ($1/144$, $1/1152$, $1/2880$, $1/5760$, $1/96$, $1/48$, $1/864$), equal
 on two routes and the skeptic's three in 141 cells; the flip lemma
 transferred to SU(2), SU(4), U(1) and Z2 and the parity theorem to SU(2),
 SU(4), SU(5), U(1) and Z2, box by box inside a Kato radius; SU(3), SO(3) and
 SU(5) recorded as obstructions with exact coefficients; and the SU(2) area
 parity in every box and for the limit.
\item New observables and a new dimension (BD2): exact two-coupling tables on
 the two-plaquette graph with the one-link flip parities, and a certified
 positive 1x2 loop at relative width at most $1.79645\times10^{-17}$ against
 $10^{-16}$; the $\mathbb Z^3$ 1x2 Wilson mean zero at first order, bounded
 by $K_2'\tau^2\approx1.34\times10^{-12}$, even for F1 and the limit, with the
 formal coefficient $7/124416$ labelled; the electric band $[\tfrac32L^2,98|\tau|]$
 for every box and the limit; and the 2+1D AM2 constants
 $G_3=9e^{3/32}$, $G_3'=118e^{3/32}$ with the cap about
 $1.5807\times10^{-3}$, no theorem claimed.
\end{itemize}
