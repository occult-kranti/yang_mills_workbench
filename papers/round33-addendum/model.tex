\section{Model dictionary, Round32 checkpoint and Round33 plan}
\label{sec:model}

This section fixes the objects that every Round33 investigation names. It
restates the Round32 model dictionary without change
(\repo{papers/round32-addendum/main.pdf}{Round32 addendum}, Section~2), adds
the two named construction families as Round32's AY1 admitted them, records
where Round32 stopped and lists the frozen Round33 plan. Nothing in this
section is a new result.

\subsection{Model dictionary}
\label{sec:dictionary}

The theory is the SU(2) Kogut--Susskind form on the spatial lattice
$\mathbb Z^3$ at fixed spacing $a$. A coarse site $b\in\mathbb Z^3$ owns the
three positive links from each fine tail $(4b_x+r,2b_y+q,b_z)$, $0\le r<4$,
$0\le q<2$: exactly 24 untruncated $L^2(\mathrm{SU}(2))$ link factors. One
coarse step is therefore $(4a,2a,a)$ in fine units. Both original endpoint
gauge actions are retained. Write $C_e=-\sum_aX_{ea}^2$ for the link Casimir,
with generators $i\sigma_a/2$, and $W_f=\tfrac12\tr U_f$ for the real
fundamental Wilson plaquette, so $\norm{W_f}=1$.

An elementary face in directions $a<c$ at $p$ has positive link tails at $p$,
$p+e_a$, $p+e_c$ and $p$, so its coarse owner set is
$M_f=\{\pi(p),\pi(p+e_a),\pi(p+e_c)\}$, with two or three elements. Grouping
by anchor gives 24 face classes per coarse site, with relative supports inside
the star $S=\{0,e_x,e_y,e_z\}$
(\repo{research/round21/forward/i1/report.md}{I1 dictionary}). Three classes
lie inside one factor and form the selected strip. Throughout Round33 their
coefficient triple is exactly $(0,0,0)$, an admissible choice inside the
closed AQ coefficient box with the Haar product as reference. It is neither
the uniform model nor a replacement of a nonzero selected vacuum by Haar. The
other 21 classes cross a block boundary. These \emph{omitted} classes
(Table~\ref{tab:dict-classes}) are the only interaction.

Two counts read off the table recur below. The classes whose support avoids
$e_x$, $e_y$ or $e_z$ number 17, 13 and 5. Those avoiding two directions
number 10 (avoiding $e_x,e_y$), 3 (avoiding $e_x,e_z$) and 1 (avoiding
$e_y,e_z$), and no class avoids all three. By translation covariance a coarse
factor lies in the owner sets of exactly $21+4+8+16=49$ omitted faces,
grouped in 15 owner sets. The faces meeting the cover $R$ defined below number 82; 16
of them contain both sites of $R$, and ten have owner set exactly $R$.

In normalized units $\delta=\alpha/8$ the omitted faces are grouped by anchor
into whole stars:
\begin{equation}
 \phi_b=-\frac{\tau}{3}\sum_{f\in O_b}W_f,\quad |O_b|=21,\quad
 \norm{\phi_b}=7|\tau|,\qquad
 h_b=8\!\!\sum_{e\text{ owned by }b}\!\!C_e .
 \label{eq:dict-star}
\end{equation}
At the zero triple the on-site operator $h_b$ is an unbounded pure Casimir sum
with compact resolvent, constant (Haar) vacuum projection $P_b$, zero ground
scalar and $h_b\ge6(I-P_b)$. The physical Hamiltonian is $\delta$ times the
normalized one; in the energy unit $\alpha$ each star is $\phi_b/8$. A site
$u$ lies in the four stars anchored at $u-S$, incoming anchors included, so
the per-site interaction sum is
\begin{equation}
 J=\max_u\sum_{b:\,u\in b+S}\norm{\phi_b}\le4\cdot7|\tau|=28|\tau|\le
 J_0=\frac{7}{25000000}\qquad(|\tau|\le10^{-8}).
 \label{eq:dict-J}
\end{equation}
The outgoing count $7|\tau|$ is a rejected control (\recid{missing_incoming_stars}) in
both sub-round-1 contracts.

For the original $xz$ Wilson plaquette $W$ at the origin, the complete-factor
cover is $R=\{0,e_z\}$: 48 stored links and 36 original endpoints. Its seven
incident whole-star anchors are
$R-S=\{0,-e_x,-e_y,-e_z,e_z,e_z-e_x,e_z-e_y\}$. The on-site product reference
on $R$ is the Haar vector $\Omega_R$ with projection $P_R$.

\paragraph{AM2 creation expansion.}
AM2 (Round29) proves, for every finite complete-factor volume and both signs of
$|\tau|\le10^{-8}$, a simple ground state and a centered gap at least
$\alpha/16$ (\repo{research/round29/advisor/am2-gate.json}{AM2 gate}). For
nonempty $I$ a creation vector $c_I\in\bigotimes_{x\in I}Q_x\mathcal H_x$,
$Q_x=I-P_x$, defines $\hat c_I=|c_I\rangle\langle\Omega_I|\otimes1$. These
operators commute, $\hat c_I\hat c_J=0$ when $I\cap J\ne\varnothing$, and with
$C=\sum_I\hat c_I$ the ground vector is $\psi=e^{-C}\Omega_0$. In the anchored
norm $\norm{c}_a=\max_u\sum_{I\ni u}\norm{c_I}$ the coefficients are the
unique fixed point in the ball of radius $R_a$ of
\begin{equation}
 c=\sum_{k=0}^{8}\frac{L_k(c,\ldots,c)}{k!},\qquad
 (L_k)_M=H_M^{-1}P_M\,\mathrm{ad}_{C_1}\cdots\mathrm{ad}_{C_k}(V)\,\Omega_0,\qquad
 \norm{c}_a\le R_a=\tfrac1{64},
 \label{eq:dict-fixed}
\end{equation}
where the commutator nest terminates at order eight. The all-order majorant
and its evaluated constants are
\begin{equation}
 \begin{gathered}
 G(t)=16e^{8t}(1+10t),\qquad G(R_a)<\tfrac{148}{7},\qquad G'(R_a)<352,\\
 J_0G(R_a)<\tfrac{37}{6250000}<R_a,\qquad 2J_0G'(R_a)<1 ,
 \end{gathered}
 \label{eq:dict-majorant}
\end{equation}
from the directed enclosure $e^{1/8}<8/7$. The Round33 gate records write
$R=1/64$ for the anchored radius; this edition writes $R_a$ so that $R$
always denotes the cover. The exact first-order collection is AV1's
$c^{(1)}_M=-(\tau/72)\sum_{M_f=M}W_f\Omega_0$, with
$\norm{c^{(1)}}_a\le t_1=49|\tau|/144$; the AV1 self-consistent remainder gives
$\norm{c}_a\le t_1/(1-352J)$
(\repo{research/round32/advisor/av1-gate.json}{AV1 gate}).

\paragraph{On-site cutoff.}
Let $Q_L=\bigotimes_x1_{[0,L]}(h_x)$. AM2 holds in every cutoff space $Q_L$
with rank-independent constants; the compressions keep supports and do not
increase $J$, and for $L\ge24$ every first-order face vector $W_f\Omega_0$ is
kept exactly. AM2~\S6 removes the cutoff for the eigenvalues and the gap, and
AV1 removes it for the ground vector. Neither defines untruncated creation
coefficients; BA1 states its coefficient bounds in each $Q_L$, uniformly in
$L$, and asserts nothing about untruncated coefficients.

\paragraph{States, dynamics and clock.}
AQ1 (Round29) supplies, by diagonal extraction along centered whole-star boxes,
locally normal stationary subsequential states, with trace-norm convergence of
the local densities on every finite region; no uniqueness, whole-sequence
convergence or rate in $N$ is assumed. AQ1~\S3 places the whole-star model in
the Nachtergaele--Sims setting and obtains the limit dynamics $T_\theta$ of
local observables. The clocks are
\begin{equation}
 G_\tau=H_\tau/\alpha,\qquad s=\frac{\alpha t_E}{\hbar},\qquad
 \theta=\frac{\alpha t}{\hbar},\qquad
 T^{F,N}_\theta(A)=e^{i(\theta/8)\widehat H^{F}_N}\,A\,e^{-i(\theta/8)\widehat H^{F}_N},
 \label{eq:dict-clock}
\end{equation}
where $\widehat H^F_N$ is the normalized box operator of a family $F$ below.
The normalized time $u=\theta/8$ is used only internally, with this
conversion, so the BA2 window $|\theta|\le8$ is $|u|\le1$. Ground scalars
cancel in the conjugation. The positive energy $\alpha$, action $\hbar$ and
spacing $a$ stay fixed, so $\theta$ and $\tau$ are dimensionless; a window in
$u$ labelled $\theta$ is an eightfold clock error and a rejected control.

\subsection{The two named construction families}
\label{sec:families}

Both families use the centered coarse cubes $\Lambda_N=[-N,N]^3$ with
$N\ge2$, exactly as Round32's AY1 admitted them
(\repo{research/round32/advisor/ay1-gate.json}{AY1 gate}):
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 \text{F1 (AQ1):}\quad&\widehat H^{F1}_N=\sum_{b\in\Lambda_N}h_b
 +\sum_{b+S\subset\Lambda_N}\phi_b,\\
 \text{F2 (I1 \S6):}\quad&\widehat H^{F2}_N=\sum_{b\in\Lambda_N}h_b
 +\sum_{f:\,M_f\subset\Lambda_N}\Bigl(-\frac\tau3\Bigr)W_f,\qquad
 \widehat H^{F2,{\rm pad}}_N=\widehat H^{F2}_N\otimes1
 +1\otimes\!\!\sum_{x\in B_+\setminus\Lambda_N}\!\!h_x .
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:families}
\end{equation}
F1 is I1's empty-boundary rule: an anchor group is retained only when its
whole star lies in the box. F2 retains every omitted face whose owner set lies
in the box, grouped at its anchor. I1~\S6 pads the box to
$B_+=\Lambda_N+S$ and places the on-site operator alone on the
$3(2N+1)^2$ padding sites. On the centered cubes F1 retains $21(2N)^3$ faces
and F2 retains $28N(2N+1)(3N+1)$, so F2 keeps
\begin{equation}
 |E_N|=28N(2N+1)(3N+1)-21(2N)^3=28N(5N+1)
 \label{eq:extra-faces}
\end{equation}
extra faces, where $E_N=F2(\Lambda_N)\setminus F1(\Lambda_N)$. They sit at
the $(2N+1)^3-(2N)^3-1$ anchors with some $b_i=N$; the corner $(N,N,N)$
carries none (Table~\ref{tab:families}). The face sets are totally ordered,
$F1(\Lambda_N)\subset F2(\Lambda_N)\subset F1(\Lambda_{N+1})$. For every
$N\ge2$ both families retain the same 82 faces meeting $R$, because the owner
sets of the faces meeting $R$ lie in $[-1,1]^2\times[-1,2]\subset\Lambda_2$.

Other prescriptions are not covered. The literal vertex boxes of I1~\S7 clip
the on-site operator and form another model; periodic and orthant boxes are
not among the named families either. Two coarse metrics occur, and neither is
a fine-site metric. BA1 and BB1 use the coarse $\ell^\infty$ metric, in which
every star has diameter 1; BA2 uses the coarse $\ell^1$ metric of AQ1~\S3, in
which every star has diameter 2; BB2 uses the first for states and the second
for dynamics. They satisfy $d_\infty\le d_1\le3d_\infty$, and no
constant mixes them.

\subsection{Round32 checkpoint}
\label{sec:checkpoint}

Round32 closed with ten investigations accepted within scope
(\repo{papers/round32-addendum/main.pdf}{Round32 addendum}). Its fourth
sub-round compared the two families statically. AY1 proved that every pair of
subsequential limits of F1 or F2 at the same coupling satisfies
$\norm{\rho_R-\rho'_R}_1\le2D\approx2.72249\times10^{-8}$ on the cover, with
$D$ the AV1 forward tier-(ii) bound. The limits share the first-order density
$\rho^{(1)}_R$ of the ten faces with owner set $R$, and beyond it they differ
by at most $2K_2'\tau^2\approx2.6835\times10^{-12}$, with the $R$-local
constant $K_2'\approx13417.53$. AY2 certified a static two-sided distance of
every limit from the Haar product on $R$, between about $4.3786\times10^{-10}$
and $4.4055\times10^{-10}$, and listed six obligations that the static bounds
leave open (\repo{research/round32/advisor/ay2-gate.json}{AY2 gate}).

\subsection{Round33 plan}
\label{sec:plan}

the next three rounds and then to apply the admitted solutions and new
equations to their related problems, if any
(\repo{research/round33/advisor/brief.md}{brief}). As in Round32, a ``round''
is read as a sub-round of two investigations with the advisor, the skeptic and
the expert panel, followed by one research assistant per lens. Round33 runs
three research sub-rounds and an applications stage.

Two deliberation loops preceded production. In loop~1 each lens wrote a memo
and a source ledger, and the skeptic wrote a prospective triage and a control
catalogue; the advisor's plan~v1 fixed four sub-rounds, adopted the rate

\paragraph{Direction rules.}
Derivation-bearing investigations are paired, with genuinely different
forward and reverse routes named in the contract. Formula-instantiating
certificates use one direction plus an independent skeptic replay written
from the contract alone, and statements use a statement plus skeptical
review. Same-author passes are never independent review. For paired
investigations the reverse producer receives only \texttt{AGENTS.md}, the
contract and its declared shared premises, and the freezing tool enforces that
inventory. After each sub-round the advisor may reorder, replace or retitle
the next pair; the change is recorded in the next selection note and in the
plan history. The applications stage records, for each admitted equation it
applies, either a labelled transfer to a named model with its own exact
checks or the obstruction that prevents the transfer.

\paragraph{Contract rules of plan~v2.}
Every Round33 contract carries the Round32 pre-registration block (model
identifier, selected triple, coupling and signs, state provenance, observable,
nodes, itemized error terms, target, outcome types, sub-labels, claim
exclusions, control ids, a mandatory sentence template quoted once as one
unbroken span, and the required gate fields), and the Round32 closing-panel
rules R1--R10, of which the contract freezer enforces R1--R5 and R8 and the
gate recorder R6 and R7. Plan~v2 adds the following rules.
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{One tier and one route per constant.} Every constant carries
exactly one tier and exactly one route from the closed lists of
Table~\ref{tab:vocabulary}.
\item \emph{Margins.} The (rate, constant) pair of a decay target is frozen
before production with a margin of at least 2 against the recorded previews.
The margin is a freeze-time property of the target; acceptance asks only that
the proved constant be at most the frozen target.
\item \emph{Scaling brackets per constant.} Each constant's
$\tau\to\tau/100$ bracket is declared before production and admits every valid
form of its $\tau$ order: $[95,105]$ for linear and $[9500,10500]$ for
quadratic constants, unless exact variants justify a narrower bracket.
\item \emph{No global Lipschitz decay.} The global Lipschitz constant of a
contraction is never a decay factor.
\item \emph{Committed source excerpts.} An external theorem is quoted from a
committed source excerpt with its PDF hash, never from a report that cites
it.
\item \emph{Cauchy quantifier.} A within-family Cauchy target is quantified
over all $M>N$; an $N$ to $N+1$ bound alone is not a Cauchy estimate.
\item \emph{Bookkeeping.} Every contract names its metric, weights, window
and clock in its parameters and gives new control semantics for each new
control id; \recid{selected_after} never names the plan, and each freeze
records the plan's sha256 in the plan history.
\end{itemize}
