Each investigation will name its model (the fixed-lattice family or finite
graph, coupling, selected coefficients, state and physical clock), its
direction (paired, single+skeptic, statement+skeptic or statement-only) and
its exclusions in a contract frozen before production. The applications stage
records, for each admitted equation it applies, either a labelled transfer to
a named model checked there by its own exact controls, or the obstruction that
prevents the transfer.
