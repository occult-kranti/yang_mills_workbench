\section{What the Round33 record does and does not establish}
\label{sec:limits}

All eight Round33 gates record the verdict \recid{accepted_within_scope}, and
each acceptance holds only inside the model its gate names. The record uses
five models, and they are kept apart:
\begin{itemize}
\item the zero-selected patterned family of the SU(2) Kogut--Susskind form at
 fixed spacing and $|\tau|\le10^{-8}$ \cite{ks}, with the two named
 construction families F1 and F2, their finite boxes and the limit of the
 named constructions (BA1, BA2, BB1, BB2, BC1, the SU(2) corollary of BD1,
 and items 3 and 4 of BD2);
\item the uniform Kogut--Susskind SU(2) model at fixed spacing and strong bare
 coupling, $g^4\ge9.6\times10^9$ at the cap, handled by route~B, with its one
 named construction (BC2);
\item the one-plaquette models and the group box models of seven gauge
 groups under a frozen convention, each box separately inside its Kato radius
 (BD1);
\item the Round11 two-plaquette graph with independent couplings, a finite
 model \cite{r11} (BD2);
\item a declared 2+1-dimensional SU(2) model, for which only the AM2 constants
 are recounted (BD2).
\end{itemize}
A result proved in one model is not a claim about another. BC2 re-derived the
chain for route~B with its own constants; it imported none. BD1 transfers
statements to named finite models by criteria, not by analogy, and no
one-plaquette or graph value transfers to a lattice limit.

\paragraph{The limit is the limit of the named constructions.} BB2 shows
that the reduced densities of F1 and F2 converge as whole sequences to one
common limit on every finite region, and that this limit equals every AQ1
and every F2 subsequential limit. That is convergence of the named
constructions. It is not uniqueness of any ground state, and it says nothing
about states outside the named constructions or about other boundary
conditions, such as literal vertex, periodic or orthant boxes. BC2 proves the
same for the one route-B construction, with exhaustion within its own
prescription in place of a common limit. Every certificate restated in BC1
and BC2, and the SU(2) area parity of BD1, holds for these limits only.

\paragraph{The obligations state.} The reviewed table of the BC1 gate has 21
rows (Table~\ref{tab:bc1-obligations}); Table~\ref{tab:limits-obligations}
summarizes their standing. Row O1 of Round32's AY2 table is split: O1a, that
all subsequential limits of F1 and F2 coincide on every finite region, is
closed within the BB2 scope, and O1b, uniqueness of any ground state together
with states outside the named constructions and other boundary conditions,
is open. Rows O2--O6 are closed within the scopes of the BB2 and BA2 gates,
and rows N1--N14 are open. Row N9, frozen before BC2 was gated, stays as the
BC1 review froze it: BC2 admits the convergence of the route-B named
construction (Section~\ref{sec:bc2}), and its review records five further open
rows for route~B (Table~\ref{tab:bc2-obligations}). The BD1 review records
eleven open rows, among them an AM2/AQ chain and a dictionary for every group
other than SU(2), and the BD2 review six, among them the area law and a
certified sign of the $\mathbb Z^3$ 1x2 mean.

\paragraph{What the applications did not transfer.}
Table~\ref{tab:apps-summary} records every cell. The link-flip lemma does not
transfer to SU(3), SO(3) or SU(5), and the first-order parity theorem not to
SU(3) or SO(3); each non-transfer is proved by an exact nonzero coefficient on
the one-plaquette model, not by the absence of a central element alone. No
AM2 fixed point, AV1 state lemma, AQ construction, BB1 or BB2 comparison or
coupling dictionary is transferred to any group other than SU(2), and no
statement for any other group holds for all box sizes at once. No flip or
parity statement is made for SU(2) at a nonzero selected triple, for F2 or
literal vertex boxes, or on periodic boxes with an odd side. The
two-plaquette graph transfers nothing to the lattice limit. In the
zero-selected family the 1x2 Wilson mean has no certified sign, only a formal
second-order coefficient; no area law is claimed, because no zero-free region
of the reduced density is admitted; and the electric band is certified but
not tight. In 2+1 dimensions only the AM2 constants are recounted, with no
finite-volume theorem, AQ chain, node or dictionary. The Einstein--QED
evidence rounds share no equation with this chain.

\paragraph{Nothing is uniform in the lattice spacing.} Every lattice result
holds at one fixed spacing and at strong bare coupling. Every rate is per
coarse step in $N$ at fixed spacing, where a coarse step is $(4a,2a,a)$, and a
rate in $N$ is not a rate in $a$. The Round32 statement AZ1 already names the
one necessary estimate the fixed-spacing family supplies along a trajectory
and shows that it supplies none uniform along $a_n\to0$ \cite{r32add}. No
Round33 gate changes that.

\paragraph{Standing of the evidence.} Every admission uses exact rational
arithmetic with directed rounding; the decimals in this addendum are previews,
and the Arb and mpmath comparisons never admit a value. The producers, the
skeptic, the advisor, the lenses and the assistants are model agents with
correlated ancestry. Their agreement is correlated model-agent work, never
external human peer review or formal verification; in the single-direction
loops, exact agreement with the skeptic's replay checks arithmetic and code,
not a second method. Contract wording defects were recorded as non-blocking
by the gates, and no frozen file was edited. The phrase scanner is a
vocabulary guard: BC1 showed that it does not see a phrasing it was not given,
so the reviewed wording, not the scanner, guards the O1 split. Inherited
premises are cited, not re-proved, and the external theorems named in the
text \cite{ns,kp,ueltschi,yarotsky,htw} are cited, not machine-checked.

\paragraph{Attribution and priority.} No scientific priority is claimed or
verified for any Round33 result. The attribution sentence of the Round32
addendum for the AM2 creation expansion and the AQ construction
\cite{r32add,gauvin} stands unchanged. Centre symmetries of lattice gauge
theories, cluster expansions and Lieb--Robinson bounds are known kinds of
argument; the contribution here is their application to the named models
with exact constants and exact obstruction coefficients.

\label{sec:limits-roadmap}

ranks five goals after the closing lens updates (Table~\ref{tab:limits-roadmap}).
Every goal is a fixed-lattice question for a named model with a named missing
premise, and every one is planned and unexecuted; any of them needs a fresh
prospective contract. No Round34 contract, producer or gate exists.

contributions is not a measure of progress on the continuum problem. Loop
counts are never a fraction of it. The four-dimensional continuum
Yang--Mills existence and mass-gap problem remains open \cite{clay}.
