\subsection{The identification premise}
\label{sec:bc1-identification}
Three admitted statements are used, all read from hash-pinned gates. For
$F\in\{F1,F2\}$, both signs, every $N\ge2$ and every $M>N$, BB2 item~1 gives
\begin{equation}
 \norm{\rho^{F,M}_R-\rho^{F,N}_R}_1\le C'q^{N-1},\qquad
 C'=\frac4{984375},\qquad q=\frac1{64},
 \label{eq:bc1-cauchy}
\end{equation}
in each on-site cutoff space and, at fixed $N$, for the untruncated ground
vectors (Section~\ref{sec:bb2-item1}). BB2 items~2--3 give that the F1 and
F2 limits coincide on every finite region and that, in the gate's words,
``this limit coincides with every AQ1 subsequential limit and every F2
subsequential limit''. BA2 item~(4) gives that the F2 limit dynamics equals
the AQ1 limit dynamics $T_\theta$ (Section~\ref{sec:ba2-limit}).

\paragraph{The identification.} At each sign of $\tau$, let $S(\tau)$ be the
set of AQ1 subsequential limits. It is nonempty by the AQ1 gate. By BB2
item~3 every member equals $\omega_\infty$ on every finite region, and two
states that agree on the local algebra agree on its norm closure. Hence
\begin{equation}
 S(\tau)=\{\omega_\infty\},
 \label{eq:bc1-identification}
\end{equation}
and the set of F2 subsequential limits is the same singleton. This is the
forward's statement \projecttag{HNM-BC1-F01}. Every statement that a Round32
gate admits for every AQ1 subsequential state, or for the whole set of them,
therefore holds for $\omega_\infty$ with the same constants. The GNS triple of
$\omega_\infty$ is that of the one AQ1 subsequential state up to the canonical
unitary equivalence, its GNS evolution implements the same automorphism
group $T_\theta$, and its generator $G$ is nonnegative (AQ1~\S5, inherited
through BB2 item~3). The AV2 window lemma applies to $\omega_\infty$ word for
word.

\paragraph{Order and what it does not license.} The identification precedes
every inheritance (control \recid{limit_identified_with_aq1_limits}). A
restatement drawn from uniform local closeness, from one BB1 comparison or
from a subsequence is rejected. AV1 and AY1 place any two subsequential
limits within $2D$ of each other on $R$, and within $2K_2'\tau^2$ beyond the
common first-order density; closeness of that kind does not make them one
state. The identification changes no
constant and adds nothing about $C(s)$ or $\omega(W)$ beyond the Round32
gates. It says nothing about states outside the named constructions or other
boundary conditions, and nothing about finite boxes except through the
explicit bound \eqref{eq:bc1-cauchy} (Section~\ref{sec:bc1-sign}).

\subsection{The finite-box sign corollary for the padded family}
\label{sec:bc1-sign}
This is the one new constant family of BC1 (\projecttag{HNM-BC1-F04}). For
every $N\ge2$, both signs and the untruncated F2 ground state
$\omega^{F2,N}$ on $\Lambda_N$ at fixed $N$,
\begin{equation}
 \bigl|\omega^{F2,N}(W)-\omega_\infty(W)\bigr|
 \le\norm W\,\norm{\rho^{F2,N}_R-\rho^{\infty}_R}_1\le C'q^{N-1},
 \label{eq:bc1-duality}
\end{equation}
where $\rho^\infty_R$ is the $R$-density of $\omega_\infty$. The proof has
five steps.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item BB2 item~1 with $F=F2$ gives \eqref{eq:bc1-cauchy} for every $M>N$,
 for the untruncated ground vectors at fixed $N$ and $M$.
\item By BB2 items~1--2 the whole sequence $\rho^{F2,M}_R$ converges in trace
 norm to $\rho^\infty_R$. The trace norm is continuous, and the closed
 trace-norm ball of radius $C'q^{N-1}$ about $\rho^{F2,N}_R$ contains the
 limit, so $\norm{\rho^\infty_R-\rho^{F2,N}_R}_1\le C'q^{N-1}$.
\item The original $xz$ Wilson loop $W$ lies in $B(\mathcal H_R)$; it is real
 and at most 1 in absolute value pointwise, so $\norm W\le1$. Trace duality
 gives the first inequality of \eqref{eq:bc1-duality}. The factor $\tfrac12$ available for an
 effect does not apply, because $W$ is not an effect.
\item Adding \eqref{eq:bc1-duality} to \eqref{eq:bc1-mean} on both sides
 gives the widened enclosure
 \begin{equation}
 \omega^{F2,N}(W)\in
 \begin{cases}
 \bigl[L-C'q^{N-1},\,U+C'q^{N-1}\bigr], & \tau=+10^{-8},\\
 \bigl[-U-C'q^{N-1},\,-L+C'q^{N-1}\bigr], & \tau=-10^{-8}.
 \end{cases}
 \label{eq:bc1-widened}
 \end{equation}
\item The widening $C'q^{N-1}$ is strictly decreasing in $N$. Define
 $N_{\rm sign}$ as the least $N\ge2$ with $C'q^{N-1}<L$, strictly. For every
 $N\ge N_{\rm sign}$ the lower end at $+\tau$ is positive and the upper end at
 $-\tau$ is negative.
\end{enumerate}
The constant is the bound value $C'=4/984375$ of the BB2 gate decision
(assembly \recid{nested_telescoping}, hypothesis source
\recid{bb1_frozen_targets}, BB1 route \recid{polymer_kp}), evaluated at the
hypothesis values. The labelled union value $1/250000$ and the labelled
re-evaluated value, about $9.0465\times10^{-7}$, are not used.

\paragraph{$N_{\rm sign}$, exactly.} Table~\ref{tab:bc1-nsign} lists the
widening at $\tau=10^{-8}$, where $L\approx6.91089641214\times10^{-11}$. The
widened interval contains 0 at $N=2$ and $N=3$ and excludes it from $N=4$ on,
