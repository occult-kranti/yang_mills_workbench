investigation~6 of~8

frozen together with BC1 on 25 September 2026 at 04:21:44 UTC, after the

title ``Hruday route-B convergence of the uniform model's named construction

\subsection{The route-B model and its single named construction}
\label{sec:bc2-model}
\paragraph{The model.} The model identifier is
\recid{AQ_uniform_routeB}, with the label ``uniform Kogut--Susskind SU(2) at
fixed spacing and strong bare coupling ($g^4=9.6\times10^9$)''. Every
elementary face has $\nu=\alpha\tau/24$, so the selected triple is
$(\tau/24,\tau/24,\tau/24)$. By the AL1 dictionary of this model,
$\tau=96/g^4$, the cap $|\tau|\le10^{-8}$ corresponds to $g^4\ge9.6\times10^9$;
the model is never at weak coupling or in the continuum. Both signs are
treated, and $\tau<0$, which has no real $g$, is the $U_E$ mirror. Route~B
keeps the Haar reference $h_b=8\sum_eC_e\ge6Q_b$, independent of $\tau$, and
groups the interaction into
\begin{equation}
 \begin{gathered}
 \phi_b\ \text{(whole star: 21 omitted faces, }\norm{\phi_b}\le7|\tau|),\\
 \psi_b\ \text{(single-factor group: 3 selected faces, support }\{b\},\ \norm{\psi_b}\le|\tau|),\\
 J'=\sum_{X\ni u}\norm{V_X}=4\cdot7|\tau|+|\tau|=29|\tau|,\qquad J_0'=\frac{29}{10^8},
 \end{gathered}
 \label{eq:bc2-grouping}
\end{equation}
where the four stars through a factor include the incoming ones (AX1 gate).
AX1 admits that every finite complete-factor route-B volume has a simple
gauge-invariant ground and a full-space gap of at least $1/2$ in normalized
units. The metric is the coarse $\ell^\infty$ metric on factor sites, in
which a whole star has diameter 1 and a single-factor group diameter 0.

\paragraph{One named construction.} The named construction FB consists of the
centered whole-star-plus-single-group boxes $\Lambda_N=[-N,N]^3$, $N\ge2$, and
the finite complete-factor route-B volumes containing $\Lambda_N$, with every
star inside the volume and every single group of the volume kept. Three
comparisons are made directly: c1B ($\Lambda_N$ against $\Lambda_{N+1}$), c4B
(any two centered boxes $\Lambda_M,\Lambda_{M'}$ with $M,M'\ge N$) and c5B (any
two finite complete-factor route-B volumes containing $\Lambda_N$). The

\recid{one_family_no_common_limit} rejects a common limit, a second route-B
family and an identification with an all-contained boundary. The gate field
\recid{common_limit_claimed} is false. Nothing is identified with the

\subsection{The coefficient input}
\label{sec:bc2-coefficients}
The route-B coefficient input is proved in full in the packet, on the pattern
of BA1's reverse route; no BA1 statement is cited as admitted. For complex
$z$ the complexified route-B map satisfies
\begin{equation}
 F_z(c)=\sum_{k=0}^{8}\frac{L^{(z)}_k(c,\ldots,c)}{k!},\qquad
 \norm{L^{(z)}_k(c_1,\ldots,c_k)}_a\le29|z|\,16\cdot8^k\bigl(1+\tfrac{5k}4\bigr)\prod_j\norm{c_j}_a ,
 \label{eq:bc2-complex}
\end{equation}
since $z$ enters only through $\norm{V_X(z)}=|z|\norm{V_{X,1}}$, with the
per-site sum $29|z|$ and termination above order 8. The constants of the AM2
steps increase with the support size, so the one-site groups are covered by
the bound for support 4 (checked for supports 1 to 4 and orders 0 to 8). For
$\rho\le7/274688$ the map sends the anchored ball into itself and contracts
it. Its fixed point $c(z)$ is continuous on the closed disc, holomorphic
inside, and equal to the route-B AM2 fixed point at real $\tau$. Only the
creation coefficients are continued in $z$; no zero-free region of the
normalization is proved, and no analyticity of the reduced density is
claimed.

\subsection{Whole-sequence convergence}
\label{sec:bc2-whole}

and $\Lambda_M$, both of which contain $\Lambda_N$ (assembly

\begin{equation}

 \label{eq:bc2-cauchy}
\end{equation}
