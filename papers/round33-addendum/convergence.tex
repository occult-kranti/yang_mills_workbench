\subsection{Item 1: whole-sequence Cauchy estimates and the limit}
\label{sec:bb2-item1}
\paragraph{Forward: nested telescoping.} For $F\in\{F1,F2\}$, $N\ge2$ and every
$M>N$, write $\rho^{F,M}_Y-\rho^{F,N}_Y$ as the sum of the nested steps
$\rho^{F,k+1}_Y-\rho^{F,k}_Y$, $k=N,\ldots,M-1$. Each step is the BB1
comparison c1 or c2 with smaller box $\Lambda_k$, and for
$Y\subset\Lambda_N$ its exponent is $d_Y+(k-N)$. Summing the full geometric
tail gives
\begin{equation}
 \begin{gathered}
 \sup_{M>N}\norm{\rho^{F,M}_R-\rho^{F,N}_R}_1\le C'q^{N-1},\\
 \sup_{M>N}\norm{\rho^{F,M}_Y-\rho^{F,N}_Y}_1\le c'_{\rm site}|Y|e^{|Y|/10^8}q^{d_Y},
 \end{gathered}
 \label{eq:bb2-cauchy}
\end{equation}
with
\begin{equation}
 C'=\frac{C_h}{1-q}=\frac{64}{63}C_h=\frac4{984375}\approx4.0635\times10^{-6},\qquad
 c'_{\rm site}=\frac{64}{63}c_h=\frac2{984375}\approx2.0317\times10^{-6}.
 \label{eq:bb2-Cprime}
\end{equation}
Each meets its target with margin $315/128=2.4609375$. The bound is the limit
of the partial sums and is not attained, and nothing of the tail is dropped.

\paragraph{Reverse: one direct comparison.} The reverse compares every $M$
with $N$ directly. For two volumes $V_1,V_2$ of one prescription containing
$\Lambda_n$, the triangle inequality through the union $U=V_1\cup V_2$ costs
the assembly factor $[V_1\ne U]+[V_2\ne U]$. For nested centered cubes the
union is the larger cube, so the factor is 1 and $C'=C_h=1/250000$,
$c'_{\rm site}=c_h=1/500000$, each with margin $5/2$. The gate binds the
forward values, the larger ones, which are valid under either route's
discharge since $(64/63)C_h\ge C_h$; the reverse values are labelled.
Re-evaluated at the admitted BB1 constants, $C'$ is about
$9.0465\times10^{-7}$ and $c'_{\rm site}$ about $8.9073\times10^{-7}$ on the
forward assembly, and about $8.9051\times10^{-7}$ and $8.7681\times10^{-7}$ on
the reverse. These re-evaluated values are labelled, not bound.

\paragraph{The limit without compactness.} For a fixed finite region $Y$ the
whole sequence $(\rho^{F,N}_Y)_N$ is Cauchy in the Banach space of trace-class
operators on $\mathcal H_Y$, so it converges in trace norm; no compactness,
subsequence or diagonal extraction is used. Positivity and unit trace are
closed conditions. The partial trace is a trace-norm contraction, so the
limits on nested regions are compatible. They define a locally normal state
on the local algebra, which extends by norm continuity to the quasi-local
algebra, the norm completion of the local algebra in the Nachtergaele--Sims
setting. Letting $M\to\infty$ in \eqref{eq:bb2-cauchy} gives
$\norm{\rho^{F,N}_R-\rho^{F,\infty}_R}_1\le C'q^{N-1}$ and the region analogue.
The order of limits is fixed. The bound holds in each cutoff space
uniformly in $L$. Then $L\to\infty$ at fixed $N$ and $M$, by AV1's
cutoff-vector removal (and the AY1 itemization for F2), which the closed
trace-norm ball survives. Only then is the supremum over $M$ taken and $N$
sent to infinity. Two exact fixtures mark what is not enough. The sequence
$\mathrm{diag}(\tfrac12\pm(-1)^N\delta)$ lies in a one-state ball in every
box yet has two subsequential limits. A zigzag walk with steps $1/(n+1)$
satisfies every $N$-to-$N+1$ bound and keeps oscillating.

\subsection{Items 2 and 3: the common limit and its identification}
\label{sec:bb2-items23}
\paragraph{Item 2.} For every finite region $Y$ and $N$ large enough, the
fixed-$N$ comparison c3 of the two prescriptions on the same $\Lambda_N$,
with item~1 twice, gives
\begin{equation}
 \norm{\rho^{F1,\infty}_Y-\rho^{F2,\infty}_Y}_1\le(2c'_{\rm site}+c_h)\,|Y|\,e^{|Y|/10^8}\,q^{d_Y},
 \label{eq:bb2-common}
\end{equation}
and $(2C'+C_h)q^{N-1}$ on $R$. The left side does not depend on $N$, so the
two limits coincide on every finite region. This common state,
$\omega_\infty$, is \emph{the limit of the named constructions}:
$\omega_\infty(A)=\lim_N\omega^{F1,N}(A)=\lim_N\omega^{F2,N}(A)$ for every
quasi-local $A$. Nothing is said about other boundary conditions or other
states.

\paragraph{Item 3.} An AQ1 subsequential limit is a state whose local
densities are trace-norm limits of the F1 densities along some subsequence
$N_k$. Every subsequence of a convergent sequence has the same limit, so
every AQ1 subsequential limit equals $\omega_\infty$ on every finite region,
hence on the quasi-local algebra. The same holds for every F2 subsequential
limit of AY1's own extraction, by item~2. The set of AQ1 subsequential limits,
nonempty by AQ1's compactness, is therefore the single state $\omega_\infty$,
whichever subsequence was chosen. Only after this identification does
$\omega_\infty$ inherit properties, and in its own GNS representation, which
is unitarily equivalent to AQ1's because the states are equal. It inherits
exactly the admitted ones:
\begin{itemize}
\item from AQ1: a compatible, locally normal, gauge-invariant state,
 stationary under $T_\theta$, with a strongly continuous GNS evolution and a
 nonnegative self-adjoint physical generator reduced by the physical cyclic
 space; for F2 limits the same list comes through BA2's rerun of AQ1~\S\S4--5,
 which now concerns the same state and the same dynamics;
\item from AQ2, for the actual AQ1 centered subsequential state: the complete
 original-endpoint gauge-fixed space equals the invariant-local cyclic
 completion and reduces the generator, and on it
 $H_{\rm phys}\ge(\alpha/16)(I-P_\Omega)$ as a quadratic-form inequality, with
 a simple vacuum on this completion;
\item the full-GNS strengthening of that inequality only as qualified in the
 AQ2 gate, which uses AM2's separately reviewed full-Hilbert finite gap.
\end{itemize}
Not inherited: uniqueness of any ground state, anything about states outside
the named constructions, equality of GNS dynamics of different states, an
unqualified full-GNS gap, and translation invariance, which AQ1 and AQ2
exclude and which is proved separately as item~4. The reverse also lists the
AQ2 Wilson-variance witness of the same state; the gate's list does not
include it.

\subsection{Item 4: coarse translations}
\label{sec:bb2-item4}
\paragraph{Covariance.} A coarse translation $v$ is the fine translation
$t_v=(4v_x,2v_y,v_z)$. It maps each fine tail $(4i+r,2j+q,k)$ to
$(4(i+v_x)+r,2(j+v_y)+q,k+v_z)$, so the links owned by $b$ go to the links
owned by $b+v$ with the same residues, every face goes to a face of the same
class, and owner sets are translated. The site relabelling $T_v$ therefore
satisfies $T_v\widehat H^P_VT_v^*=\widehat H^P_{V+v}$ for both prescriptions,
in each cutoff space; the ground states are simple, so
$\rho^{V+v}_{Y+v}=T_v\rho^V_YT_v^*$.

\paragraph{The bound.} For $N\ge|v|_\infty+2$ the volumes $\Lambda_N+v$ and
$\Lambda_N$ both contain $\Lambda_{N-|v|_\infty}$, but for $v\ne0$ they are not
nested and their union is not a centered cube. The direct BB1 comparison c5
of two one-prescription volumes containing $\Lambda_{N-|v|_\infty}$, used by
both producers, gives
\begin{equation}
 \norm{\rho^{\Lambda_N+v}_R-\rho^{\Lambda_N}_R}_1\le C_h\,q^{N-\norm v_\infty-1},\qquad
 C_h=\frac1{250000},
 \label{eq:bb2-translation}
\end{equation}
with the hypothesis constant itself. Re-evaluated at the admitted $C$, it is
about $8.9051\times10^{-7}$ (labelled). A two-step comparison through the
union, with factor 2, is labelled only; the skeptic notes that it is valid
but looser than the available $C_h(1+q^{|v|})$. The region form of c5 gives
$|\omega^{P,N}(\alpha_v(A))-\omega^{P,N}(A)|\le c_h|Y|e^{|Y|/10^8}
q^{N-|v|_\infty-\max_{y\in Y}\norm y_\infty}\norm A\to0$ for $A\in B(\mathcal H_Y)$,
so with item~1 the limit is invariant under every coarse translation, on
every finite region and on the quasi-local algebra. Nested cubes alone could
not give this: a covariant one-dimensional domain-wall fixture has constant
nested marginals and a translated marginal at trace distance 2 for every $N$.

\paragraph{Non-coarse shifts are rejected, face by face.} Of the 64 fine
translations of the period window $[0,8)\times[0,4)\times[0,2)$, exactly 8
preserve the selected set, every face class and the owner map, namely
$(4v_x,2v_y,v_z)$ with $v\in\{0,1\}^3$. The witness $t=(1,0,0)$ maps the
selected $xy$ face at $r=2$, $q=0$ to the class $r=3$, $q=0$, which is omitted
with owner set $\{0,e_x\}$. Similarly $(0,1,0)$ maps the selected row to an

\subsection{Item 5: correlation functions on the window}
\label{sec:bb2-item5}
\paragraph{The bound.} For $A\in B(\mathcal H_R)$ put
\begin{equation}
 c^{F,N}_A(\theta)=\omega^{F,N}\bigl(A^*T^{F,N}_\theta(A)\bigr)-|\omega^{F,N}(A)|^2,\qquad
 c^\infty_A(\theta)=\omega_\infty\bigl(A^*T_\theta(A)\bigr)-|\omega_\infty(A)|^2 ,
 \label{eq:bb2-correlations}
\end{equation}
centered with the complex mean; centering with $\omega(A)^2$ is rejected,
since at $\theta=0$ it is not the variance. $T_\theta(A)$ lies in the
quasi-local algebra by Nachtergaele--Sims Theorem~4.1
(Section~\ref{sec:ba2-ns}). With the local approximant
$X_r=T^{F1,r}_\theta(A)\in B(\mathcal H_{\Lambda_r})$ and $r=r_N$, the
difference splits into four terms:
\begin{itemize}
\item the finite-box evolution against $X_r$, bounded by the BA2 Cauchy
 constant $K_{\rm F1}/(r-1)$, plus for F2 the BA2 comparison $b(N)$;
\item the state difference on the region $\Lambda_r$ that the approximant
 reaches, not only on $R$, bounded by the region form of item~1;
\item $X_r$ against the limit dynamics, bounded by $K_{\rm F1}/(r-1)$;
\item the means, bounded by $2C'q^{N-1}$.
\end{itemize}
The new lemma $(5N+1)(r_N-1)\le N^3/4$ for $N\ge5$ turns
$b(N)=K_{\rm cmp}(5N+1)N^{-3}$ into $(K_{\rm cmp}/4)/(r_N-1)$. It follows from
$2(r_N-1)\le N-3$ and
\begin{equation}
 N^3-2(5N+1)(N-3)=N^3-10N^2+28N+6=N(N-5)^2+3N+6>0 ,
 \label{eq:bb2-lemma}
\end{equation}
an identity the skeptic used to re-prove it; the lemma was also checked
exactly for $N<4000$. The result, for $N\ge5$, $|\theta|\le8$, both families
and both signs, is
\begin{equation}
 \begin{gathered}
 \bigl|c^{F,N}_A(\theta)-c^\infty_A(\theta)\bigr|\le\norm A^2\Bigl[
 \frac{C_{\rm dyn}}{r_N-1}+c'_{\rm site}\,|\Lambda_{r_N}|\,e^{|\Lambda_{r_N}|/10^8}q^{N-r_N}
 +2C'q^{N-1}\Bigr],\\
 C_{\rm dyn}=2K_{\rm F1}+\frac{K_{\rm cmp}}4=\frac{78057}{622883200000000},
 \end{gathered}
 \label{eq:bb2-item5}
\end{equation}
with $C_{\rm dyn}\approx1.2532\times10^{-10}$ (tier
\recid{polynomial_lieb_robinson}, route \recid{duhamel_inner_f1}, one constant
for both families, margin about $3.99$). The three constants are separate,
each checked against its own target. The reverse gives one constant per
family, $2K$ from the BA2 gate, and these are labelled:
$C_{\rm dyn}[F1]=9261/77860400000000\approx1.1894\times10^{-10}$
(\recid{duhamel_inner_f1}, margin about $4.20$) and
\[
 C_{\rm dyn}[F2]=\frac{1055961408185869563}{15240701171875000000000000000}\approx6.9286\times10^{-11}
\]
(\recid{duhamel_inner_f2}, margin about $7.22$). At $N=5$ the forward bracket
is about $1.0946\times10^{-9}$, with the region term dominant; at $N=10$ it is
about $4.179\times10^{-11}$, with the dynamics term dominant.

\paragraph{The rate, and where it stops.} The inequality holds for every
$N\ge5$, but its rate is explicit only on a certified range. On
$5\le N\le14000$, $N\le10(r_N-1)$ and the region term is at most
$c'_{\rm site}g(N)$ with $g(x)=x^4e^{x^3/10^8}8^{-(x+1)}$. The function $\ln g$
has a convex derivative that is negative at 5, so $g$ decreases and then
increases on the range, and $g(14000)\le g(5)$ exactly. Hence
\begin{equation}
 \begin{gathered}
 N\,\bigl|c^{F,N}_A(\theta)-c^\infty_A(\theta)\bigr|\le K_5\norm A^2\qquad(5\le N\le14000),\\
 K_5=10C_{\rm dyn}+c'_{\rm site}\,g(5)+10C'q^4\approx6.10\times10^{-9},
 \end{gathered}
 \label{eq:bb2-K5}
\end{equation}
about $5.96\times10^{-9}$ and $5.46\times10^{-9}$ with the reverse F1 and F2
constants. The reverse's own block certificate, about $1.415\times10^{-8}$,
is valid but looser. Beyond the range the frozen region factor takes over.
$|\Lambda_{r_N}|$ is of order $N^3$ inside $e^{|\Lambda_{r_N}|/10^8}$ and
eventually beats $q^{N-r_N}$. The skeptic certifies with logarithmic
enclosures that the bracket stays below 2 up to $N=14418$ (the tightest point
is $N=14417$). It is at least 2 at $N=14419$ and at every larger $N$, for the
forward and both reverse constant sets, at the hypothesis and at the admitted
values. Since each centered correlation has modulus at most $\norm A^2$, the
bound is vacuous from $N=14419$. The historical assistant found the same
first exceedance by its own scan, and the reverse certified vacuity at
$N=14421$. The finite-box correlation functions still converge to
$c^\infty_A(\theta)$ along the whole sequence, uniformly for $|\theta|\le8$,
but without a rate outside the certified range. The inequality with any fixed
$r\ge2$ in place of $r_N$ gives
$\limsup_N|c^{F,N}_A-c^\infty_A|\le C_{\rm dyn}\norm A^2/(r-1)$, and $r$ is
arbitrary. This does not re-choose $r_N$ for any quantitative statement.

\paragraph{The rejected overclaim and defect D12.} The forward report states
that the rate is $O(1/N)$ while the state terms decay geometrically, the cube
volume growing only like $N^3$. That paragraph is false from $N=14419$ and is
rejected; it enters no gate text. The forward checker evaluates item~5 only
for $N\le59$, and its enclosure $e^x\le1/(1-x)$ cannot be applied from
$N=465$. The forward's inequality and constants are correct, and its frozen
files are retained as evidence. The reverse producer found the problem (its
wording finding W1). The contract's required item~5 asks for a rate in $N$ of
$O(1/N)$, which the frozen $r_N$ and region form allow only on a certified
range. The skeptic's own pre-comparison derivation repeated the unqualified
statement, and it records the defect against itself as D12. The gate text
therefore claims no correlation rate beyond the certified range. The
skeptic's planning advice notes that a region form without the exponential,
such as BB1's iterated\_split form $c_{\rm site}\sum_{y\in Y}q^{N-\norm
y_\infty}$, would give a rate for every $N$; that is a candidate goal, not a
result.

\paragraph{What item 5 does not say.} Only the correlation numbers converge.
$\omega^{F,N}$ and $\omega_\infty$ are different states, and one automorphism
group can carry two invariant states with different correlations (the fixture
with $(3\pm4i)/5$), so no equality of GNS dynamics of different states is
asserted. The BA2 constants hold for $|\theta|\le8$ and grow with the window,
so nothing is uniform in time.

\subsection{Evidence and review}
\label{sec:bb2-evidence}
Each producer implements all 34 contract controls as damaging mutations: the
forward checker runs 52 exact checks with 122 rejected mutations, the reverse
59 exact checks with 162. The skeptic's pre-comparison package (81 checks, 38
controls, 142 rejected mutations) was committed after the forward commit and
before the reverse commit, so the forward could not have read it. The
post-comparison checker runs 11 checks. The review adds 68 validator
weakenings, one per control per producer, and 13 must-abort source edits,
all caught. It also records 6 silent edits: three are caught only by the
review's value validator and three by the reverse checker itself. The two
unmutated copies reproduce the frozen results. The 18 discharge controls are
described above. All four producer replays reproduce the frozen outputs byte
for byte under normal and optimized Python. Both closures (42 files each)
verify, and each producer holds exactly its 38 declared inputs.

Both producers disclosed name-only exposures to untracked file names of other
agents after their first freeze; they opened none of them, and the state
constants are fixed by the contract. One of the skeptic's replay outputs was
written outside the checkout by an empty shell variable. It is identical to
the others and is disclosed. The review found no blocking issue for the
loop. Its recorded findings include:
\begin{itemize}

 gate-field value, which the Jung assistant found outside the closed
 vocabulary;
\item the reverse's item-5 map, which omits c3 for its F2 part, and its

\item the non-discriminating ``exactly 1'' brackets;
\item the contract readings of the union route (D2), the composite $C_{\rm
 dyn}$ (D3, D10), the untargeted $c'_{{\rm site},2}$ (D5), inherited control

 outcomes (D9) and the scope of the item-3 inheritance (D4, D11).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Meaning.} BB2 establishes convergence of the named constructions.
The reduced densities of F1 and F2 converge as whole sequences, at the rate
$q^{N-1}$ per coarse $\ell^\infty$ step at fixed spacing, to one common limit
on every finite region. That limit is every AQ1 and every F2 subsequential
limit and is invariant under coarse translations, and its correlation
functions on $|\theta|\le8$ are the limits of the finite-box ones. It is not
uniqueness of any ground state and says nothing about states outside the
named constructions. It is not equality of GNS dynamics of different states,
and nothing in it is uniform in time or in the lattice spacing.
