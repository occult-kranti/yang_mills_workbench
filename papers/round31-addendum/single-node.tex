\section{AT5: one certified datum at a declared nonzero interaction}
\label{sec:at5}

\textbf{Contribution \projecttag{HNM-C-AT5}.} After AT4's review, a new contract
fixed $\tau=10^{-14}$, $s=1$, $L=10^9$ and the same absolute point-error target
$10^{-6}$. The smaller coupling selects a different interacting Hamiltonian and
its own AQ subsequential state. It does not repair AT4 retroactively or identify
the states at the two couplings. The increased $L$ changes a proof resource.

Apply \eqref{eq:certificate} at the new parameters, using outward rational
enclosures $D^+$, $\pi^-$ and the logarithm's upper bound. Let $\mathcal E^+$ be
the resulting complete model-comparison allowance and
$f^-\le e^{-3}/4\le f^+$. Export the exact rational datum and radius
\begin{equation}
 d=\frac{f^-+f^+}{2},\qquad
 r=\mathcal E^++\frac{f^+-f^-}{2},\qquad
 |C_\tau(1)-d|\le r.
 \label{eq:export}
\end{equation}
The evaluated forward datum is
\begin{equation}
 d=\frac{995741367357278859586848313}
 {80000000000000000000000000000}
 =0.0124467670919659857448356039125.
 \label{eq:datum}
\end{equation}
Its exact rational radius satisfies
\begin{equation}
 r<8.086109564653\times10^{-7}<10^{-6}.
 \label{eq:node-radius}
\end{equation}
A short, outward-rounded presentation of the actual interval is
\begin{equation}
 C_{10^{-14}}(1)\in
 [\,0.0124459584810095,\;0.0124475757029225\,].
 \label{eq:node-interval}
\end{equation}
The unrounded rational endpoints are in the
\repo{research/round31/forward/at5/output/results.json}{frozen AT5 output}.
This is an analytic enclosure for the actual interacting scalar, not a
finite-box solver result. The exact free-reference value remains inside the
interval, so the interaction-induced shift is unresolved.

The independently implemented reverse calculation retains the more
conservative tail $4s/(\pi L)$ and also passes. Both report interval width equal
to twice their exported radius. The forward width is about
$1.61722191293055\times10^{-6}$: it exceeds $10^{-6}$ while correctly satisfying
the required \emph{point-error} tolerance. Radius and width must not be confused.

The calculators accept exact rational inputs and enforce the zero selected
triple, $|\tau|\le10^{-8}$, positive time/cutoff/scales and their frozen-design
constraints. Binary floats, booleans, malformed/nonfinite values and model
deformations are rejected. Admissible inputs with a broad radius return an
explicit insufficient result. Both coupling signs receive the same absolute
certificate; the exact $\tau=0$ branch uses its separate free identity. Keeping
the old cutoff at the new coupling fails because of its tail; keeping the old
coupling at the new cutoff fails because of its state term. Neither failure is
concealed.

Directed arithmetic uses integer square-root brackets, alternating Taylor
enclosures with argument reduction and positive squaring, positive logarithm
series with an explicit remainder, and Machin/arctangent bounds for $\pi$.
Every admission decision is rational arithmetic, and the exponential midpoint's
own error is included in \eqref{eq:export}. Separate normal and optimized
producer runs reproduce their frozen outputs. The reviewer audited the APIs,
domain mutations, radius sums and preservation of the free reference.
