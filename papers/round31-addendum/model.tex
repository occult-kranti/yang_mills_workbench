\section{Model, inherited premises and fixed physical clock}

This addendum starts from the reviewed AQ construction of Round29 and the
same-state Wilson estimates of Round30. Its compactness/GNS construction
retains the prior-strategy attribution to Gauvin's Supplement~A.10, with the
actual AQ operator and dynamics dictionary checked separately
\cite{gauvin,ns,aq}. This imports neither that source's different-model
constants nor its larger physical claims. The theory here is at fixed lattice
spacing. A coarse site $b\in\mathbb Z^3$ owns all three positive links from each
fine tail $(4b_x+r,2b_y+q,b_z)$, $0\le r<4$, $0\le q<2$: exactly 24 untruncated
$L^2(\mathrm{SU}(2))$ link factors. Both original endpoint gauge actions are
retained. Write $C_e=-\sum_a X_{ea}^2$, with generators $i\sigma_a/2$, and
$W_f=\tfrac12\tr U_f$ for the real fundamental Wilson plaquette.

The selected strip coefficients obey
\begin{equation}
 |\lambda_L|,|\lambda_R|\le\alpha/2,\qquad |\mu_M|\le\alpha/8.
 \label{eq:selected}
\end{equation}
Round31 selects $(\lambda_L,\mu_M,\lambda_R)=(0,0,0)$. This is an admissible
closed-box parameter choice, not a replacement of a nonzero selected vacuum by
Haar. The 21 remaining anchored face classes have the nonzero coefficient
$\nu=\alpha\tau/24$. Whole stars
$S=\{0,e_x,e_y,e_z\}$ are retained only when $b+S\subset\Lambda_N$, where
$\Lambda_N=[-N,N]^3$. The raw finite Hamiltonian is
\begin{equation}
 H_{N,\tau}^{\rm raw}
 =\alpha\sum_{e\text{ owned by }\Lambda_N}C_e
 -\frac{\alpha\tau}{24}
   \sum_{b+S\subset\Lambda_N}\sum_{f\in O_b}W_f,
 \qquad |O_b|=21.
 \label{eq:model}
\end{equation}
Its actual ground energy is subtracted before spectral statements. A magnetic
additive scalar, an on-site reference ground shift and the actual interacting
ground energy are different objects; scalar cancellation in a conjugation does
not license omitting ground centering in a correlation.

This is a \emph{patterned, nonuniform magnetic model}. Three selected $xy$ classes
have zero coefficient. Equality with the uniform Kogut--Susskind model would
require $\lambda_L=\mu_M=\lambda_R=\nu$, which fails for $\tau\ne0$ here. The
original AL dictionary $\tau=96/g^4$ for equal coefficients is not assigned to
this subfamily. Small $\tau$ does not establish a weak-bare-coupling or continuum
trajectory.

The positive energy $\alpha$, action $\hbar$, lattice spacing $a$ and comparison
energy $E_\star$ remain fixed. AQ uses $\delta=\alpha/8$. Let $\omega_\tau$ be
any of the centered-box subsequential states supplied by the AQ construction
for the declared coefficients, and let $H_\tau\ge0$ be its actual physical GNS
energy generator, with vacuum $\Omega_\tau$. The inherited gap in that
representation is at least $\alpha/16$. No uniqueness of all thermodynamic
states is assumed. Both the reference and interacting clocks use
\begin{equation}
 K_\tau=H_\tau/\alpha,\qquad
 s=\frac{\alpha t_E}{\hbar},\qquad
 t=\frac{\alpha t_{\rm real}}{\hbar}.
 \label{eq:clock}
\end{equation}
Thus $s,t,\tau$ are dimensionless, while a reduced inverse-energy form has
units $1/\alpha$. A real-time cutoff $L$ is a proof resource, not a physical
deformation. Changing $\tau$ between prospectively frozen investigations is an
explicit interaction change, not a redefinition of units.

For the original $xz$ Wilson plaquette at the origin set
\begin{equation}
 m_\tau=\omega_\tau(W),\qquad
 \chi_\tau=(\pi_\tau(W)-m_\tau)\Omega_\tau,\qquad
 C_\tau(s)=\langle\chi_\tau,e^{-sK_\tau}\chi_\tau\rangle.
 \label{eq:observable}
\end{equation}
It is dimensionless and centered in the actual state. Its complete-factor cover
is $R=\{0,e_z\}$: 48 stored links and 36 original endpoints, with seven incident
whole-star anchors
\begin{equation}
 R-S=\{0,-e_x,-e_y,-e_z,e_z,e_z-e_x,e_z-e_y\}.
 \label{eq:seven}
\end{equation}
This seven-star bulk region must not be replaced by the two-star orthant
configuration. The on-site product reference at the chosen zero selected triple
is Haar; its single-plaquette vector has variance $1/4$ and energy $3\alpha$.
Those facts describe the reference, not an exact interacting spectrum.
