\begin{abstract}
We give analytic enclosures for the actual centered Wilson correlation in the
zero-selected, nonuniform AQ lattice family, retaining its chosen
infinite-volume subsequential state and physical energy generator. A local
reset estimate, seven-star bounded-perturbation comparison and positive
Poisson integration yield a complete error budget. At the initially fixed
coupling $\tau=10^{-8}$, that budget misses the $10^{-6}$ point-error target.
After this reviewed failure, a prospectively declared smaller nonzero coupling
$\tau=10^{-14}$ yields a certified datum at $s=1$. A third investigation keeps
that coupling and state fixed, proves a continuous bound on $0\le s\le128$,
and exports all 4097 rational proxies with actual-correlation error at most
$10^{-6}$. The resulting reduced inverse-energy form $I=\alpha\mathcal R$
has a certified interval of width below $1/500$, improved below $1/2500$ by
paying the actual endpoint tail. The stronger displayed enclosure is
$I\in[0.083174562675757,\,0.083535946641292]$.
It contains the free value $1/12$: no interaction-induced shift is resolved.
These are analytic actual-model bounds, not measured or simulated interacting
trajectories. They establish neither uniform Wilson theory nor a continuum
Yang--Mills construction.
\end{abstract}
