\subsection{Historical and unconventional material: questions, not premises}
Newton's selected folios of \href{https://newton.dlib.indiana.edu/text/ALCH00081/normalized}{\emph{Of Natures obvious laws}} motivate asking which terms or reservoirs have been omitted from a proposed mechanism. His \href{https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/normalized/NATP00100}{October 1666 fluxions tract}, Proposition 7 and selected reconstruction passages, motivates testing whether an inverse returns the originating relation. Missing rendered formulas prevent a complete transcription or derivation audit. Neither source validates an alchemical mechanism or supplies a modern gauge-theory term.

Tesla's \href{https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/My_Inventions}{\emph{My Inventions}, section VI} was inspected in a transcription at selected retrospective mystical and mechanistic passages. It is neither the original magazine scan nor a controlled observation. \href{https://patents.google.com/patent/US685957A/en}{US685957A}, around figures 1, 3 and 4 and the claims, specifies incident radiation, storage, receiver control and output conditions. Its proposed apparatus does not establish unrestricted loading or instantaneous transmission. The associated forum exchange is a record of claims to test against the patent, not corroborating physical evidence.

Jung's \href{https://www.gutenberg.org/files/48225/48225-h/48225-h.htm}{\emph{Collected Papers on Analytical Psychology}} was read in the verified 1920 second-edition reprint: selected occult-phenomena, number-dream and rumour passages are three writings at one URL, not three books. Publisher opening excerpts of the \href{https://www.jstor.org/stable/jj.34571099.5}{\emph{I Ching} foreword} and \href{https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctv10crg6v}{``Psychology and Spiritualism''} do not amount to reading the full essays. The operational question is whether a fixed interpretation separates a prediction from post hoc selection, remembered matches and ignored misses. Two purposively sampled discussions do not establish prevalence, authenticate every circulating quotation or verify paranormal effects.

There is \textbf{no newly read government-primary source} in these Round28 ledgers and no newly inspected original Pauli letter. Earlier FBI, DOE and CIA material remains in Round27 at its original reading depth and is not counted again. Clay is an official mathematical-problem source, not a government program. Historical or mystical interpretations add no Hamiltonian coefficient, measured frequency, physical clock or continuum map.

\subsection{Technical comparisons and remaining applicability tests}
Three lenses revisit \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.13907}{Del Vecchio--Fr\"ohlich--Pizzo, arXiv:2108.13907}, with selected induction, domain, assumption and theorem passages recorded separately. The 2021 preprint and 2023 journal publication motivate an applicability audit. An existential stability threshold cannot be cited as an evaluated numerical threshold or a completed workbench induction. Selected local-transformation passages in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1105.0675}{Bravyi--DiVincenzo--Loss, arXiv:1105.0675} and exact-algebra passages in \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.12621}{Ying--Li--Po, arXiv:2509.12621} reinforce full transformed-operator accounting. Spin and qubit fixtures cannot replace the full $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ support and domain arguments.

Tesla's selected \href{https://arxiv.org/html/2606.20441v1}{noise-canceling observable} and \href{https://arxiv.org/html/2609.13108v1}{spacetime mitigation} papers motivate retaining model bias, joint omitted events, normalization and sampling resources. They imply no automatic cancellation of this workbench's error disks. Jung's \href{https://academic.oup.com/biomet/article/113/2/asaf070/8321921}{relative-fit inference comparison} distinguishes inference under misspecification from a true-model statement. The \href{https://arxiv.org/html/2608.10350v1}{Hamiltonian observation-geometry comparison} makes sampled data a parameter pseudometric unless injectivity is proved. Deterministic interval bounds have no statistical coverage interpretation without a sampling model.

Penrose's selected \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.10610}{gauge-fixed Hamiltonian} and \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.12323}{variational ground-state} comparisons require the full induced generator, Gram matrix and omitted channels. A variational energy is not a certified gap. \href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.11374v2}{Dahlqvist's exact integration work} motivates exact quotient calculations, with its surface, group and limit assumptions retained. A \href{https://arxiv.org/html/2602.12145v1}{quantum-channel spin-network extension} changes the dynamics and is not silently imported into a closed Hamiltonian model.

The supplemental Feynman review inspects selected assumptions and theorem passages of the \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19362}{Faizal--Shabir four-dimensional construction claim}; it does not audit the entire 593-page compilation. Questions about comparison direction, quantitative defect margins, time factors and tuning-map rank require checking the other supporting sections before a conclusion. The panel neither validates nor refutes the whole construction. \href{https://arxiv.org/html/2608.10133v2}{Nair's lower-dimensional proof outline} was inspected at selected framework, equations and regularization passages. Its closed domains, regulator limits and vacuum/energy centering remain applicability obligations; it is not a four-dimensional matching theorem. The formalization lead \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15770}{2603.15770} was unread by that lens in this pass and excluded from its reviewed count.

Version discrepancies remain explicit. Two experimental HTML headers display dates different from their version histories; no later revision is inferred. Dahlqvist's landing abstract and version-2 theorem use different genus thresholds, so the specified theorem/version controls the theorem-scope claim. The two different math-ph identifiers in screening and bibliographic records are not merged. Root metadata checks are separate from full-proof audits. The ledger retains exact source pointers and reading limits rather than hiding these discrepancies.

\paragraph{How the survey changes the tests.} For the homogeneous branch, deleting a quadratic class, overlapping ordered word, factorial coefficient, scalar correction or transported remainder must fail reconstruction. For canonical readouts, retain higher odd terms, joint/adversarial error, phase and clock uncertainty and denominators containing zero; a cancellation coefficient containing unknown $q$ must be candidate-indexed or carry its estimation error. For graph enlargement, require exact Gram null relations, every reached and omitted channel, boundary return and an added-region observable. These are source-motivated controls for frozen goals, not new physical premises or extra loops.

The \href{https://www.claymath.org/millennium/yang-mills-the-maths-gap/}{official status} and \href{https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/yangmills.pdf}{Jaffe--Witten problem statement} remain the target references. Nontrivial four-dimensional construction, the required axioms, compact simple groups, a positive physical mass gap and scale matching remain distinct obligations. Source counts, network edges and loop completion supply no defensible percentage of that theorem.
