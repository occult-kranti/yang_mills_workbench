\begin{abstract}
We collect the derivations, computational certificates, counterexamples, and unresolved obligations of a gauge-theory research workbench. Its surviving record begins with regulated Maxwell--Dirac and gravitational consistency studies and develops into finite-graph and fixed-spacing $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ calculations. The main results include full-representation one-square spectral certificates, shared-link Haar contractions, conditional local stability, projected-memory and boundary-response formulas, canonical slow-time Wilson readouts, and controlled physical heat evolution. The latest cycle constructs a graph-preserving Gaussian correction with an explicit full-source residual, an explicitly enumerated 293-state physical compression on an 18-vertex graph, and an all-time evaluated heat approximation with true-output-relative error below $3.7\times10^{-5}$ on a specified preparation class. A restricted compact-time filter also gives per-source contraction with finite original support-cardinality-weighted norms. We explain the brief derivations, parameter domains, changes from earlier results, possible applications, and failure modes, and provide calculators, plots, a contribution catalog, and a skeptical audit. The record contains 121 study or loop entries across Rounds 3--26; separate Round1/2 packages are absent. The double-star marker identifies a contribution newly derived or implemented in this workbench, not established priority in the literature. The results concern explicitly different models and do not construct continuum Yang--Mills theory or establish its mass gap.
\end{abstract}
