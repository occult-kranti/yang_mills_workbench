A full-rank fit inside one finite family does not identify unrestricted positive mobility. For N initial slopes, the N functionals on degree-at-most-N polynomials have a nonzero nullspace. Check full marginal support so a nonzero polynomial is a nonzero function, normalize by its coefficient l1 norm, and use an explicit baseline positivity margin before adding it. This construction concerns a finite list of initial slopes. Test higher derivatives or complete time curves before extending the ambiguity claim to them.

Treat fitted or introduced mobility coefficients as model deformations until physical matching is independently established. Distinguish convergence on fixed vectors from a supremum over moving vectors. A perturbation with nonzero constant operator norm can tend strongly to zero.
