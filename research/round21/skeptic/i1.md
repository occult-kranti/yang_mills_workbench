# Independent skeptical review of I1

Decision recommendation: **accept the complete locality dictionary and the
qualitative fixed-spacing stability application; retain quantitative
insufficiency**. Both directions were frozen before this review. No producer
source was changed. This review adds no research loop.

## Source theorem and physical scope

I inspected the source's definitions, Eq.(3), and Theorems 1–3 on printed pages
2–4 of [Yarotsky, math-ph/0411042v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math-ph/0411042).
The arbitrary site spaces may be infinite dimensional, and onsite operators may
be unbounded, nonnegative and self-adjoint with a unique gap-one vacuum.
Interactions are bounded self-adjoint operators supported on translates of one
fixed finite set. Theorems 1–3 do not require translation invariance; that
additional premise appears after Theorem 3 for quasiparticle results. Thus using
varying onsite strip coefficients or decoupled negative-site spectators does not
violate the stated source hypotheses.

The theorem gives constants depending only on the finite range, independent of
local dimension and the finite volume. The reconstructed normalized coefficient
is `epsilon=7|tau|`. Therefore the symbolic interval

`0<|tau|<min(c1(S),1/(2*c2(S)))/7`

does imply `Delta >= alpha/16`, and an energy-family bound requires a positive
lower bound on `alpha/E_star`. Neither direction has evaluated `c1` or `c2` or
certified a specific numerical nonzero coupling. The source proofs were not
independently reconstructed by this audit. This is an application of an existing
theorem, with priority of the model-specific dictionary unverified.

The claim concerns a homogeneous **omitted** coupling on the selected-strip
reference; the whole Hamiltonian is not asserted to have one uniform coefficient
on every face. The source construction is on the full link-observable algebra.
Identification of a gauge-invariant observable GNS representation remains a
separate task. Fixed-spacing existence of this gapped ground-state construction
does not settle a nontrivial four-dimensional continuum Yang–Mills limit.

## Independent geometry and norm checks

I rebuilt each face as a four-vertex oriented path, recovered its undirected edge
tails, and divided those tails into `4x2x1` blocks without importing either I1
checker. The six omitted support classes have multiplicities
`1,3,1,10,2,4`, respectively for
`{0,ex}`, `{0,ey}`, `{0,ex,ey}`, `{0,ez}`, `{0,ex,ez}`, `{0,ey,ez}`.
Their sum is 21. No omitted face is contained in one block, and actual
three-block supports occur. Tail ownership of the selected strip uses ten links;
the complete block owns 24, leaving fourteen free factors.

The norm coefficient is independently `21*(alpha/24)/(alpha/8)=7`. The asserted
equality, rather than just an upper bound, uses the common signed coupling and
simultaneous Wilson value one near the identity configuration. Haar measure has
full support on the finite link product, so the zero measure of the exact
identity configuration does not defeat the essential-supremum argument. Equality
need not hold for arbitrary independently signed face weights; the stated
homogeneous coefficient premise is necessary and retained.

Finite phase enumeration checks implementation; Euclidean division and the
explicit tail formula establish completeness over arbitrary translations. It
would be wrong to promote a finite sample alone to that universal statement.

## Boundary audit

**Whole-star empty boundary.** The source keeps an anchor term only when all of
`b+S`, `S={0,ex,ey,ez}`, is inside the volume. The displayed formula in each
report matches Eq.(3). A face's smaller actual support is a different retention
rule. I independently verified that `{0,ez}` contains ten actual omitted faces
anchored at zero, and `{0,ex}` contains one, while neither contains a whole star.
The reports correctly retain this discrepancy.

**Actual-support finite boundary.** Padding the volume to contain every whole
star, but setting each anchor operator to only its actually supported face
subset, is an admissible inhomogeneous interaction with the same norm bound.
All padding sites decouple. Tensor gap comparison gives the original subsystem's
gap because its excitations embed with padded vacua. This construction is valid
for each finite volume; it does not identify different thermodynamic state
limits by itself.

**Literal rectangular link graph.** Boundary links must have both endpoints in
the literal vertex box. Full tail-block ownership is not generally the same
graph. The forward report handles arbitrary clipping by changing the finite
onsite reference to the retained strip components and free links; the inherited
A1 component estimate supplies gap `alpha/8`. The reverse report's narrower
aligned-box statement uses vertex counts `(4Nx,2Ny,Nz)` and complete strips;
there the added terminal outgoing links are free spectators. These statements
are compatible. Contiguous x clipping yields exactly `L,M,R,LM,MR,LMR`; it cannot
silently produce a separated `LR` component. Empty sites are harmless
one-dimensional factors. Because the source constants depend only on the range,
these finite-volume changes of site spaces do not change the symbolic bound.

**Positive orthant.** The source is stated on `Z^3`. The reports explicitly add
gapped spectator sites at negative coarse coordinates and set every outside
anchor interaction to zero. Since the retained star offsets are nonnegative,
no positive anchor reaches a negative site. Symmetric exhausted volumes factor
into the source-prescription orthant system and negative spectators. Taking the
source local-state limit therefore produces the claimed orthant state, and the
cyclic orthant subspace inherits the generator gap through this decoupling.
This does not establish the same state for arbitrary literal-box boundary
sequences or identify it with the earlier summable product representation.

No hidden global summability premise is needed for this argument. For nonzero
homogeneous omitted coupling the total absolute interaction budget grows with
the number of anchor groups; substituting the older summable-tail proof would
be an invalid inference, and neither report makes it.
