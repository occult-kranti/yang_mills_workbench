## Recommendations for the next three goals

The following goals are proposed only after the completed M2 review. They are
**queued research**, not admitted new results. For each, freeze the first-loop
contract before execution and choose its second loop only from that first
loop's evidence and skeptical objection. The advisor should preserve the
first-three-then-two selection discipline.

**N. Test a sharper local real-time comparison.** M2 bounds evolution on
`B Omega` by a global vacuum residual. Its centered ground-state correlation
also has a Heisenberg formulation in which the scalar ground energy cancels.
Test whether a bounded local-commutator comparison removes the sigma term
from the dynamical error while preserving complete reference factor support.
One candidate target, not accepted here, is

`|C_q(t)-C_0(t)| <= ||A||||B||[6d_q+(2alpha tau_q |t|/hbar)D_FB(q)]`.

The first loop must prove or reject that inequality using a strong operator
integral with a valid uniform norm estimate, including complex centering,
negative times and arbitrary bounded local observables. If proved, examine
the resulting sufficient window exponents; a formal power count alone does
not admit a new window theorem. The second loop should address the actual
remaining endpoint or support-growth obstruction, selected after review.
This targets the specific information lost in M2's otherwise valid bound.

**O. Make the homogeneous local stability interval quantitative.** Begin from
I's exact four-site star dictionary and retained unitary remainder. Construct
an explicit interaction norm and count every support generated by overlapping
stars. The first-loop acceptance test is a numerical contraction or a proved
obstruction with the exact missing inequality identified. Check unbounded
onsite domains and fixed positive physical scale. A finite-star spectrum, a
fitted constant or dropping the remainder cannot pass. Select the second
loop from whether that local iteration closes; it may test an alternative
applicable theorem or the boundary conditions of a successfully constructed
interval. No new interaction coefficient should be called physical matching
without an independent derivation.

**P. Test physical generator matching with held-out dynamic information.**
Specify one concrete proposed map between the full-link physical model and
the conditional mobility family. Freeze its state space, observables,

Try to derive a matching identity or a discriminating mismatch. Reserve
observations beyond the fitted initial slopes, using L2's curvature test as
an example of why a held-out check matters. A synthetic forward/inverse fit
certifies arithmetic only. If no physical map or observations are available,
the accepted outcome is a precise underidentification or incompatibility
statement, not a fabricated calibration. Its second loop is selected after
that actual result.

**The final two goals remain unselected.** After N, O and P complete their
