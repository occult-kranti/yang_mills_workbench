## Uniformity in time and the energy subtraction

The common-domain bounded-perturbation Duhamel identity extends by density
to every vector as a strong vector integral. The norm estimate is

`sup_|t|<=T ||(U_q(t)-U_ref(t))psi||`
`<= (T/hbar) sup_|s|<=T ||V_q U_ref(s)psi||`.

For fixed psi and finite T the reference orbit is norm compact. A finite
epsilon-net and uniform boundedness turn pointwise strong convergence into
uniform convergence on that orbit. Both reports give this compactness step;
pointwise-time convergence alone would not state the full claimed result.
The oriented integral also covers negative times.

The scalar correction has error at most
`T sigma_q² ||psi||/(hbar*gbar)`, because
`|e_q|<=sigma_q²/gbar`. Thus subtracting the ground energy preserves the
compact-time strong limit. Neither this bound nor the fixed-T compactness
argument establishes convergence on arbitrary growing time windows.
