## Forcing norm and relative-bound obstruction

Each of the 21 omitted anchored faces has two independent reference-Haar links
outside all selected strips. Two different square faces share at most one
link, leaving an unmatched free link that kills their cross expectation.
Internal entanglement within a selected strip does not affect this free factor.
The one-face second moment is `1/4`, so independent arithmetic gives
`||phi Omega||²=21 tau²/(9*4)=7 tau²/12`. Whole-face independence is unnecessary
and is not claimed. The zero mean supplies `v=phi Omega` in the orthogonal
complement of the vacuum.

For nonzero tau, the inverse of `H0|Q` exists with norm at most one. It maps v
into `D(H0)`, since `H0 u=v`. The strictly positive `a=<u,H0 u>=<u,v>` makes
the trial form ratio for `Omega+t u` diverge as `2/|t|`; normalization does not
alter the ratio. This is a proof against the proposed pure relative bound,
rather than a finite-sample inference. It does not exclude a bound with an
additive bounded term. At tau zero, phi vanishes and the obstruction disappears.

## Domain, sign and exact remainder

The finite-rank skew operator `S=|u><Omega|-|Omega><u|` has range in
`span{Omega,u} subset D(H0)`, and `exp(tS)-I` has range in the same finite
dimensional space. Both conjugation directions therefore preserve the operator
domain. The commutator `[S,H0]=-A`, with
`A=|v><Omega|+|Omega><v|`, extends boundedly with norm `||v||`.
I also verified this sign in a separate exact three-level fixture with unequal
excitation energies 2 and 5, without importing either producer implementation.

Since `P phi P=0`, `phi-A=Q phi Q`. Thus the correct transformation is
`exp(S)(H0+phi)exp(-S)`. The reverse sign doubles the leading mixing term.
The two reports' remainder integrals agree because
`[S,[S,H0]]=-[S,A]`. Both retain the factor `1/2` from the weighted first
integral, giving

`||R|| <= ||S|| ||v|| + 2 ||S|| ||phi||`

and hence

`||R|| <= (7/12 + 14 sqrt(7/12)) tau² <= (707/60) tau²`.

I independently checked the rational rounding:
`(4/5)²>7/12` and `7/12+14*(4/5)=707/60`. Norm convergence of these integrals
uses bounded commutators and a bounded generator, while the original H0 remains
unbounded on its unchanged domain. No smallness of tau is needed for this
single-star identity. To express R in physical energy units multiply by
`delta=alpha/8`.

The compressed first-order part is purely relative because
`|<psi,Q phi Q psi>| <= 7|tau| ||Q psi||² <= 7|tau| <psi,H0 psi>`.
The retained R can again mix the vacuum with excited states. Removing R changes
the Hamiltonian and cannot be advertised as exact decoupling.

pure-relative obstruction, and the absence of a certified numerical global
interval. I checked the alternating rational sine/cosine enclosures and the

are different discriminating tests, not independent lattice spectral solves.
Their wrong-sign and omitted-remainder controls genuinely change the matrices
and distinguish the claimed defect.

The primary-source audit identifies where unspecified range-dependent constants
enter the theorem, contraction argument, commutator estimate and resolvent
expansion. It explicitly records that the detailed earlier proof was not
quantitatively reconstructed. This supports an **insufficient extraction
attempt**, not a theorem that computable constants cannot exist. The alternative
relative-perturbation source is not silently applied without its additional
hypothesis dictionary.

The projector in this lemma is the vacuum projector of one finite four-site
star. An entire infinite-lattice vacuum projector would not have the claimed
locality. Overlapping stars produce additional commutators and extended
supports; no all-order summable interaction estimate or convergent infinite
product has been established. Consequently the evaluated local fixture at
tau `1/64` supplies local bounds only. It cannot admit that tau into I1's
existential global interval. Fixed-spacing stability and continuum physical
mass matching remain separate.

Finite-rank vacuum dressing and Duhamel remainder estimates are established
operator methods. The explicit Haar forcing coefficient and instantiated

unverified. No material implication error was found within this scoped claim.
