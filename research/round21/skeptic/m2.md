## Independent quantitative reconstruction

Freeze the canonical full-link profile, with fixed `alpha/E_star>0`, fixed
spacing and `hbar>0`, `0<eta<1`, `0<q<1`, and
`tau_q=eta/[8B(q)]`. Put `gbar=alpha(1-eta)/8`,
`d_q=||P_q-P_Omega||`, and `sigma_q=||V_q Omega||`. The premises are
`d_q<=sigma_q/gbar`, `|e_q|<=sigma_q^2/gbar` and M1's complete-factor local
bound. The numerical dyadic J2 gap is not a premise for this profile.

Let `U_q(t)=exp[-it(H_q-e_q)/hbar]`, `U_0(t)=exp[-itH_ref/hbar]`, and define

`C_q(t)=<Psi_q,A* U_q(t) B Psi_q>-conj(omega_q(A))*omega_q(B)`.

For bounded complex A and B, pure-state trace duality gives

`|omega_q(A*U_q B)-omega_0(A*U_q B)|<=2d_q ||A||||B||`.

The subtraction contributes two additional differences. Expanding its product
with one intermediate mixed product and bounding each mean by the relevant
operator norm gives

`|conj(omega_q(A))*omega_q(B)-conj(omega_0(A))*omega_0(B)|`
`<=4d_q ||A||||B||`.

The coefficient six is therefore a sufficient sum of `2+2+2`; it is not
claimed optimal. This argument uses complex conjugation correctly and includes
time zero.

Let F_B contain every complete reference factor meeting B, and let E(F_B)
contain every link in those factors. The reference Hamiltonian is a sum of
independent complete-factor Hamiltonians. Hence reference evolution factorizes
and `U_0(s)B Omega=B_s Omega`, where B_s is bounded, has the same norm and
acts on those same complete factors for every real s. This is an operator
support statement, not a claim that B preserves an unbounded operator domain.
M1 applies with this fixed support for every s:

`||V_q U_0(s)B Omega||<=||B||[sigma_q+2alpha tau_q D_FB(q)]`.

Since V_q is bounded self-adjoint, the bounded-perturbation Duhamel identity
on B Omega is legitimate by density, even when B Omega is outside D(H_ref).
The scalar ground-energy phase has error at most `|t||e_q|/hbar`. Thus

`|C_q(t)-C_0(t)|<=||A||||B||`
`*[6d_q+(|t|/hbar)(|e_q|+sigma_q+2alpha tau_q D_FB(q))]`.

Negative times obey the same estimate using an oriented integral and absolute
time. Inserting `D_FB(q)<=|E(F_B)|/6` and the spectral premises gives the
contract's second bound uniformly for `|t|<=T`.

The full bounded local-factor algebra is irreducible in the fixed reference

A_n over its finitely many endpoint gauge groups. The weak operator average

`||E(A_n)Psi_q-xi||<=||A_n Psi_q-xi||`. Hence the physical cyclic space
equals the fixed invariant subspace. This establishes equality for the
specified bounded observable algebra, without asserting irreducibility of

Psi_q and its nonzero spectral threshold is at least the profile's gbar.
At each fixed q the summable interaction tail and finite-factor reference

No assertion of point-norm continuity for arbitrary bounded local operators
is required. The nonzero Wilson variance proved above makes the eventual
