# Paired research in this workbench

When continuing the requested paired physics program, the root integrator is the advisor and owns contracts, decisions, shared skills, site integration and publication. Assign separate forward and reverse researchers and a skeptic when the user requests the team workflow. Each worker owns only its assigned directory and must not alter another direction's accepted source or evidence.

Freeze a loop contract before execution. Each researcher writes a mathematical report and an executable check under `research/round21/<direction>/<loop>/`, with fresh outputs and source bindings. Do not read the other direction's current solution until both reports are frozen. Shared contracts and inherited premises must be named; separate implementations are not independent physical observations.

The advisor selects each second loop only after the first-loop evidence and skeptical objection. Goals L and M remain unselected until I, J and K each complete their two loops. Completed research may have a limited or insufficient target verdict; count executed loops honestly and preserve unresolved goals.

Use a fixed positive physical energy reference. Classify added parameters and their units. Changes in mobility, spatial profile or action are separate model deformations unless a matching proof identifies them with the original theory. Require complete support and theorem hypotheses; exact fixtures do not prove infinite-volume completeness.

Historical Round19/20 gates are immutable. Repair an admission defect through a new source inventory and explicit repair record. Do not admit interpreter caches, generated local environment files or untracked dependencies. Validate a staged release from its Git tree before publication. Scientific priority is unverified unless a checked primary-source comparison establishes the actual difference.
