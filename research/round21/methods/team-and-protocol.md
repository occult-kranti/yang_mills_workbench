# Round21 team and evidence protocol

The root integrator acts as advisor. A forward researcher and reverse researcher independently implement the frozen contract. A separate skeptic first repairs the C2 release inventory, then reviews scientific implications and falsifiers. These are AI-agent reviews, not credentialed human peer review.

I, J and K come from the accepted Round20 post-ten roadmap. L and M are not selected until the six initial gates exist. Loop 2 of each goal is chosen from the actual first-loop findings. Ten research loops are planned; the C2 repair and final audit are separate reviews and do not inflate that count.

Each researcher owns only `forward/<loop>/` or `reverse/<loop>/`. Root owns `contracts/`, `advisor/`, shared integration and publication. The skeptic owns `release/` and `skeptic/`. Both directions freeze their report and executed sources before root exchanges findings. A shared hypothesis or advisor proposal is attributed explicitly.

The gate records a target verdict separately from whether validation passes. Mathematical proofs cover infinite statements; computations check finite identities, geometry, exact coefficients and rejecting cases. Missing source constants or physical data stay missing. Neither an invented parameter nor a passed JSON field supplies them.

Release admission uses actual source paths, fresh execution, structured comparisons and a clean Git-tree check. Historical gates stay immutable. Repeated optimized execution adds no research loops. New profile, mobility or observable parameters must include units, scope, original-case recovery and a discriminating failure test.

The inherited handoff records earlier role reassignments. Those descriptions are historical process notes; the resumed review below records the workers and executions used for this release.
