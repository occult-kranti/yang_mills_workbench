# Parameters and derived quantities

A new symbol closes a missing equation only when its defining relation and applicability are proved. A fitted number cannot replace an unproved convergence or physical matching premise.

| Symbol | Units and role | What it can establish | What it cannot replace |
|---|---|---|---|
| alpha, E_star | Positive physical energies | Fixed physical comparison alpha/E_star | A continuum scaling construction |
| delta=alpha/8 | Inherited reference gap lower scale | Normalize onsite operators | An evaluated perturbation-theorem constant |
| tau | Dimensionless actual omitted-coupling amplitude | Specifies homogeneous I or dyadic J after the profile is declared | Interchange of those models |
| q | Dimensionless spatial decay profile | Continuous family of summable interactions for q<1 | Lattice spacing a or fixed nonzero homogeneous interaction |
| eta | Dimensionless fixed absolute-budget fraction | Canonical ray tau(q)=eta/(8B(q)) | A nonzero homogeneous return limit |
| epsilon_loc=7 abs(tau) | Derived dimensionless local norm | Exact homogeneous anchor interaction budget | Numerical source constants c1(S),c2(S) |
| u=(H0 restricted to Q)^(-1)phi Omega | Dimensionless local vector after normalization | Defines a bounded finite-rank dressing generator | An independent added field |
| S_u | Dimensionless skew-adjoint local operator | Exact unitary change when the remainder is retained | A convergent infinite product of overlapping rotations |
| R | Dimensionless normalized energy operator in I2 | Bounded local quadratic remainder; physical remainder is delta R | An optional term that can be discarded without changing the model |
| d=||P-P_ref|| | Dimensionless projector distance | Transfer of both bounded moments and actual variance | A mass or energy measurement |
| kappa | Dimensionless static conditional-density parameter | Defines rho proportional to exp(kappa S) in the separate F/K model | The lattice Hamiltonian coupling tau |
| c | Positive energy in the conditional diffusion family | Rates after dividing by hbar | Physical Yang–Mills matching inferred from static moments alone |
| zeta | Dimensionless affine mobility deformation | Parameter in m=1+zeta x, reconstructible in a declared family | An inferred original-theory parameter |
| a_obs (written a in K2) | Dimensionless observable-design coefficient | Chooses g_a=x+a x² and its rate sensitivity | Lattice spacing or a new field |
| xi | Dimensionless cubic mobility coefficient | In m=1+zeta x+xi p3; three-rate inverse at kappa=0 | Uniqueness among unrestricted smooth mobilities |
| epsilon_m (written epsilon in L2) | Dimensionless hidden mobility amplitude | Positive family m0+epsilon_m P with all selected initial slopes fixed | Equality of complete time correlations |
| gamma | Dimensionless observation-window exponent | Defines a requested growing-time scale after a proved error bound | A dynamics parameter or proof of endpoint convergence |
| T_q | Physical observation time | Compare correlations on a specified window using hbar/alpha | A matched continuum limit |

Only admitted gates establish loop-specific results. The K/L entries below describe admitted model deformations, not matched physical fields.
