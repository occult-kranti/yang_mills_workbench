# S2 targeted source comparison

Read [Bachmann, Michalakis, Nachtergaele and Sims, arXiv:1102.0842v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1102.0842), Assumption2.1 and Proposition2.4 with equations(2.3)–(2.6), printed pages3–5, plus the S1 targeted reading of Corollary2.8. The construction concerns transport of an isolated spectral projection along an admissible Hamiltonian path. Its filtered generator is not a residual-free inverse on arbitrary equal-energy excited blocks. Here the contract instead fixes a compact triangular filter and an unchanged initial G; the residual is derived explicitly and retained. No source gap threshold, numerical Lieb–Robinson velocity or full theorem application is imported. The domain argument is direct weak integration by parts. The strong-resolvent premise is proved from the inherited actual absolute form bound, rather than inferred from a finite matrix or merely named as a limit.

Inherited O1 and R2 provide the actual support-counting framework and the initial product-reference form. S1 provides the genuine cubic source and source-sector discrimination. The targeted primary comparison establishes method context, not scientific priority or novelty.
