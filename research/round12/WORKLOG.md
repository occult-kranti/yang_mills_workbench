## Accepted direction after equation review

The advisor, solver and skeptic independently identified a high-degree block/Duhamel argument. The common variable is accumulated absolute magnetic action. It yields a factorial truncation bound without adding a physical mass. The original drive remains an inconclusive accuracy example at its published cutoffs; a weaker drive gives a useful exact bound.

The volume review distinguishes a positive bound on each open graph from a constant uniform across graphs. A disconnected tensor benchmark shows why deterioration of an estimate does not establish deterioration of the true gap. Strong-coupling Gibbs results need an explicit relation to a physical Hamiltonian before transfer.

The closure review introduces an existing variance observable into the first exact compact moment identity. Its value is constrained by the measure and higher identities. Formula-specific critique of arXiv:2608.05415v1 is retained with exact conventions and independent matrix/rank checks; it does not invalidate Haar integration by parts or every scalar ansatz.
