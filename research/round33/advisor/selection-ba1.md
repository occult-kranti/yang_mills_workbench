# Selection of BA1 (sub-round 1, investigation 1 of 8)

Roadmap goals 1 (uniqueness of the local restriction) and 3 (whole-sequence convergence and a rate in N) from Round32 meet in one mechanism: the AM2 fixed point is a contraction with a large margin at the cap (J_0 G(R) about 5.9e-6 against R=1/64), so a change of the box boundary can reach the creation coefficients on supports meeting the cover only through chains of stars, each costing the ball ratio 64 J G(R) = 37888|tau| (148/390625 at the cap). The advisor selects BA1 as a paired derivation of exactly this coefficient decay for the named construction families F1 and F2: the forward producer uses a weighted anchored norm with the loss paid once per interaction; the reverse producer uses complex-coupling analyticity of the fixed point with an order-versus-distance count and Cauchy's estimate. The (rate, constant) pairs are frozen before production with margins of at least 2. Coefficient decay is not decay of the reduced density (the normalization couples all supports); that step is BB1.
