## Where the panel converged

1. **Boundary decay is feasible, but the rate is not the brief's factor.** The historical lens, the modern lens and the skeptic all correct the brief: J·G(R) < 1/64 is an absolute smallness statement. The per-step rate is the ball ratio J·G(R)/R = 64·J·G(R) = 37888|tau|, which is 148/390625 (about 3.79e-4) at the cap (modern and skeptic agree exactly). The global Lipschitz constant J·G'(R) is not a decay factor (skeptic control `global_lipschitz_not_decay`).
2. **Two routes to coefficient decay give the same constant.** A weighted anchored norm (weight w per connected star, or per unit of coarse l-infinity diameter; loss paid once per interaction) keeps the contraction for w up to 390625/148 (skeptic, modern). Complex-coupling analyticity: AM2's estimates use only norms, so the fixed point is analytic on a disc of radius 390625/148 times the cap; a coefficient on a support meeting R differs between two boxes only at orders reaching the boundary, so a Cauchy estimate gives the same rate with constant 1/32 (modern, skeptic). The historical lens's minimum-order-versus-distance lemma is the order-counting half of the second route; the three inputs differ on the metric (l1 with star diameter 2 versus coarse l-infinity with diameter 1), so the contract must name the metric (skeptic veto) and the producers must verify it.
3. **Coefficient decay is not state decay.** The R-marginal depends on the normalized outside vector through straddling supports and the normalization (the AV1 trap). This needs a new lemma: an exact hard-core polymer representation with a Kotecky-Preiss condition (modern: a=1/10, sum <= 1/32, candidate q=56832|tau|, K <= 2) or an iterated AV1 split (skeptic). This is the round's main risk and gets its own paired loop.
4. **Dynamics is mechanical.** A Duhamel sum over the 28N(5N+1) extra F2 faces at l1 distance at least N-1 with the AQ1 Nachtergaele-Sims constants gives ||tau^{F2}_theta(A) - tau^{F1}_theta(A)|| <= 3969 tau^2 theta^2 e^{127008|tau||theta|}(5N+1)/N^3 ||A|| (modern), which is 2.566e-11 (5N+1)/N^3 at |theta|=8; the skeptic's independent value is 2.549e-11 (5N+1)/N^3. The within-family Cauchy rate is only O(1/N) with the polynomial F(r)=(1+r)^-4 (modern dissent 5; skeptic agrees); an exponential F is a new labelled instance. Honest meaning: algebraic Heisenberg dynamics on compact windows, not equal GNS dynamics unless the state also converges (skeptic).
5. **Consequences restate, never strengthen.** If a limit exists, the AV2 node and AW2 sign certificates become statements about that limit with unchanged radii, in new files; Round32 gates stay untouched. AX2 transfers only after route B is re-instantiated (J'=29|tau|).
6. **Applications.** Center symmetry: the link-flip lemma holds exactly when a central element acts as -1 on the loop representation (SU(2k), U(1), U(N), Z2, Sp, SO(2k)); it fails for SU(N) with N odd, and for SU(3) the exact moment E[(Re Tr U/3)^3]=1/108 gives a first-order centered shift (named obstruction). First-order mean coefficients under the declared convention: 1/144 (SU(2)), 1/96 (U(1)), 1/1152 (SU(3)), 1/2880 (SU(4)), 1/48 (Z2), to be derived by the producers; the SU(2) dictionary tau=96/g^4 does not transfer. New observables: 1x2 Wilson loop at order tau^2 (7 tau^2/124416 on Z^3 and 7 lambda^2/216 on the two-plaquette graph, previews), electric Casimir (unbounded; energy-weighted norm needed), 2+1 dimensions (all counts redone). The site calculators only display recorded results (Jung dissent, adopted); the Einstein-QED rounds share no equation (recorded as no transfer).
