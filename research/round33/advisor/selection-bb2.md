# Selection of BB2 (sub-round 2, investigation 4 of 8)

With the locality of the reduced densities (BB1, used through its frozen targets as explicit hypotheses) and the admitted BA1 and BA2 constants, the finite-box reduced densities of each named construction form Cauchy sequences in N without compactness. The advisor selects BB2 as a paired derivation of whole-sequence convergence, the common limit of F1 and F2, its identification with every AQ1 and F2 subsequential limit (so it inherits their stationarity, GNS continuity and the gap alpha/16), coarse translation invariance through the general-volume comparison, and the convergence of correlation functions on the compact window |theta| at most 8 with three separately checked constants: forward by nested telescoping, reverse through unions. BB2 is frozen with BB1 and gated after it; its conclusions become unconditional only if the BB1 gate admits constants at most the frozen targets. The object is the limit of the named constructions, never uniqueness of every ground state and never uniform in the lattice spacing a.
