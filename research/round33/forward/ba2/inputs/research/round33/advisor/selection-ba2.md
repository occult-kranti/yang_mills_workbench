# Selection of BA2 (sub-round 1, investigation 2 of 8)

Round32's AY2 obligations O5 (boundary independence of dynamics on compact windows) and O6 (dynamics of the padded family) named a Lieb-Robinson/Duhamel route with the AQ1 Nachtergaele-Sims constants. The deliberation found it mechanical: the comparison of the F1 and F2 Heisenberg evolutions on the same box is quadratic in tau and of order (5N+1)/N^3, and the within-family Cauchy rate in N is the honest O(1/N) rate of the polynomial decay function. The advisor selects BA2 as a paired derivation (forward: Duhamel over the extra F2 faces; reverse: Duhamel with the inner F2 evolution and the owner-set constants, each family against its own Nachtergaele-Sims limit for the Cauchy estimates, and identification of the limits), independent of BA1, with frozen targets 6x10^-11 (5N+1) N^-3 and 2.5x10^-10/(N-1) for all M above N. Its meaning is algebraic dynamics of the named constructions on a compact window; equality of GNS dynamics or of correlation functions needs a common state (BB2).
