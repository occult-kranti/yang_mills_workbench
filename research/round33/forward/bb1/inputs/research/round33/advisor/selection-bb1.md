# Selection of BB1 (sub-round 2, investigation 3 of 8)

BA1 admits boundary decay of the AM2 creation coefficients of the named constructions at the rate q=1/64, and BA2 admits the algebraic comparison of their dynamics on compact windows. Coefficient decay is not decay of the reduced density: the density on the cover depends on the normalized outside state through straddling supports and the normalization (the AV1 normalization trap). The advisor selects BB1 as a paired derivation of the locality of the reduced densities of the named constructions, ||rho^{box1}_Y - rho^{box2}_Y||_1 at most a constant times q^(N-1) on the cover and on finite regions, in each on-site cutoff space and at fixed N for the untruncated ground vectors: forward by a hard-core polymer representation with a Kotecky-Preiss condition and a new mixed-weight contraction lemma, reverse by the AV1 product-ordering split applied recursively with per-site charging. The rate q=1/64 is frozen; the per-step ball ratio is not a target for densities. Uniqueness of every ground state stays excluded; the Dobrushin/HTW loop is not scheduled because the weighted contraction is the stronger form of the same smallness and cannot reach boundary conditions outside the named constructions (Jung dissent recorded).
