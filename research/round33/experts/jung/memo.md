## 1. The observation map Round33 must freeze before any outcome

Round32's discipline carries forward unchanged: region, observable, state provenance and topology are frozen and hashed in the contract before a single producer file exists (`update-5.md` section 1's AZ1/AZ2 timestamps are the standard to match). What follows is that map, direction by direction, for the four candidates the brief names. None of this is a decision; the advisor selects and the skeptic triages.

### Direction A — boundary-influence decay giving whole-sequence convergence and a common limit (roadmap goals 1 and 3 together)

- **Region.** A fixed finite box/cover `R`, frozen before production, exactly as AY1's own rule ("`R` fixed before production and never enlarged after inspection," `loop2-response.md` section 4). This is the *fixed* region on which convergence is measured; it is not the growing box whose index `N` is the sequence variable.
- **Observable class.** The `R`-marginal (trace-norm restriction to `B(H_R)`) of the finite-box ground vector/state of a named construction family, indexed by the box size `N`. Two named families are in play (the brief's "both construction families"), each tracked as its own sequence in `N`.
- **State provenance.** The whole `N`-indexed sequence of finite-volume ground states of one named family — not a subsequential limit chosen after the fact. This is the one place Round33's provenance vocabulary must differ sharply from Round32's: AY1/AY2 provenance was `AQ1_centered_whole_star_subsequence`, i.e. explicitly a *subsequence*; a convergence contract's provenance names the *whole sequence*, e.g. `finite_volume_ground_state_whole_sequence(family=<F_i>)`, and the contract states in `preregistration.claim_exclusions` that no subsequence selection occurs in the convergence argument itself.
- **Topology.** Trace norm on `B(H_R)`, inherited from AY1/AY2's own choice — `||rho_R^{(N)} - rho_R^{(N+1)}||_1` (or, more generally, `rho_R^{(N)}` against `rho_R^{(M)}`, `M>N`) is the quantity a Cauchy bound is built on.
- **Target form.** An explicit, summable bound on `||rho_R^{(N)} - rho_R^{(N+1)}||_1` as a function of `N` — the brief's own sketch is a chain-of-overlapping-supports argument costing a factor of order `J G(R) < 1/64` per link, so the natural target form is `||rho_R^{(N)} - rho_R^{(N+1)}||_1 <= C * (J G(R))^{d(N)}` for an explicit, non-decreasing chain length `d(N)`, with the further requirement that `sum_N (J G(R))^{d(N)}` is finite (so the sequence is genuinely Cauchy, not merely bounded term-by-term). A second, separate target form compares the two families' limits once each is shown to exist: a bound on `||rho_R^{F1,infinity} - rho_R^{F2,infinity}||_1`, built from the AY1 two-sided distance tier at finite `N` if that tier is itself uniform in `N` (a fact to be checked, not assumed).
- **Outcome vocabulary.** `accepted_within_scope` with sub-label `convergence_of_named_constructions` (both the per-family Cauchy bound and, if attempted, the common-limit bound close); a weaker `limited` outcome with sub-label `boundary_decay_rate_only` (a decay factor per chain step is proven, e.g. below one, but the chain length `d(N)` does not visibly close the tail sum, or grows too fast, so convergence itself is not established); `insufficient` (no decay of boundary influence beyond the existing `N`-uniform, non-decaying AY1-type bound).

**Note on goal 1 (uniqueness of the local restriction).** The brief pairs this direction with roadmap goal 1 because a closed whole-sequence argument, if it succeeds, gives something *stronger* on `R` than the Dobrushin/HTW-type test the roadmap's own `proposed_first_loop_test` names for goal 1: a single limit per family, hence trivial agreement of any two subsequential limits of that family on `R`. This is not a claim that Direction A subsumes goal 1's Dobrushin route in general (a failed Cauchy chain does not resolve goal 1, and the Dobrushin route remains available as an independent loop), only that a successful Direction A loop would answer the *question* goal 1 asks, by a different route, as a byproduct. The contract for Direction A should say this explicitly rather than silently retitling itself a "uniqueness" loop.

### Direction B — dynamics: Lieb-Robinson/Duhamel comparison on compact time windows (roadmap goal 2)

- **Region.** The same fixed `R`, plus the boundary shell at distance `N-1` the roadmap already quantifies (`28N(5N+1)` extra faces).
- **Observable class.** A single Heisenberg-evolved local observable `A` in `B(H_R)`, `||A||<=1`, compared between the two named families' dynamics on `[0,T]`, `T` frozen in the contract. This is an *operator-level* comparison, not a state-level one — the AQ1 Nachtergaele-Sims constants (`F(r)=(1+r)^-4`, `C<=224`, `||Phi||_F<=2268|tau|`, `||Phi'||_F<=1323|tau|`) bound the difference for every state at once via the interaction norm, which is exactly why this direction's topology differs from Direction A's.
- **State provenance.** None required for the bound itself (an operator-norm Lieb-Robinson/Duhamel estimate does not reference a particular state); the contract should say so explicitly (`state_provenance: "n/a (operator-norm dynamics comparison, holds for every state")`) rather than leaving the field to be inferred, closing exactly the kind of pre-registration gap `update-5.md` section 1 found in AZ2's Hamiltonian.
- **Topology.** Operator norm on `B(H)` for the difference of the two Heisenberg-evolved observables — a second, distinct topology from Direction A's trace norm; a Round33 contract or gate must never let the two be conflated under one unqualified word ("closeness", "distance") without naming which.
- **Target form.** `||alpha_t^{F1}(A) - alpha_t^{F2}(A)|| <= C(T) * (boundary term in N)`, valid only for `|t|<=T`, `T` a fixed rational named in the contract before production — never a claim for all `t`.
- **Outcome vocabulary.** `accepted_within_scope` sub-label `dynamics_on_compact_windows` (the bound is proven and decays in `N` for the frozen `T`); `limited` (a bound is proven for the frozen `T` but does not decay in `N`, or only for one small `T` rather than as an explicit function of `T`); `insufficient`.

### Direction C — consequences for the node and sign certificates (contingent)

- **Region/observable.** Unchanged from AV2/AX2 (the window-kernel node datum) and AW2 (the Wilson-mean sign); this direction adds no new observable of its own.
- **State provenance.** The limit constructed by Direction A — which means this direction's contract cannot be meaningfully frozen, let alone executed, ahead of or independent of Direction A's own gate. A Direction C contract carries a mandatory field naming that dependency, `contingent_on: "<Direction A loop id>"`, and `contingent_condition: "accepted_within_scope with sub_label convergence_of_named_constructions on the same family and region R"`.
- **Topology.** Inherited from AV2/AX2/AW2 unchanged.
- **Target form.** A logical restatement, not a new computation: given the existing certified enclosure/sign (already proven for "every AQ-type subsequential limit" in Round32's language), show it applies a fortiori to "the" limit once that limit is unique within the construction. This is a short derivation from an already-admitted fact plus Direction A's output, and its own contract should say so (a certification-style target, R5's feasibility/format-check note applies).
- **Outcome vocabulary.** If Direction A has not been admitted with `convergence_of_named_constructions` by the time Direction C would be selected, Direction C is either not frozen this sub-round, or, if frozen anyway as a statement of what *would* follow, is recorded `insufficient` and keeps the "every subsequential limit" phrasing unchanged. No new sub-label is needed here beyond the existing `uniform_local_closeness_not_uniqueness` (retained, unweakened) for the case where Direction A did not close.

### Direction D — the applications stage (sub-round 4, "if any")

- **Region/observable/topology.** None inherited by default; each application freezes its own, for its own named model (section 5 below).
- **State provenance.** Re-declared for the named model; never inherited from the SU(2) 3+1D provenance strings.
- **Target form.** Either a certified transfer (the admitted Round32/33 equation holds for the named model under an explicit dictionary map, checked exactly) or an explicit, named obstruction (a specific step of the original proof that fails for the named model, stated as a mathematical fact).
- **Outcome vocabulary.** `accepted_within_scope` sub-label `transfer_to_named_model` (proof/equation genuinely carries over, dictionary map exact and checked); sub-label `obstruction_recorded` (a specific, citable mathematical reason the transfer fails — a retained positive result, never scored as a research failure); `insufficient` (neither a proof nor a clean obstruction is found; the question stays open).

## 2. Mandatory sentence templates

A convergence statement (Direction A) must say **"convergence of the named construction[s]"**, never **"uniqueness of the infinite-volume ground state"**, and must keep **"rate in N"** and **"rate in a"** as two separate, never-merged phrases (Round32's roadmap already states the reason: "A rate in N is not a rate in a," `roadmap.json` goal `whole_sequence`).

**T-A1 (single-family convergence).**
> "The `R`-marginal of the finite-box ground [vector|state] of the named construction [family `F1`] converges in trace norm on `B(H_R)`, region `R` fixed before production, as `N -> infinity`, with a certified rate in `N` of order `<rate(N)>`. This is convergence of the named construction, not a statement that the infinite-volume ground state is unique, that any rate in `a` is thereby obtained, that the construction is translation invariant, or that dynamics beyond the compact window `[0,T]` (if any is claimed) is boundary independent."

**T-A2 (common limit, append to T-A1 when both families converge).**
> "The named construction families `F1` and `F2` converge to a common limit on `R`, certified to `<distance/order>`. This is not a statement of uniqueness of any construction not named here, nor of the infinite-volume ground state of the underlying model; it is agreement, on the fixed region `R`, of two specific, named sequences."

**T-B (dynamics, compact window).**
> "On the compact time window `|t| <= <T>`, `T` fixed before production, the Heisenberg-evolved observable `A` in `B(H_R)`, `||A||<=1`, differs between the named dynamics of [family `F1`] and [family `F2`] by at most `<bound(N,T)>` in operator norm. This is a statement about dynamics on the fixed window and the fixed region `R`; it is not a statement that the infinite-volume dynamics beyond `R` is boundary independent for unbounded time, that the bound holds outside `[-T,T]`, or that any state converges."

**T-C (consequence, contingent).**
> "Given that [Direction A loop id] is accepted within scope with sub-label `convergence_of_named_constructions` for the family and region used here, the enclosure/sign already certified in [AV2/AX2/AW2] for every AQ-type subsequential limit of that family applies to its unique limit on `R`. This restates an already-admitted enclosure under a newly-established uniqueness-within-the-construction; it certifies no new numerical value."

**T-D (application/transfer).**
> "The [named equation/certificate] is transferred to the named model (`model_id`: gauge group `<G>`, dimension `<d>`, observable `<O>`, coupling `<lambda>`) via the explicit dictionary `<map>`; [the transfer is exact and certified to `<order/enclosure>` | the transfer fails at `<step>` because `<explicit obstruction, named as a mathematical fact>`, recorded as `obstruction_recorded`, not as a research failure]. This is a statement about the named model only; it is not a statement about SU(2) in 3+1 dimensions beyond what Round32/33 already admitted, and, where an obstruction is recorded, it is not a claim that the obstruction is permanent or that no other dictionary could succeed."

## 3. Forbidden and required phrasings for Round33

Carried forward unchanged from Round32 (`update-5.md`, `AZ1`'s `forbidden_phrasings`): "the continuum limit exists"; "any fraction of the continuum problem" / "fraction of the problem"; "unique" without an explicit "not" in the same clause.

**Newly forbidden, always, this round:**
- "the infinite-volume ground state" — the task's own instruction names this explicitly; it is never asserted this round in any form, because even a fully successful Direction A loop proves convergence of a *named construction*, not an independent characterization (e.g. GNS uniqueness in a C*-algebraic sense) of an infinite-volume ground state.
- "uniqueness of the infinite-volume ground state" — forbidden absolutely; `uniqueness_of_ground_state_claimed: false` is exported by every gate this round that could be misread as touching it (section 4).
- "boundary independent" (or "boundary independence") **unqualified** — every occurrence must carry both a window (`on the compact window [0,T]`, or, for Direction A's static case, `on the fixed region R`) and, for dynamics, the observable it is stated of. A bare "the dynamics are boundary independent" is forbidden even inside a `limited` or `insufficient` record.
- "rate" used without the qualifier "in `N`" or "in `a`" attached in the same clause — this is a phrase-scanner rule (section 3 below reuses the whole-sentence, negation-aware discipline, extended to require the qualifier rather than a negation).

**Newly, conditionally allowed:**
- "the thermodynamic limit" — allowed **only if** (a) a contract/gate has actually admitted whole-sequence convergence (`whole_sequence_claimed: true` with a non-empty `whole_sequence_scope`, sub-label `convergence_of_named_constructions`) for the specific construction referenced, **and** (b) every occurrence reads "**the limit of the named construction [F_i]**" or "**the thermodynamic limit of the named construction [F_i]**" — never a bare "the thermodynamic limit" with no construction named. This is the brief's own instruction ("only as 'the limit of the named construction'"), made precise: the definite article attaches to "limit," never floats free of "of the named construction."
- "a common limit" (indefinite article) — allowed once both families' individual convergence is admitted and a distance bound between their limits is certified, however small and nonzero. "**the** common limit" (definite article) is allowed only when that distance is certified to be exactly zero (an exact equality, not merely a small enclosure containing zero — the `reference_unresolved` lesson applies here too: an enclosure of the distance that merely *contains* zero is not the same as a certified zero).
- "boundary independence of the dynamics of `A` on the compact window `[0,T]`" — the only allowed *positive* phrasing of Direction B's result; always carries the window and the named observable in the same clause.

## 4. Rules the freezer does not yet enforce, and how to enforce them

`freeze_contract.py`'s own docstring names what it checks: R1 (placeholder spans), R2 (template field required when cited), R3 (finite-model term-by-term Hamiltonian), R4 (no byte-identical copied vocabulary across model families), R5 (statement-loop target note), R8 (`selected_after_note` for a not-yet-existing predecessor gate), plus the controls-mirror invariant. Missing: **R6, R7, R9, R10** — four of the ten rules `update-5.md` section 3 states, none of them contract-freeze-time checks in the way R1-R5/R8 are, which is exactly why the freezer does not (and structurally cannot, for R7 and R9) catch them.

**R6 — forbidden/required-phrase scans are whole-sentence and negation-aware, with the template excluded as one quoted span.** This is not a fact about a *contract* (nothing has been written yet at freeze time); it is a fact about how a *producer's or skeptic's own scanner* must behave once report text exists. Proposal: a single shared module, `research/round33/tools/phrase_scan.py`, used by every producer and skeptic script, implementing exactly the AZ1 skeptic's and producer's discipline (`update-5.md` section 1, quoting `skeptic/az1-contract-review.md` reading 3): exclude the contract's `mandatory_sentence_template` as one normalized literal span; exclude fenced/code blocks; split the remaining prose into sentences; flag a forbidden phrase only when it appears without an explicit negation frame ("not a statement that", "does not assert that", "is not claimed that", or a recorded synonym) in the *same* sentence. Enforcement point: not the freezer, but `record_gate.py` at gate-recording time — it should refuse to write a gate with `--verdict accepted_within_scope` unless `skeptic/<loop>.json` carries a `forbidden_phrase_scan` object with `method: "whole_sentence_negation_aware_template_excluded"` and `passed: true`. This is a gate-time check, in the gate recorder, exactly the second option the task poses.

**R7 — once a contract carries `mandatory_sentence_template`, the gate's `accepted`/`decision` text quotes it as one uninterrupted span.** `record_gate.py` currently never reads the contract at all (it reads only `skeptic/<loop>.json` and the CLI-supplied `--decision`/`--model` text); this is precisely why the AZ1 gap recurred undetected until the closing audit. Proposal, exact check to add to `record_gate.py` immediately after it loads the skeptic review: load `contracts/<loop>.json`; if `preregistration.mandatory_sentence_template` is present and non-empty, normalize both it and the concatenation of `review['supported_statement']` + the CLI `--decision` text (collapse all whitespace runs to a single space, strip), and `fail()` unless the normalized template string is a contiguous substring of the normalized gate text. This is a gate-time check, in the gate recorder, and it is the same normalize-and-match routine `update-5.md` section 1 credits `assistant-4` and `assistant-5` with already building by hand; making it a `record_gate.py`-level refusal turns a closing-audit finding into a pre-publication block.

**R9 — a final-integration field-mirror sweep, run once at round close.** This is explicitly, in the rule's own text, a separate script "run once at round close," not a per-contract freeze check — it needs every contract, skeptic review and gate to exist first. Proposal: `research/round33/tools/field_mirror_sweep.py`, invoked from `reproduce.py --complete`'s final-integration phase (or as a standalone pre-release step, before `release/verify_release.py`), which: (a) walks every `contracts/*.json`, `skeptic/*.json` and `advisor/*-gate.json` under `research/round33`; (b) for a maintained list of shared field names (`region`, `topology`, `states_compared`, `closeness_order`, and, new this round, `whole_sequence_scope`, `dynamics_window_T`, `named_model_id`, `tier_label` — "any future per-topic value field," per the rule's own text), collects every occurrence of that key at any JSON path; (c) for occurrences that share the same loop id or the same declared `model_id`, requires byte-identical values; (d) writes every disagreement (with both values and both file paths) to `advisor/field-mirror-report.json`, **never blocking the release on a disagreement**, exactly as the rule specifies ("records every disagreement without blocking on it"). This is a final-integration sweep, the first option's alternative in the task's own phrasing, and it is the right one here because the object being checked (agreement *across* files) cannot exist as a property of any single contract at its own freeze time.

**R10 — a `tier` name is drawn from a vocabulary closed the same way `sub_labels_allowed` is closed.** `sub_labels_allowed` is closed by being copied, word for word, into every contract's own `preregistration` block from the round's first freeze onward, with any addition recorded as a dated entry in `advisor/plan.json`'s `preregistration_vocabulary_extensions` (the mechanism Round32 actually used twice). Proposal: extend `freeze_contract.py` itself (this one *is* a freeze-time, per-contract check, unlike R6/R7/R9) with a check mirroring the existing R4 cross-glob logic already in the file: collect every string value in the contract under a key literally named `tier` or `tier_label` (via the file's own `strings()` walker, filtered on the last path segment); require `preregistration.tier_labels_allowed` to be present as a list whenever any such value exists; require every collected value to be a member of that list; and require that list itself to be a superset of every `tier_labels_allowed` list already frozen in an earlier contract in `contracts/*.json` (the same `sorted((path.parent).glob('*.json'))` pattern R4 already uses) **unless** the new entry is also named in a dated `preregistration_vocabulary_extensions` entry in `advisor/plan.json` — mirroring, exactly, how `the_AQ_state_phrase` and `target_of_a_certification_loop` were extended in Round32's `plan.json` rather than silently widened inside a single contract.

## 5. Pre-registration vocabulary for Round33

**New sub-labels** (appended to, never replacing, the five inherited: `reference_unresolved`, `sign_certified_finite_graph`, `sign_certified_below_cap`, `static_not_dynamic`, `uniform_local_closeness_not_uniqueness`):

- `transfer_to_named_model` — Direction D, an admitted equation/certificate is proven, with an explicit dictionary, for a different named model.

- `common_limit_claimed: <bool>` paired with `common_limit_families: [<F1>, <F2>]` when true.
- `uniqueness_of_ground_state_claimed: false` — a permanent guard flag, exported `false` by every Direction A/C gate this round; never settable `true`, and its presence at `false` is itself the record that the phrase was considered and excluded, not merely never mentioned.
- `dynamics_window_T: "<exact rational>"` and `boundary_independence_of_dynamics_scope: "<non-empty string iff boundary_independence_of_dynamics_claimed is true>"`, replacing Round32's bare `boundary_independence_of_dynamics_claimed` boolean (AY1/AY2's six-name union) with a scoped version for any Round33 loop that actually attempts the claim rather than only excluding it.

- Direction D only: `transfers_to_named_model: <bool>`, `obstruction_recorded: <bool>` (within one application loop these are mutually exclusive; a sub-round may contain one of each across its two loops), `named_model_id`, `named_model_gauge_group`, `named_model_dimension`, `named_model_observable`, `named_model_coupling`.

**Target-note wording.** Certification loops (restating/replaying an already-admitted quantity on the same or a closely related model — e.g. Direction C's restatement, or an applications loop whose dictionary map is itself already fixed by an earlier contract) keep AZ1/AZ2's own phrasing, praised in `update-5.md` section 1 as satisfying the rule proactively: "**feasibility/format check on `<the admitted quantity/construction named>`, not a blind discovery threshold; the `<statement/enclosure>` itself is the deliverable.**" Blind loops (a genuine discriminating target — does the chain argument close, does the Lieb-Robinson bound decay in `N`, does the dictionary map transfer exactly) use the roadmap's own goal-1 phrasing as the model: "**a blind discriminating threshold: `<comparator>` against `<value>`; a result on either side of `<value>` is a different, retained finding.**"

## 6. The applications stage from the observation-map side

Each application in the brief's candidate list is a transfer to a **named model**, and the observation map requires the same five items frozen for it that a physics contract requires for its own model: `model_id`, `gauge_group`, `dimension`, `observable`, `coupling` — declared explicitly, term by term where a Hamiltonian is involved (R3's own discipline, extended from "finite graph" to "any named target model").

Reading the brief's own candidate list against this frame:

- **Gauge-group transfer (U(1), Z2, SU(N)) of the parity theorem and the link-flip lemma.** `gauge_group` is the whole point of the loop; the brief already names the obstruction shape for SU(3) ("the flip needs -1 central; SU(3) has center Z3"). The observation-map discipline is: freeze which finite group element the flip lemma actually needs (order exactly 2, central) *before* checking which candidate gauge group has one — U(1) has a full circle of central elements of order 2 only trivially (just `-1`, if U(1) is realized as unit complex numbers, which does have `-1`); Z2 trivially has one; SU(N) has center `Z_N`, which has an element of order 2 iff `N` is even. This is exactly the kind of fact that should be recorded as `obstruction_recorded` for odd `N` (SU(3), SU(5), ...) and `transfer_to_named_model` for even `N`, U(1) and Z2 — never as a blanket "the lemma does not generalize."
- **The state lemma and window kernel in 2+1D.** `dimension` is the frozen parameter; `observable` and `coupling` are re-declared for the lower-dimensional lattice (face/plaquette counts, incidence numbers all change with dimension — this is not a relabeling, it is a new model, in the same sense AZ2's finite graph was "a different, finite model," never presented as a resolution of the 3+1D question).
- **Other observables (larger Wilson loops, electric-flux or Casimir observables).** `observable` is the frozen parameter here, `gauge_group`/`dimension`/`coupling` held at their admitted SU(2) 3+1D values; this is the cleanest application shape, closest to an in-model certificate rather than a genuine cross-model transfer, and should be labelled accordingly (`model_id` unchanged from the source result, only `observable` new).
- **The two-plaquette graph and earlier finite-model rounds.** `model_id` names the specific finite graph (as AZ2 already required, R3); no `dimension` or continuum language attaches to a finite graph at all (AZ2's own repaired `parameters.cutoff` field, `update-5.md` section 1, is the precedent for naming a finite-model parameter correctly rather than inheriting the wrong field shape).
- **The site calculators.** This item needs a caution the others do not. `CLAUDE.md`'s own rule states plainly: "Website pages display recorded results from source-bound data; they never compute admissions." An "application" to a calculator page is therefore never itself a research loop with a contract, a gate or a sub-label — it is a presentation-layer task, governed by the existing renderer-test discipline (synthetic fixtures only), that may *display* an already-admitted Round33 result once a Direction D loop for some other named model has actually produced one. The observation map here is: no `model_id`/`observable`/`coupling` triple originates on a calculator page; if a Round33 number reaches `dist/`, it arrives already gated, and the calculator's job is display, not admission.

**Obstruction as an outcome, not a failure.** Every Direction D contract's `claim_exclusions` should include the sentence "a recorded obstruction is not evidence that the underlying SU(2) 3+1D result is wrong, nor that no other dictionary could succeed" — the direct application of Kepler's own precedent (`memo.md`, Round32, section 2: "the harmonies are in the extreme angular velocities, not in the periods... Kepler's habit of writing down where the archetype failed is the method worth keeping"). An `obstruction_recorded` gate is exactly this habit, made machine-checkable.
