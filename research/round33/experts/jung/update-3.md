## 3. The lesson this sub-round: the phrase scanner's vocabulary, not the scanner, is what failed to guard O1

This sub-round's sharpest finding is not mine; it is the skeptic's, and it is worth stating precisely because it corrects a comfort this lens has taken for granted since Round32's R6 proposal. The forward BC1 report recorded AY2's row O1 as `closed_within_scope` as a whole, and the round's negation-aware phrase scanner — which excludes negated and quoted occurrences correctly, exactly as designed — gave **no hit** on this. Not because the sentence was negated, but because neither the round's forbidden-phrase list nor the BC1 contract's own list contains the phrase "uniqueness of the limit" or "uniqueness of every infinite-volume ground state": the scanner is negation-aware, but it can only scan for words that are in its vocabulary. A negation-aware scan of an absent phrase finds nothing to negate. The skeptic's own review states this exactly: "The reviewed wording, not the scanner, guards O1." This is a distinct failure mode from anything R6/R7/R9/R10 were built to catch — those check how a scan is run and how a template is quoted, not whether the vocabulary a scan runs against is complete for a *new* row inherited from an earlier round's obligations table. My own sub-round-2 update already carried a fragment of this lesson (BB2's unqualified rate-in-N restatement, caught by the skeptic's own D12, not by a phrase scan), and this sub-round confirms it as a pattern: a **rate stripped of its range**, and a **row-status word absent from any forbidden list**, are both instances of the same failure — the true-sounding claim that a vocabulary-based guard cannot see, because it was never told to look. The skeptic's own advice (add "uniqueness of every infinite-volume ground state" and "uniqueness of the limit" to later contracts' `forbidden_phrasings`; freeze a carried-over row's reviewed name with the contract) is the correct machine-checkable response, and it belongs in this lens's own R10 proposal, extended: a contract that inherits an obligations-table row from an earlier round must also inherit, or explicitly restate, that row's own forbidden-phrase entries.
