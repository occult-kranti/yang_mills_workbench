## 3. The lesson this sub-round: restatement versus re-derivation

BC1 and BC2 are the round's clearest test of a distinction this lens has held since loop 1 but never had to apply this sharply: a certificate that already holds "for every AQ1-type subsequential limit" needs no new arithmetic to hold for **the** limit once that limit is shown (by a different loop, BB2) to be the whole set — it needs only a continuity argument, and the gate's own `node_certificate_restated_for_limit` and `sign_certificate_restated_for_limit` fields say exactly this: true only with the datum and radius **equal** to the unchanged Round32 gate rationals. Confusing "restated with unchanged constants" for "re-derived with the same constants by coincidence" would be a real error, and it is the error the finite-box sign corollary makes visible: `N_sign=4` is not a restatement of anything — it is the one place BC1 actually computes a new exact rational, from an old certificate (AW2) and a newly available bound (BB2's `C'`). The applications stage inverts the discipline: BD1/BD2 cannot restate anything, because no group other than SU(2) and no model other than the named ones has a prior certificate to restate (`am2_not_reinstantiated`, `su2_constants_not_transferred`) — every BD1/BD2 number must be a first derivation in its own convention, checked by its own two independent routes (BD1: paired) or a single route plus skeptic replay (BD2), with no coefficient borrowed by analogy from the SU(2) chain.
