## 3. The lesson this sub-round: single-route loops and the silent-constant risk

BC2's structure is the clearest instance yet of a risk this lens has named in the abstract since loop 1 ("the two mirrored couplings are used as a control, never as a magnitude estimate," and, in its own §9, "silent route-A constants... the main risk") and has now watched actually tested, not merely asserted. A single-producer, single-route loop with a skeptic replay is only as strong as the replay's independence from the producer's own helper code; here the skeptic's replay used its own code for every route-B constant and additionally ran 22 direct substitutions of route-A values into the producer's own checker, each aborting at a named guard rather than silently producing a route-A number under a route-B label.
