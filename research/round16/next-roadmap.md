# Next roadmap after the shared-face subproblem

The original explicit volume-uniform physical stability threshold remains open. The shared-face calculation is a finite graph dependency, not a replacement target.

## 1. The next local operator obligation

Fix a family of open cubic spatial graphs with vertex extent n>=2, Gauss law at every vertex, no external charges, and the untruncated physical Hamiltonian H_N=α_N[sum_e C_e−sum_p r_p x_p]. To infer a common physical gap from a common dimensionless lower bound, require α_N>=α_min>0 in a common energy unit. Do not identify the static Euclidean coefficient κ_p with r_p without deriving a state and time-generator relation.

Forward substeps: (a) choose the exact local decomposition; (b) specify physical domains and boundary terms; (c) establish the local relative form/norm estimates; (d) enumerate overlap constants; (e) propagate source-theorem constants into one explicit smallness threshold that is independent of the number of plaquettes. Backward substeps: start at Δ(K_N)>=d>0; expose every source-specific lemma needed by the proposed bound; reject any use of the extensive global sum_p|r_p| as a uniform per-plaquette threshold. The first unevaluated constant remains an explicit obstruction.

Two proposed future loops: reproduce the precise applicable local proof or isolate its first missing estimate, then calculate that estimate with a separately checked combinatorial/operator argument. Passing the current finite Haar calculations does not execute these future loops.

## 2. Use the new Haar data in a physical finite trial space

The next finite spectral experiment can use Ω and the eleven fundamental face characters, but it must DERIVE their Gram matrix and every electric/magnetic matrix entry. The new parity classification helps eliminate distinct low-order moments; repeated-index moments still require explicit evaluation. Check that the trial vectors lie in the physical domain and remove any linear dependence.

A ground-energy trial value is an upper bound on E0. Pair it with a separately valid full-operator lower bound on E1; never read a lower physical gap from the finite trial matrix's own gap. Retain the earlier extensive-bound bottleneck. Acceptance requires exact matrix reconstruction and an independent eigenvalue/inertia or variational check, with units and graph recorded. A larger finite trial space is not itself the desired local uniform theorem.

## 3. Generalize the shared-link integration only where it is discriminating

The current complex reduces to three disks sharing one boundary. A next geometry should contain an intertwiner contraction not reducible to that same three-character scalar integral. Start from explicit edge/face incidence, SU(2) fusion multiplicities and invariant projector normalization. Derive the new contraction before choosing a nonzero-coupling observable. The present positive fusion coefficients do not prove arbitrary spin-foam weights are positive.

Retain one-cube and omitted-shared-face references. Center-even support is necessary, not sufficient to know the amplitude. A coefficient deformation must carry an explicit changed action and removal/comparison estimate. A shared variable cannot be introduced solely to make an unproved implication appear connected.

## Continuum requirements

Nontrivial four-dimensional pure Yang–Mills construction, reconstruction axioms, the physical vacuum and time generator, matched units, controlled volume/spacing limits and a positive limiting spectral threshold remain unproved in this project. The bidirectional planner may expose these requirements; it cannot admit them as starting facts merely because the finite routes close.
