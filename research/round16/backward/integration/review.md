# Independent Round16 proof-adapter review

The final adapter and evidence replay are accepted within their declared finite scope. Sixty-seven named independent gates pass under ordinary and optimized Python, with byte-identical review reports. There are twelve required routes: three proved dependency routes and nine blocked routes. The unchanged proof-search core is bound to its previously reviewed hash; this review does not repeat its earlier broad algorithm audit.

## What the routes establish

The finite shared-action effect follows through eight reviewed rules. The premises include the actual signed graph and Haar convention, the declared all-eleven observable and fixed action coefficients, the independently reconstructed shared-edge projector, the exact Taylor coefficients, the full numerator and normalization remainder, and the positive normalized comparison. Each of the five required numerical or graph gates is independently withdrawn and the corresponding route becomes underivable. The separate Haar-reduction route and physical scaling counterexample also pass. Withdrawing the scale gate blocks the latter.

The physical scaling route establishes an exact relation for positive scalar multiples of a common-domain operator and the free-box counterexample. It does not supply an interacting volume-uniform bound. The open uniform route still requires an applicable reviewed local-gap theorem or estimate with explicit constants, a common volume-family contract and verified smallness. A physical gap then additionally requires the common energy scale and the proved dimensionless bound. The continuum route retains a matched generator, continuum axioms, a nontrivial limit and controlled transfer of spectral bounds. These are absent premises; finite arithmetic does not admit them.

The word "proved" in this adapter refers to a replayed conventional dependency certificate for the stated model. It does not mean a proof-assistant kernel has verified every analytic premise, or that the Millennium problem has been solved. Explicit local numbers without an applicable theorem would not justify the uniform rule; the final statement preserves this obligation.

## Independence and admission

The review reconstructs forward closure independently of the search engine. For each positive result it separately replays the ordered certificate and the first meeting candidate, checking every step's exact premises, conclusion and cost against the reviewed library. The saved trace must contain actual forward and backward search events. The finite route has eight steps. This prevents a forward-only certificate from being described as evidence of an actual two-front meeting.

Fresh library admission copies the complete reviewed byte inventory into an operating-system temporary directory and invokes independent scientific replay in a new interpreter. The review reexecutes admission for the finite effect, the scale counterexample and the blocked four-dimensional target. It compares the newly constructed libraries with the saved results. Successful scientific replay supplies only six named gates; no caller-provided passed object can supply initial facts.

The five semantic declarations—hypotheses, gates, open targets, statements and rules—are checked before and after admission and before a library is returned. Runtime injection tests add `uniform_energy_scale` or `matched_generator` as hypotheses, add `uniform_gap` as a gate, remove an open declaration, replace a statement, and introduce a rule to the four-dimensional target. All are rejected. A hidden assumption added to a conclusion is rejected by the unchanged core's assumption validation.

## Concrete failure controls

The review rejects an empty or incomplete frozen source map, changed scientific bytes, a shortened or empty disk inventory, an omitted source file, a symlink replacement, a warm source cache after disk modification, unsupported or duplicate premise withdrawal, and malformed ordered proof metadata. Forged passed certificates with a missing prerequisite step or changed conclusion fail independent replay.

A separate isolated-copy control runs the arithmetic replay executable directly after changing the saved primary interval to [1,2]. It fails and writes no admission record. This checks the scientific verifier beyond the adapter's outer hash rejection. All original project files remain unchanged.

The full final `proof_routes.py` and `replay_evidence.py` were read, including source snapshots, semantic guards, fresh-process replay, rule construction, assumption propagation, fixed route inventory and result binding. The inventory and actual saved results were checked against their bytes. No unresolved scoped defect was found in this review. The earlier genuine mutable-character-cache failure remains preserved in Loop1; no artificial failure is reported as a discovered bug.
