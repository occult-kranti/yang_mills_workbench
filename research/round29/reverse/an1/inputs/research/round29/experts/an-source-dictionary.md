# AN source dictionary — prospective, not an admission

External result: Henheik–Teufel–Wessel, *Local stability of ground states in locally gapped and weakly interacting quantum spin systems*, LMP 112, 9 (2022), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11005-021-01494-y), Theorem 7 (attributed there to Yarotsky 2005, Theorem 2), Definitions 1/2/6 and Lemma 8. Read with its boundary-convention footnote.

For two normal states satisfying the common bulk ground-state condition, the theorem gives

\[
|\operatorname{Tr}[(\rho_1-\rho_2)A]|
\le e^{c_1|Y|-c_2\operatorname{dist}(Y,\mathbb Z^3\setminus\Lambda_*^\circ)}\|A\|,
\qquad
\Lambda_*^\circ=\{x:\operatorname{dist}(x,\mathbb Z^3\setminus\Lambda_*)>2R\}.
\]

Its onsite spaces may be infinite dimensional. Self-adjoint onsite terms have a unique zero-energy vacuum and uniform gap; bounded interactions have radius R and sufficiently small anchor norm. Constants are existential. “Bounded commutator” includes preservation of the Hamiltonian domain.

Do not identify the two finite restriction prescriptions at their boundaries. The I1 whole-star rule and the HTW anchor-distance rule can drop different terms. Establish equality of their commutators on a common interior, with the full two-radius erosion. Then take the norm-one supremum on the full bounded local algebra; restriction to the gauge-invariant subalgebra is immediate. Local normality alone and a common gap alone do not establish the comparison.

Proposed controls: a fixed near-boundary region; a changed bulk plaquette interaction; a dropped incoming star; wrong R=0; and deletion of the new smallness premise. Finite matching is a geometry/domain control, not the proof of the infinite-volume estimate. This is a source-led application of an existing theorem, not an HNM theorem of general boundary independence.
