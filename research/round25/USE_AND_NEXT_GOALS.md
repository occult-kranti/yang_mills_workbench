## Skeptical assessment of distance to the full problem

The workbench has useful finite-graph spectral certificates, fixed-spacing conditional statements, inverse-identification counterexamples and an exact observable endpoint in a summable family. These are substantive tools and obstructions. Counting completed loops is not a fraction of Yang–Mills solved.

The main missing chain is still: obtain quantitative homogeneous control; extend it uniformly in volume; establish the required renormalized continuum family as a approaches zero; prove nontrivial limiting gauge-invariant correlations with reconstruction axioms; and retain a strictly positive physical gap in that same limit. The current canonical q-to-one trajectory weakens local coefficients and returns the reference state. It is not that renormalization trajectory. AA's resonant endpoint demonstrates a nonuniform long-time phenomenon, not the needed continuum mass. Historical unity, matching numerical frequencies or adding a fitted constant cannot close those missing implications.
