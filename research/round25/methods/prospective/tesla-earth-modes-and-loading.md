# Earth modes, hidden media and loading

Selected primary reading updated 2026-09-21. Extend this map when new evidence is needed; do not call it complete state-of-the-art coverage of all Tesla's fields.

## Keep the historical models distinct

[Tesla US787412A](https://patents.google.com/patent/US787412A/en), 1905, describes an Earth-conductor stationary-wave proposal, a diameter/quarter-wave condition and a lowest rate near six per second. Treat patent claims as proposals. The modern reconstruction c/(8R) follows that condition; Tesla did not thereby derive the later Earth-ionosphere shell equation. The [museum archive catalogue](https://tesla-museum.org/en/legacy/archive/identity-documents/) dates his death to 1943; [Schumann's paper](https://doi.org/10.1515/zna-1952-0202) is from 1952. Original Schumann full text was inaccessible in this reading; do not claim to have inspected its derivation.

Use [Simões/Pfaff/Freudenreich, NASA manuscript](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20120000051/downloads/20120000051.pdf), inspected pp.2–4, for the thin lossless-shell formula omega_l=c sqrt(l(l+1))/R and observed lower, lossy peaks. The listed mean frequencies and one satellite spectrogram are different datasets. Neither forms exact integer harmonics. A fitted effective speed from one peak must face held-out modes.

[Vivekananda's letter to Sturdy, 13 February 1896](https://www.ramakrishnavivekananda.info/vivekananda/volume_5/epistles_first_series/057_blessed_and_beloved.htm), reports Tesla's interest in Vedantic prana, akasha and kalpas and an anticipated demonstration. It supplies no completed mathematical derivation. Preserve the letter's author and cultural context. Do not promote this to a Tesla energy theorem, a 3–6–9 law or a demonstrated hidden medium.

## Separate eigenvalues, driven peaks and observables

For the illustrative damped mode u''+2 gamma u'+omega_0^2 u=d(t), use H(omega)=1/(omega_0^2-omega^2-2i gamma omega). With white forcing and constant displacement readout, the power peak is sqrt(omega_0^2-2 gamma^2), if real. Colored forcing, a different readout or mode interference changes that peak. Include source work in E'=d u'-2 gamma(u')^2. Label this as a simplified response model, not a full Earth simulator.

The modern primary paper [Koloskov et al., 18 Sep 2026](https://angeo.copernicus.org/articles/44/949/2026/), inspected introduction and calibration/conclusions, explicitly distinguishes forced spectral maxima from cavity eigenvalues and uses multiple mode/field observations. [Bozóki et al., 2025](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025JD043989), inspected conclusions, compares analytical and FDTD responses with continuing-current drivers. Use these to motivate separate source, boundary, loss and readout variables. Require an identifiable parameter map and independent predictions; a fit is not a physical law.

## A resonant component can close only after a loading proof

Compute every degenerate transition, then test external channels. Prove a smaller observable-reachable component reducing for the averaged operator before exponentiating it as the whole response. In strip/free tensor models, an external face may necessarily excite distinct charged factors above the initial energy. Establish spectral support and all fusion branches; a mean-energy argument is insufficient. Distinguish this from instantaneous invariance, which may fail.

The workbench Round25 AA component has sixteen states and several spectral weights, not just the original resonant pair. Its source-specific participating-frequency bound evaluates a previously unknown averaging constant. It remains a same-author canonical-model result, not independent validation, a measured physical frequency or a continuum gap. Changing the observable requires a new reachability proof.

Keep complete state, spatial, averaging, representation and arithmetic errors beside the physical clock and readout resources. A tiny error at an enormous time is not a feasible device. Use modern local-Krylov truncation research, such as [2503.11888v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.11888v2), to propose bases, then certify the actual omitted map for the chosen model.
