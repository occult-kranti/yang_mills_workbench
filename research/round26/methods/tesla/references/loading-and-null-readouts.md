# Delayed loading and null readouts

Apply these modern workbench lessons to mechanisms with retained and omitted channels. They are not historical Tesla claims. The source example is model-agent-reviewed Yang–Mills Round26 AB–AD (2026-09-22); scientific priority is unverified.

## Follow the loading after the initial instant

Let P=P_old+N reduce the unbounded reference energy and let B=Q L P be the complete retained-to-omitted map. The identity B P_old=0 does not make the retained dynamics invariant. Write y=N exp[-t(A-mu)]x and its exact equation. If y(0)=0 but C=N A P_old is nonzero, newly retained channels load and subsequently leak. Bound the driven convolution using the full retained evolution, including feedback. Preserve the metric of nonorthonormal coordinates; symmetry cancellation in one block does not erase external channels.

Compare each heat evolution about its own ground energy. Retain the scalar difference mu-epsilon separately from B. Combine the increasing early-time Duhamel error and decreasing late spectral error at a proved joining time, then divide by a lower bound on the actual full output norm. A uniform bound on the exact retained semigroup is not an executed matrix-exponential calculation. For a computed vector add center-enclosure, polynomial/integration, arithmetic and export errors explicitly.

## Check what the output can see

Compute the full selection rules before calling a nonzero-variance observable a resonance detector. Its reached source may be annihilated by the averaged coupling and have a constant phase-removed endpoint. Such a null probe does not identify the slow rate. A replacement probe must have its own physical support, normalization, multiplication norm and complete reached energy channels.

Matching an observable-created vector transfers only the scalar mechanism actually proved. A multiplication operator and a rank swap can differ on other states and in their fourth moments. Charge stationary-state replacement with the real operator norm. An exact finite-parameter disk that excludes a null signal is a mathematical witness; record its physical observation time and preparation requirements before describing an experiment or device.

## Preserve the unresolved mechanism

A boundary-complete regulated inverse can contract one source while retaining a resonant component. Do not erase the component in a device diagram or assume the next source has the same bound. Operator norm, graph-domain regularity, spatial localization and a summable interaction decomposition are separate requirements. A positive upper budget is not measured loading or a lower bound.
