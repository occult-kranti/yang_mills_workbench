# Checked sources and contribution scope

Consulted 21–22 September 2026. These are selected primary-source comparisons, not an exhaustive priority search. New equations below are workbench derivations unless a specific literature comparison establishes more.

- Del Vecchio, Fröhlich, Pizzo and Rossi, [Local iterative block-diagonalization](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2007.07667), v2. Read abstract, introduction, §1.1 assumptions and §1.2 theorem. The displayed model has finite on-site dimension and bounded finite-range interactions. Its uniform small-coupling gap does not automatically supply this workbench's infinite-dimensional inverse or numerical all-stage constants. Weighted support accounting is an established method.
- Tong, Albert, McClean, Preskill and Su, [Provably accurate simulation](https://arxiv.org/html/2110.06942v2), §2 and Appendix D, Theorem 6 and surrounding proof. Distinguishes truncation of a state from truncation of dynamics under a Hamiltonian. Its premises include controlled changes of local quantum numbers and specified initial states. This cycle's small retained subspaces require their own complete leakage estimates. The paper explicitly limits its analysis to fixed lattice spacing.
- Jaffe and Witten, [Quantum Yang–Mills Theory](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/yangmills.pdf), §4 and the opening of §5. The target requires a nontrivial theory on four-dimensional space satisfying reconstruction axioms and a positive physical spectral gap. Neither a finite matrix nor a summable fixed-spacing endpoint supplies those conclusions.

Historical method sources and exact reading limits remain in `research/methods/newton-tesla/README.md` and `research/round25/RESONANCE_AND_HISTORY.md`. Newton's backward analysis and forward synthesis guide identifiability checks. Tesla's phase and loading analysis guides retained/omitted channel checks. Their authority, symbolism and historical metaphysical interests supply no new mathematical premise or fitted physical coefficient.

An unavailable HTML rendering of the block-diagonalization paper was replaced by its readable arXiv PDF. No claim is made to have verified every proof in these papers. This cycle does not claim a new universal physical law or established scientific priority.

- **Nachtergaele–Sims, arXiv:1410.8174v1**, [primary text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1410.8174). Advisor inspected §2 operator-valued strong/weak calculus, §3 assumptions and Theorem 3.1, and the finite-volume comparison around equations (83)–(88). This supplies a relevant framework for bounded intersite interactions with unbounded onsite terms. Time continuity and spatial convergence require separate topology statements. The same literature lead was sent to both AE directions after contracts froze; current solutions were not exchanged.
- **Lieb–Robinson bounds with exponential-in-volume tails, arXiv:2502.02652v2**, [primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.02652v2). Advisor inspected the introductory theorem summaries and Definition 3.4, which uses finite-dimensional qudits. This is a prospective cluster-decomposition lead, not an admitted extension to the workbench's infinite-dimensional SU(2) factors. Full theorem proofs and their possible adaptation were not audited in this round.
