## How to use the computation

Run the [reproduction commands](README.md). AF2 forward exports 18 complete complex-coordinate vectors (vacuum, normalized real face input and normalized imaginary face input) at six times. Reverse exports 12 complete real-coordinate vectors for two inputs at six times. Shared real outputs are independently compared in their actual diagonal Gram, not a Euclidean coordinate norm.

For an additional exact rational time and vacuum, the reverse evaluator exposes:

```bash
python3 -B research/round26/reverse/af2/check.py \
  --sigma 7/2 --output /absolute/new/heat-at-seven-halves
```

Its optional `--input-vector PATH` accepts a JSON list of 293 **real rational strings** and enforces exact normalization, original P21 support and the radius 0.01 condition. The reverse CLI does not accept complex coordinates. Forward executes complex demonstrations but exposes no general complex-input CLI. The underlying polynomial/projector operator estimates cover complex inputs; do not conflate that theorem with the scope of either command interface.

The implementation accepts exact rational input times. Its uniform error proof defines the approximation for every real heat time, without claiming an arbitrary irrational time can be represented exactly by a command. Measured or rounded input/time uncertainty needs an additional budget. Returned heat vectors are not renormalized. Their coordinates, numerical retained-center uncertainty, complete physical leakage and true denominator remain separate.
