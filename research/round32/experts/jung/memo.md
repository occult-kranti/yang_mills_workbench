## 2. Source pass: new items, historical assertion separated from physical claim

Reading depth, provenance, versions and access results are in `sources.json`; the occult/government subset is summarized in `../occult/reading-ledger.md`. **Jung, Synchronicity (Bollingen LI, Pantheon 1955, tr. Hull), ch. 1–2 and appendix.** Historical assertion: a "coincidence in time of two or more causally unrelated events which have the same or a similar meaning" (p. 36) is a principle of nature. Documented record: the astrological marriage experiment ran a pilot of "180 married pairs" (p. 60), then grew to "483 marriages, or 966 horoscopes" (p. 64); the maxima were selected among fifty aspects after tabulation; "Calculations undertaken by a mathematician showed unmistakably that the average frequency of 10% in the first batch and subsequently in all three batches is far from representing a significant figure" (p. 64); the probability of the "maximal numbers" was computed by Fierz as "approximately 1 : 10,000. Later, on checking the calculation, he found an error whose correction raised the probability to 1 : 1500" (p. 80), corrected again in a footnote (p. 81) and again at "the eleventh hour" (p. 85), with Jung's own verdict: "The errors all tend to exaggerate the results in a way favourable to astrology" (p. 85). On Rhine: "the number of hits scored tends to sink after the first attempt, and the results then become negative" (pp. 25–26), and "distance has no effect in principle shows that the thing in question cannot be a phenomenon of force or energy" (p. 24). Physical claim status: none survives; the text itself is the cautionary record of post-hoc maxima, growing batches, thrice-corrected probabilities and experimenter expectation. That record, not the synchronicity thesis, is what this lens carries forward.

**Pauli, The Influence of Archetypal Ideas on the Scientific Theories of Kepler (same volume, tr. Silz).** Historical assertion: Kepler's trinitarian sphere symbol and Fludd's quaternary symbolism are psychological "attitudes" (pp. 205–206). Documented physics content: Fludd's harmonic table (p. 193: "Diapente = Fifth, Proportio sesquialtera 3 : 2") and the sentence "According to Kepler, on the other hand, only that which is capable of quantitative, mathematical proof belongs to objective science, the rest is personal" (pp. 193–194). Pauli's closing is explicitly open: "It would be most satisfactory of all if physis and psyche could be seen as complementary aspects of the same reality. We do not yet know, however, whether or not we are here confronted ... with a true complementary relation" (p. 209). Physical claim status: none; Pauli leaves the psyche/physis relation as a surmise. Nothing here licenses a consciousness or observer variable in a Hamiltonian.

**Kepler, Harmonices Mundi Libri V (Linz 1619; Latin OCR, Smithsonian/Burndy copy).** Located in the raw OCR (long-s normalized): Book V ch. III prop. XIII, "quod quemadmodum proportio mediorum motuum in binis planetis, est sesquialtera eversa proportionis orbium ..." with the back-reference "per VIII. praemissum"; the opening of ch. IV, which records a **null result**: "Non deprehenditur igitur Creator Deus inter has morarum summas collectas in Tempora periodica, voluisse introducere proportiones harmonicas" (no harmonic proportions in the periodic times); the observational anchor "per accuratissimas Tychonis Brahei observationes ... pertinacissimo septendecim annorum studio"; and the Appendix on Fludd (p. 253): "Primum quas ille harmonias docere intendit, eae sunt meri Symbolismi ... poeticos potius esse aut oratorios, quam philosophicos aut mathematicos." The famous prop. VIII sentence itself was not located in either OCR and stays unverified in this pass. Historical assertion: the harmonies are in the extreme angular velocities, not in the periods. Physical claim status: the 3/2 power law survived as an empirical regularity; the harmonic archetype did not. Kepler's habit of writing down where the archetype failed is the method worth keeping.

**Dee, Monas Hieroglyphica (Antwerp 1564; Hamilton-Jones 1947 tr.).** Theorem I: "It is by the straight line and the circle that the first and most simple example and representation of all things may be demonstrated"; Theorem II: "It is, therefore, by virtue of the point and the Monad that all things commence to emerge in principle"; Theorem VI on ternary and quaternary; Theorem VII: "this a man learns by experiment"; Theorem X: separation "by the application of Fire". ## 4. Pre-registration rules proposed for Round32 (observation map, selection, null records)

R1. **Frozen before any outcome is seen** (in the contract, hashed in `freeze.json`): model id (family, selected triple, `tau` sign and magnitude, boundary/state provenance, physical clock); the observable formula including the centering convention (vector centering with `m` computed in the same state); `s*` or the whole grid; the five-term error structure (state/boundary, dynamics/cutoff `L`, truncation `j_max`, quadrature, arithmetic) with the target; the outcome taxonomy; and `claim_exclusions` containing "free reference inside the enclosure implies no interaction claim".

R2. **Outcome taxonomy, all retained:** `accepted_within_scope`, `limited`, `insufficient`, plus two sub-labels: `reference_unresolved` (interval contains the matched free value) and `sign_certified_finite_graph` (non-AQ). A `limited` or `insufficient` record must name the dominating term and its numerical share.

R3. **Insufficient outcomes** are recorded with the failing term and the smallest change of a frozen parameter that would pass, as a planning note; no rerun with changed parameters inside the same loop. A changed parameter (the AT5 precedent `tau: 10^-8 -> 10^-14`) is a new loop with a new contract and is named as a model change, not a refinement.

R4. **No post-hoc `s*`.** If a grid is used, the whole grid is reported (AT6 style); no node of maximal separation is selected afterwards. Pilot runs at reduced size are allowed only if declared and excluded from the counted result (Jung's 180-pair pilot was folded into the 483).

R5. **Discriminating controls** required for any claimed interaction shift:
- centering artifact: vector vs scalar-subtraction identities with a deliberately perturbed `m_hat` (Round31 memo) and the `s=0` endpoint check `|C(0)-v_tau|`;
- boundary/state difference: two boundary families or completions and the local reset bound; a sign or magnitude that depends on the completion is not bulk;
- truncation artifact: monotone `j_max` and `L` sequences with certified tails;
- arithmetic artifact: exact `Fraction` admission with a python-flint Arb or `mpmath.iv` replay and the `-O` byte-identical replay;
- null control: `tau=0` exact replay; sign control: `tau -> -tau`.

R6. **Independence.** The AIR report's shared-judge confound is the same-author problem. A claimed shift needs either paired producers with unread current work or a single producer with a skeptic replay from the contract alone; shared premises are declared, and same-author passes are never called independent.

R7. **Deterministic "blinding".** Random blinding factors are meaningless for exact arithmetic, but the *decision* can be blinded: the comparison to the reference value is computed by `check.py` from frozen inputs, the threshold is in the contract, and the producers commit code before the first full-size evaluation (the muon g-2 lattice practice, "The analyses are then frozen, the blinds are removed, and the analysis scripts are rerun", adapted to a deterministic setting).

R8. **Labelling a finite-graph sign certificate** so it cannot be misread as an AQ-state result: mandatory fields `model_class: "finite_graph_exact"`, explicit graph (vertices, links, plaquettes, completion), `j_max`, `tau`, `statement: sign(Delta_G) and enclosure`, `transfer_status: "not_transferred_to_AQ"`, `claim_exclusions: ["AQ thermodynamic state", "infinite volume", "uniform Hamiltonian", "continuum"]`; the gate sentence "This certificate concerns the finite graph G; it is not an AQ-state result" is required; check ids carry the prefix `FG-`; the website badge shows the model class. Kepler's separation of "Symbolismi" from mathematical proof is the historical precedent; the modern rule is a label the validator can test.

## 6. Modern pass (2024–2026)

(a) **Certified bounds from Euclidean/positivity data.** Kazakov–Zheng (arXiv:2404.16925, SU(2), D=2,3,4) bound the free energy per plaquette with loops up to length 24, within 0.1% of Monte Carlo in 3D/4D; Guo–Li–Yang–Zhu (arXiv:2502.14421, JHEP12(2025)033) give rigorous SU(3) plaquette bounds from Hermitian, reflection and twist-reflection positivity plus loop equations; Cho–Gabai–Lin–Yeh–Zheng (arXiv:2511.08560, JHEP08(2026)147) bound continuous-time Euclidean two-point correlators of quantum-mechanical ground/thermal states via reflection positivity, Heisenberg equations and KMS/ground-state positivity, demonstrated on one-matrix QM. Mutzel–Tilloy remains as recorded in Round31. Relevance: these are Euclidean Wilson-action or finite-QM statements; none is our Hamiltonian patterned family, and the AL1 dictionary `tau=96/g^4` applies only at uniform coefficients. They are candidate *independent* preview methods for a finite-graph correlator (route a), not admission tools.

(b) **Reproducibility and pre-registration.** The Lattice 2024 panel (Bennett et al., arXiv:2502.03593) recommends publishing "the exact code that was used to generate the outputs" and discusses barriers; it contains no pre-registration or blinding guidance. The muon g-2 lattice programme does: Fermilab/HPQCD/MILC (arXiv:2411.09656, PRD 111, 094508) blind window observables with software-level multiplicative factors and state "The blinds are held in place until all individual analyses are complete. The analyses are then frozen, the blinds are removed, and the analysis scripts are rerun"; Keshavarzi et al. (PRD 111, L011901, 2025) blind data-driven HVP. Rules R1–R7 above are the deterministic-arithmetic analogue.
