### 5.1 Observation 1 — derivation sketch of `|omega_tau(X)-omega_0(X)| <= c_1|tau|`

Group by the excited site set `M = ∪F`: `psi = sum_M psi_M`, sectors mutually orthogonal, `psi_∅ = Omega_0`. (E2)

(A1) *Star-connectivity:* `c_I = 0` unless `I` is connected in the star graph (`u~v` iff some `b+S` contains both; 12 neighbours per site). Sketch: the Picard iterates from `c=0` have this property because `L_k(c,…,c)_M` is supported on `M ⊂ N∪X` with every `I_j` meeting the connected star `X`; the property is closed in `||·||_a`; the fixed point is the limit. Numerator for `X ∈ B(H_R)`: `<psi_M, X psi_{M'}> ≠ 0` requires `M\R = M'\R`.
