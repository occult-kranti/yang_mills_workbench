## 3. AW2: controls that discriminate a real first-order effect, and the below-cap gate statement

Signature of a real first-order effect (all in exact rationals): the enclosures at `+tau` and `-tau` are mirror images to second order, i.e. `E_+ + E_-` is enclosed in `[-2K_2 tau^2, 2K_2 tau^2]` and `(E_+ - E_-)/2` in `[tau/144 - K_2 tau^2, tau/144 + K_2 tau^2]`; the lower endpoint at `+tau` is strictly positive and the upper endpoint at `-tau` strictly negative; the margin `(1/144)/(K_2|tau|)` is reported and must be `>= 2`.

- **Centering artifact.** `omega(W)` is uncentered by definition; the trap is importing a centering residue (`m^2`, `-2md-d^2`) or an `m_hat` into its path. Controls: `vector_versus_scalar_centering`, `first_order_mean_charged`; a mutation that inserts any `m_hat` into the `omega(W)` computation must be rejected; the `s=0` datum of any companion correlation must satisfy `C(0)=omega(W^2)-m^2` with `m^2=tau^2/144^2+O(tau^3)` charged, never dropped.
- **Sign-convention error.** The sign of `1/144` rests on I1.5 (`phi_b=-(tau/3)sum_f W_f`, `V_b=phi_b/8`), on the reduced resolvent sign and on the energy `3` of `W Omega_0`. - **Boundary/state difference.** The finite-volume enclosure must be volume-uniform: the formula is evaluated symbolically for two centered boxes `N` and `N+1` and must be byte-identical; a mutation inserting an `N`-dependent term is rejected; `aq_passage_trace_norm_only` executed; the first-order density `rho^(1)_R` computed for the centered whole-star family and for the all-contained-face family must coincide exactly (skeptic goal-4 note), and the gate says the enclosure holds for every AQ-type subsequential limit, not for "the" AQ state (item 4).
