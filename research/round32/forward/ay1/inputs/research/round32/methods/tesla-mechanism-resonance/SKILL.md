# Tesla: mechanism, resonance and imagination

Use [the research reference](references/research.md) for the historical and mathematical foundation. This selected corpus is not all Tesla's knowledge and this workflow does not reproduce his mind.

## Working procedure

1. **Draw the mechanism mentally, then specify it.** Name components, states, couplings, boundaries, source and output. Turn a vivid idea into a diagram, operator or set of equations that another person can inspect. Tesla's retrospective account of visualization motivates preparation, not skipping experiments.
2. **Track phase and energy.** Specify the actual generator and units. Derive eigenfrequencies, detuning, coupling strengths and losses. For degenerate states, compute the matrix element and verify selection rules. Never equate electrical resonance with quantum resonance without a mathematical map.
3. **Test the whole device.** Add loading, environment and omitted modes. Account for bulk return paths. A resonant two-state compression need not evolve autonomously. Bound the remainder before extending short-time behavior to a growing time window.
4. **Audit a patent as a proposal.** Read claims, drawings, input supply and operating assumptions. Separate application, grant, demonstrated performance and historical priority. Group patent families before counting inventions. A patent alone proves no efficiency, free-energy or cosmological assertion.
5. **Use esoteric material to pose questions.** Preserve the source's exact cultural category and evidential status. Translate unity, cycles or hidden media into a proposed symmetry, periodicity or latent variable, then name a falsifier. If no operational translation exists, retain the idea in historical commentary.
6. **Act as skeptic.** Detune or remove the coupling; reverse a phase; include dissipation or extra factors; inspect a null observable. Distinguish an actual lower bound from a positive upper budget. Admit failure and conflicting readings.
7. **Preserve a reproducible record.** State model scope, source passages, exact calculations versus fixtures, controls, uncertainties and next missing premise. Apply project guidance prospectively and preserve historical contracts.

For operational witnesses and measurement resources, read [complete readout mechanisms](references/readout-resources.md).

For Tesla's terrestrial proposals, Schumann modes, Vedantic correspondence or a resonant component's external channels, read [Earth modes and loading](references/earth-modes-and-loading.md). Distinguish ideal eigenfrequencies from driven spectral peaks before introducing latent variables.

For evolving retained spaces and resonance probes, read [delayed loading and null readouts](references/loading-and-null-readouts.md). Charge later loading and actual multiplication norms before claiming a complete output.

## Advisor and skeptic roles

As advisor, propose the simplest explicit working mechanism. As skeptic, challenge loading, dimensional consistency, selectivity, missing channels and claimed amplification. Also let the Newton skill challenge the inverse inference. Honor no-subagent instructions; label serial same-author role passes as self-review, never independent verification.

For Yang–Mills, historical resonance is motivation only. Require actual gauge-invariant states, complete factor support, fixed scales, the original time generator, and rigorous full-system error bounds. Do not substitute an ether, numerology or an adjustable clock for an unresolved premise.

## Attribution rules

Distinguish a contemporary notebook, patent text, retrospective autobiography, another person's recollection and modern attribution. Do not invent quotations, occult memberships, successful demonstrations or missing equations. No authenticated primary derivation is supplied here for popular 3–6–9 cosmology, limitless energy or a completed Tesla theory of gravity. Treat those as unverified claims requiring sources, not an inherited doctrine.
