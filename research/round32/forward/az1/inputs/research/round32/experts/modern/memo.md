## 1. Headline findings of this loop

1. **The AT4 dynamics "floor" at the cap is an artifact of the Poisson kernel, not of the real-time comparison.** The kernel s/(pi(s^2+theta^2)) is the Fourier transform of e^{-s|x|}, whose kink at x=0 forces 1/theta^2 decay and a logarithmically divergent first moment. Since the centered spectral measure lives on x >= 0, any f in L^1 with f-hat in L^1 and f(x)=e^{-sx} for x >= 0 gives C(s)=int f-hat(theta) c(theta) dtheta exactly. A C^2 taper on [-a,0] has f-hat = O(theta^-4), finite first and second moments, and no cutoff L. The floor 1.2651e-6 (reproduced below) disappears; the dynamics cost becomes k*M_1 with M_1 ~ 2.4-3.0 (preview), i.e. ~3e-7 at tau=1e-8.
2. **For the xz Wilson loop in the zero-selected subfamily the first-order (O(tau)) correction to both the state expectations and the real-time correlator vanishes identically**, by two selection rules: Haar single-occurrence orthogonality (a face inserted once leaves some link in a nontrivial representation appearing once) and SU(2) center symmetry (<W^3>_Haar = 0). Hence C(s) = e^{-3s}/4 + O(tau^2), |omega(W^2)-1/4| = O(tau^2), while omega(W) = tau/144 + O(tau^2) is the only O(tau) shift available on this observable family. This changes what goals 1 and 2 can mean (Section 4).
3. **AM2's expansion does give a local O(tau) bound without any square root**, and the normalization/adjoint obstacle has a clean answer: the rank-one spectral projection of the ground state is P = e^{-C}|Omega_0><l| e^{C} with l the left eigenvector of e^{C}He^{-C} normalized by <l,Omega_0>=1, so omega_N(A) = <l, e^{C} A e^{-C} Omega_0> with no division and no e^{-C^dagger}. What remains unproved is an anchored-norm bound for l (an annihilation-type fixed point) or, equivalently, a support-size-weighted Kotecky-Preiss condition for the polymer form.
4. **The nearest external certificate is Gauvin arXiv:2503.15539v3, Theorem 2.3 (2026-09-14):** SU(3), b/a <= 1/648, gap 8a/3, creation-equation radius 1/24, J/delta <= 1/128, thermodynamic ground state (Theorem 2.5). Structurally it is the AM2/AQ programme for SU(3). Its versions v2/v3 are eleven days old and the arXiv category is physics.gen-ph; whether Theorem 2.3 already existed in v1 (2025-03-06) must be diffed by the skeptic before any priority statement about AM2 is written.
5. **No patent found claims quantum simulation of SU(2)/SU(3) lattice gauge theory as such**; the patent record adds no premise. Hardware in 2025-2026 reports real-time observables (string breaking, thermalization, entanglement) on truncated Z2/U(1)/SU(2) models, never a certified spectral gap or Euclidean correlator with an error enclosure.

## 3. Technical assessment of the advisor's observations

### (i) Observation 1: a local O(tau) bound from AM2 without the square root

Setting (delta = alpha/8 units, zero-selected subfamily): H = H_0 + V, h_x = 8 sum C_e on factor x with Haar ground Omega_x, V = sum_b phi_b, phi_b = -(tau/3) sum_{21 omitted faces} W_f (I1 forward report, the displayed phi_b definition at line 75), ||phi_b|| = 7|tau|, J <= 28|tau|. AM2: psi = e^{-C} Omega_0, C = sum_I c-hat_I, ||c||_a <= 1/64, fixed point c = sum_k L_k(c,..,c)/k! with ||L_k||_a <= J 16 8^k (1+5k/4) ||c||_a^k.

*Normalization and adjoint.* Since e^{C} is a (nilpotent, invertible) similarity, D := e^{C} H e^{-C} = H_0 + Delta has Omega_0 as a right eigenvector with eigenvalue E. Let l be the left eigenvector, D^dagger l = E l, <l, Omega_0> = 1. The Riesz projection onto psi is P_psi = e^{-C}|Omega_0><l| e^{C}, hence for any bounded A

  omega_N(A) = Tr(P_psi A) = <l, e^{C} A e^{-C} Omega_0>.                              (M1)

No <psi,psi> and no e^{-C^dagger} appear. Creations disjoint from R and from every other creation in a term commute through and cancel, so e^{C}Ae^{-C}Omega_0 is a finite sum over creation collections connected to R; only sectors M meeting R contribute, and only the components l_M with M near R are needed. The vector l solves an annihilation-type fixed point l_M = -((D^dagger - E)_{QQ})^{-1} (Delta^dagger)_{M0} Omega_0 at first order, with (H_I c_I)-hat^dagger Omega_0 = 0, so l = Omega_0 - c^{(1)} + O(J^2) with c^{(1)}_M = H_M^{-1} P_M V Omega_0 exactly the k=0 term of AM2's fixed point.

*Which creations meet R.* For A supported in R = {0, e_z}, first order needs I subset R. The omitted faces with owner support exactly {0,e_z} are the ten faces anchored at 0 that AL1 already counts (6 xz with r<=2, 4 yz with s=0); there are none with owner support {0} or {e_z} (those slots are the selected xy classes, zero here). Enumeration in scratch (`faces.py`) confirms: 7 incident anchors, 10 omitted faces with owners inside R, 82 omitted faces with at least one owner in R.

*Constants.* Because each W_f Omega_0 is an H_0 eigenvector of energy 24 in delta units (4 links at 8*3/4), c^{(1)} = (tau/72) sum_f W_f Omega_0 restricted to M = owners(f), and ||c^{(1)}_{{0,e_z}}|| = (tau/72) sqrt(10)/2 = 0.02196 tau (orthogonal face states). The first-order expectation change is 2 Re <Omega_0, A c-hat^{(1)} Omega_0>; for A = W it equals 2 (tau/72) <W W_{f_W}>_Haar = tau/144 (positive for tau>0 with I1's sign), and for A = W^2 and for A = W alpha^0_theta(W) it is exactly zero (Section 3(iii)). The all-order remainder from AM2's majorant: self-consistently ||c||_a <= 448.04 tau, ||c - c^{(1)}||_a <= 288 J ||c||_a <= 3.61e6 tau^2, and the second-order state remainder estimate is ~1.4e7 tau^2 (1.4e-9 at the cap).

*Candidate c_1.* Two honest levels: (A) crude, majorant-only: |omega(W)| <= 4 ||c||_a <= 1792 |tau| + O(tau^2) (1.8e-5 at the cap, 45x better than D = 8.08e-4 but above 1e-6); (B) exact first order with majorant remainder: omega(W) = tau/144 + r, |r| <= c_1' tau^2 with c_1' of order 1.5e7 (so |omega(W)| <= 0.0069 tau + 1.5e-9 at the cap). For W^2: |omega(W^2) - 1/4| <= c_2 tau^2, c_2 ~ 1.5e7, i.e. the brief's O(tau) target is beaten by one order.

*Unproved steps, in order of risk.* (1) An anchored-norm bound for the left eigenvector l (or a support-size-weighted Kotecky-Preiss condition for the polymer form sum over pairs of creation collections); AM2's anchored norm has no e^{a|I|} weight, so either AM2's combinatorics must be re-run with weights w^{|I|} or l must be bounded directly by the same 16 8^k (1+5k/4) counting with annihilators. (2) Exactness of the first-order coefficient: it is the k=0 fixed-point term, so this is a bookkeeping identity, but the diagonal/scalar terms retained in AM2 must be shown not to contribute at first order in the local sector. (3) Passage to AQ: AQ1 (report line 37) takes a trace-norm convergent subsequence of centered-box ground densities, so a bound uniform in N passes to omega_num, provided the centered-box Hamiltonians are exactly AM2's family (same whole-star boundary), which the contract must state.

### (ii) Observation 2: the ~1.2-1.3e-6 floor and the sharper comparison

Reproduced with mpmath (30 digits) and cross-checked with Arb (`floor.py`): with k = 49 tau/4, s = 1, tau = 1e-8 and an O(tau) state term, cost(L) = (k s/pi) log(1+L^2/s^2) + (1/2)(2s/(pi L)) is minimized at L* = 4.0816e6 with value **1.26509e-6** (log term 1.1871e-6, tail 7.80e-8); with AT4's D the minimum is 1.26515e-6 at L* = 4.085e6; Arb enclosure at L = 1/(2k): [1.26508822330e-6 +/- 1.97e-18]. AT4's frozen L = 1e4 total 8.4152e-4 is also reproduced. So the brief's statement is correct for this kernel.

*Most promising sharper comparison: change the transfer kernel, not the real-time bound.* The identity C(s) = int f-hat(theta) c(theta) dtheta holds for every f in L^1 with f-hat in L^1 and f(x) = e^{-sx} on [0, infinity) (Fourier inversion plus Fubini on the finite measure eta; no gap needed, only nonnegativity, and the free reference uses f(3) = e^{-3s}). Take f(x) = e^{-sx} for x >= 0, f(x) = e^{-sx} P((x+a)/a) on [-a,0] with P the C^2 quintic step, f = 0 below -a. Then

  |C(s) - C_0(s)| <= (D_state) M_0 + k M_1,  M_j = int |theta|^j |f-hat(theta)| dtheta,        (M2)

with no L and no logarithm. Preview values (`kernel2.py`, s=1): a=1: M_0 ~ 1.29, M_1 ~ 2.99; a=2: M_0 ~ 1.67, M_1 ~ 2.44. Since M_0 >= sup|f| = 1 for any kernel and the Poisson kernel has M_0 = 1 exactly, the state term can lose up to ~30% while the dynamics term drops from 1.27e-6 to k M_1 ~ 3.0-3.7e-7 at the cap. A crude rigorous-style bound through the total variation of f''' gives only M_1 <= 10.2 (1.25e-6): the production route must evaluate f-hat in closed form (polynomial times exponential on an interval is elementary) with directed rounding, which the project's rational exp/log enclosures already support; Arb is the natural cross-check.

*Second-order structure makes the whole budget O(tau^2).* The Dyson remainder for the relative unitary e^{i theta G} e^{-i theta A} with bounded B gives ||alpha_theta(W) - alpha^0_theta(W) - (first order)|| <= (k theta)^2/2, and the first-order Duhamel term has zero Haar expectation by the same selection rules (one face insertion), so its contribution in omega is <= (c_R tau)(k theta). With M_2 finite for the smooth kernel,

  |C(s) - e^{-3s}/4| <= S_2 tau^2 M_0 + c_R k tau M_1 + k^2 M_2 / 2,                   (M3)

which is ~1.5e-9 at the cap with the majorant constants (all three terms). The hypotheses are: the AT4 relative-unitary domain argument extended to second order (same bounded-B Dyson expansion, no new domain issue), the (M1) local expansion with a proved l-bound, and the kernel inversion conditions. If only first order is proved, (M2) gives ~3.5e-7; if the l-bound fails, one falls back to the reset bound D and (M2) gives ~1.04e-3, worse than AT4. A Euclidean-side route through AM2's similarity transform (finite-volume C_N(s) = <(W-m)psi, e^{-s(H-E)}(W-m)psi>/<psi,psi> with A = H_{0,R} + H_out and Davis-Kahan for psi against the product ground state of A) is also O(tau) with constants ~ 400 tau at the cap, but it needs a volume-uniform comparison of ground energies E and E_A and is weaker than (M3); keep it as a derivation control, not the main route.

### (iii) Observation 3: the interaction shift and an exact finite-graph model

*Order counting first.* For the frozen observable family the interaction-induced shift of C(s) and of omega(W^2) is O(tau^2), not O(tau): every first-order term carries one inserted face W_f and either shares no link with the observable (Haar integral of a single nontrivial character vanishes) or is the W face itself (<W^3>_Haar = 0 by center symmetry). An O(tau)-error method therefore cannot see it, and an O(tau^2)-error method cannot either; resolving it at the cap needs the exact second-order coefficient kappa_2(s) plus an O(tau^3) remainder (width ~1e-24 x constant, arithmetically trivial in `Fraction`, analytically a third-order derivation). The only O(tau) shift on this family is the mean, omega(W) = tau/144 + O(tau^2) = 6.9e-11 at the cap: resolvable only with an O(tau^2) remainder below 6.9e-11, i.e. constant below ~7e5, which the crude majorant (~1.5e7) misses by a factor ~20. Route (b) is therefore a real derivation target with a quantified gap; the executable route is (a).
