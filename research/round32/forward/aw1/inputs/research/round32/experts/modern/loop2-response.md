## 3. Left-eigenvector/Riesz versus psi=psi_out+delta

**Equivalent as identities, not as proofs.** For real tau the Riesz projection is `|psi><psi|/||psi||^2`, and the normalized left vector is `l=e^{-C*}psi/||psi||^2`, so `<l,e^C A e^-C Omega_0>=<psi,A psi>/||psi||^2`: the same number the skeptic's decomposition evaluates. The decomposition instead uses positivity, orthogonality and AM2's ||c||_a only.
- **Safer forward route for AV1:** `psi_out+delta` with the explicit reduced density (as drafted). Its precise unproved step, the ground-*vector* cutoff removal, closes in three lines: for the nested cutoff ground vectors `psi_N` (in the form domain, `<psi_N,H psi_N>=E_{0,N}`), Eckart's inequality with AM2's untruncated gap gives `1-|<psi,psi_N>|^2<=(E_{0,N}-E_0)/(E_1-E_0)<=2(E_{0,N}-E_0)`, which tends to 0 because AM2 §6 proves `E_{0,N}` decreases to `E_0`; so `||rho_R^N-rho_R||_1<=2 sqrt(2(E_{0,N}-E_0))->0` and the N-uniform bound passes, then to AQ by local trace-norm convergence.
