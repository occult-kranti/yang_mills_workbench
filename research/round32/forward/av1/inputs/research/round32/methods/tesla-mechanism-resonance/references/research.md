# Tesla research reference

## Coverage and reading status

Selected passages consulted 21 September 2026. Patents below were inspected as historical technical proposals, not certified performance. Museum catalogues provide expansion routes, not a claim to have read every patent or book. Modern equations are our explanatory reconstructions. The source map deliberately retains a lower-confidence niche transcription rather than presenting its provenance as settled.

## Mathematical engineering

**T1 — US381968A, Electro-magnetic motor (1888)**, [patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/US381968A/en). Read description and phased field arrangements. Multiple circuits produce a moving magnetic field. The transferable practice is representing a mechanism through relative timing and geometry, not merely invoking “vibration.”

Modern reconstruction: orthogonal field components B_x=B₀cos(ωt), B_y=B₀sin(ωt) yield constant |B|=B₀ and rotating direction. A phase error δ makes the trajectory generally elliptical. This is a checkable mechanism with a discriminating control: identical in-phase components do not yield the same rotating field. Do not attribute this notation or every modern motor theorem to Tesla.

**T2 — Experiments with Alternate Currents of High Potential and High Frequency (1892)**, [lecture transcription](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/13476/old/13476-h/13476-h.htm). Read coil construction, adjustment of capacity/self-induction, and losses in gaseous media. Tesla discusses empirical tuning and limits on calculating behavior from simplified dimensions. It qualifies the later claim that mental simulation alone always suffices.

Modern reconstruction: LQ̈+RQ̇+Q/C=V(t), with stored energy E=LQ̇²/2+Q²/(2C), gives Ė=VQ̇−RQ̇². In the ideal undamped case ω₀=1/√(LC); underdamped free motion has ω_d²=1/(LC)−R²/(4L²). Coupled oscillators need their full capacitance/inductance matrix. Resonant buildup is supplied by an input and limited by losses or model assumptions. Quantum level degeneracy has an analogous phase bookkeeping problem, but electrical circuits do not establish a gauge-theory theorem.

**T3 — Colorado Springs Notes, June 21–22, 1899, p.43**, [1978 edition transcription](https://teslauniverse.com/nikola-tesla/books/nikola-tesla-colorado-springs-notes-1899-1900/43). Read the estimated current, wire quantities and modified primary connection. Tesla explicitly marks assumptions in computing losses and describes sharp tuning of two interacting vibrations. This supports a notebook practice combining estimates, material quantities and revised arrangements; it does not verify global wireless power claims.

## Patents: mechanisms and boundaries

**T4 — US645576A, System of transmission of electrical energy (1900)**, [patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/US645576A/en). Read elevated terminals, generator and natural-media conduction claims, including synchronized receiving circuits. Record the source, propagation assumptions and receiver. Granting these claims is distinct from measuring useful global transfer efficiency.

**T5 — US685957A, Apparatus for utilization of radiant energy (1901)**, [patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/US685957A/en). Read the exposed plate, condenser, ground connection and discharge mechanism. It names an external radiation source and offers a particle interpretation. It is not a source-free energy machine, and its historical explanation is not silently identified with modern microscopic physics.

**T6 — US613809A, control of moving vessels (1898)**, [patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/US613809A/en). Inspected identity and apparatus/control description. Use it to distinguish a transmitted command from onboard actuation and power. Remote control is not transfer of all propulsion energy. **T7 — US1061206A, Turbine (1913)**, [patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/US1061206A/en), inspected identity and disk-fluid mechanism description. Performance still requires flow, torque, losses and load conditions.

The [Tesla Museum patent catalogue](https://tesla-museum.org/en/nikola-tesla-2/patents/) distinguishes national equivalents and invention families. Its displayed counts contain an apparent internal arithmetic inconsistency, so do not repeat an exact total uncritically. The [archive activity catalogue](https://tesla-museum.org/en/legacy/archive/activity/) spans far more documents than this selection.

## Imagination and speculative physics

**T8 — My Inventions I: My Early Life (1919)**, [autobiography](https://teslauniverse.com/nikola-tesla/articles/my-inventions-i-my-early-life). Read visualization, external impressions and habits. Tesla retrospectively claims detailed mental operation before construction. This is self-report, not independently measured cognition. His reported repetitions divisible by three do not supply a 3–6–9 law of nature. Convert mental rehearsal into inspectable models and tests.

**T9 — The Problem of Increasing Human Energy (1900)**, [article](https://teslauniverse.com/nikola-tesla/articles/problem-increasing-human-energy). Read the opening mechanical analogy and discussion of a hypothetical social “velocity.” The text itself does not operationally determine that velocity. It demonstrates both the generative power and limit of extending E=MV²/2 beyond mechanics. In our project, an analogy needs a measurable map before its equation enters an inference.

**T10 — Talking with the Planets (1901)**, [article](https://teslauniverse.com/nikola-tesla/articles/talking-planets). Read the transmission argument and interpretation of signals. Preserve the distinction between an observed disturbance, an instrumental account and an inferred intelligent source. A striking pattern does not identify its cause. Use alternate explanations and calibration controls rather than treating this essay as verified extraterrestrial contact.

## Esoteric associations and provenance

**T11 — Vivekananda to E.T. Sturdy, 13 February 1896**, [Complete Works V, letter LVII in this edition](https://www.ramakrishnavivekananda.info/vivekananda/volume_5/epistles_first_series/057_blessed_and_beloved.htm). The contemporary letter reports Tesla's interest in prana, akasha and cycles, and an anticipated mathematical demonstration. It records Vivekananda's report and expectation; it does not contain that completed proof. Some secondary sites number it LVIII. Cite date and recipient to avoid this mismatch. Vedanta is a philosophical/religious tradition, not a synonym for all Western occultism.

**T12 — Man's Greatest Achievement**, attributed New York American, 6 July 1930, [niche transcription](https://teslaresearch.jimdofree.com/articles-interviews/man-s-greatest-achievement-by-nikola-tesla-new-york-american-july-6-1930/). Read the akasha/prana and matter-formation passage. This host points to another non-archival site; the newspaper facsimile was not verified in this pass. Label the text as an attributed transcription with weaker provenance. Its metaphysical language motivates questions about substrate, transformation and control, but supplies neither an operational model nor demonstrated mind-over-matter effects.

Do not convert an ether claim into a modern quantum vacuum identity, equate prana with a measured energy variable, or infer a hidden theory from absent papers. Do not assume claims about magical powers or occult membership are facts. Maintain a ledger: documented historical statement / proposed interpretation / operational hypothesis / derived consequence / observed result. A historically real belief can still be physically unsupported.

## Entire-project application and expansion

For research, select actual states and calculate coupling and detuning before naming resonance. For computation, separate finite fixtures from full-model estimates. For site design, display the input assumptions and certified interval rather than an invented trajectory. For roadmaps, pursue the omitted channel most likely to overturn the mechanism. These are modern adaptations of selected practices.

Expand through the [Museum writings catalogue](https://tesla-museum.org/en/nikola-tesla-2/writings/), [lectures](https://tesla-museum.org/en/nikola-tesla-2/lectures/), patent families and the complete Colorado notebook. Reading leads, not completed reviews: 1888 polyphase lecture; 1891 illumination lecture; 1893 lectures; 1896–97 X-ray papers; 1919 True Wireless; remaining My Inventions chapters; turbine papers; authentic correspondence and original newspaper scans. Trace niche articles back to these records. Exhaustive reading of all patents, books and attributed statements is not claimed.
