# Historical physics panel

Recover the current source-bound research checkpoint, paper and missing proof obligations before selecting a question. Use the Newton, Tesla and paired-physics skills when applicable. Read [the lens and evidence guide](references/lenses-and-evidence.md) before interpreting a historical or occult claim.

## Ground the panel

Treat each named expert as a contemporary research lens based on selected documented work. Never claim participation, endorsement, omniscience, recreated private thoughts or human peer review. Record shared premises and correlated model authorship. Use separate agents when the user requests a team; honor explicit bans on delegation.

For each source record its URL, version/date, actual passage or section read, reading depth, provenance, claim, relevance and unresolved verification. Separate a primary historical assertion from its truth as a physical claim. Treat patents as technical proposals, government custody as provenance, and subreddit posts as evidence of discourse. Trace surprising quotations and claims back to original records. Leave unread books and inaccessible records as leads. Search current technical primary literature; distinguish accepted theorem, preprint proposal, numerical evidence and claimed solution. A search hit is not a verified theorem.

## Translate inspiration into a test

Write historical idea → proposed modern interpretation → explicit model → observable → discriminating control. Without the last three, retain cultural interpretation. Never add a force, golden ratio, frequency, hidden medium or consciousness variable solely because an analogy suggests it. Classify every parameter as coordinate, regulator, coupling, state preparation, measured quantity or model deformation, with units and an identifying map.

Check what observations actually determine. Distinguish a dimensionless curve, a physically timed measurement, a demodulated trace and a known carrier. Derive exact parameter fibers before fitting. Differentiate an asymptotic readout formula only as that formula; do not turn its slope into an exact derivative of finite-parameter dynamics. Carry full model errors, time uncertainty, phase aliases and omitted channels into any operational discriminator.

## Run adaptive loops

1. Collect bounded recommendations from each lens and a skeptical triage.
2. Have the advisor select one explicit target, record disagreements, and freeze contract, model, scales, sources and acceptance tests before production.
3. Where paired work is appropriate, keep current forward and reverse reports unread across producers until both freeze. Use independent implementations and a mathematical completeness argument; repeated execution alone is not independent reasoning.
4. Review proofs and executed controls. Keep accepted narrow claims, failed certificates and unresolved parent targets separate. Correct defects before admission and retain repair provenance.
5. Select the next loop from that review. Do not pre-execute all loops and relabel them feedback. Count research loops separately from source searches, replays, publication and skill tests.
6. Stop after the authorized count. Update the roadmap, evidence network and draft addendum without rewriting accepted historical evidence.

For support estimates, distinguish per-source operator contraction, finite interaction norm and contraction in the same interaction norm. Weight loss must be paid at every nonlinear step; one conjugation bound does not prove an infinite induction. A failed positive upper certificate does not imply actual divergence.

## Assess progress and publish

Report what changed, derivation, exact versus numerical evidence, application, nearest checked prior source, novelty status and limitations. Bind executed code, contracts, dependencies and outputs; test the staged source tree and verify the published remote revision. Keep website deployment distinct from source push.

For open mathematical problems, report named completed investigations and unresolved proof obligations. Do not invent a percentage of the final proof, probability of success or completion date from counts of equations, pages, plots or graph nodes. A task-completion fraction is permissible with its explicit denominator and must not be labeled percent of the theorem solved. If a requested percentage has no defensible metric, say so and supply the obligation table.
