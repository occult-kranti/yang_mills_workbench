### Observation 1 — "AM2 may remove the square root"

**Attempt to break it.** The brief's mechanism, "only creations meeting `R` enter `<Omega_tau, A Omega_tau>`", is false as stated: both `<psi, A psi>` and `<psi, psi>` contain every creation in the volume, and `<psi,psi>=1+sum_M||psi_M||^2` grows with volume (the product-vacuum overlap of the true ground state decays exponentially in `|Lambda|`). Dropping outside creations from the numerator alone, or normalizing by `1+O(tau^2)`, is the orthogonality-catastrophe error and would be volume-dependent. Non-unitarity of `e^{-C}` is not itself an obstacle: `e^{-C}` is never used as a map on states, only as the explicit vector. The adjoint enters only through `||c_I><Omega_I| (x) 1||=||c_I||`. The passage to the AQ state indeed needs only local trace-norm convergence, provided the finite-volume bound is uniform in the volume.

**What a correct statement needs (skeptic's sketch, to be derived independently).** Creators commute and `chat_I chat_J=0` whenever `I` and `J` overlap (AM2 §2), so `e^{-C}=prod_I(1-chat_I)`. Order the product: `psi=prod_{I meets R}(1-chat_I) psi_out`, `psi_out=prod_{I disjoint from R}(1-chat_I)Omega_0=Omega_R (x) phi_out`. Expanding the first product, only pairwise-disjoint families survive and each member must meet the two-site `R`, so at most two factors appear: `psi=psi_out+delta`, `delta=-sum_{I meets R}chat_I psi_out+sum_{I ni 0, J ni e_z, I cap J empty}chat_I chat_J psi_out`. Every term of `delta` is excited on at least one site of `R`, so `delta` is orthogonal to `psi_out`, and `||delta||<=(2||c||_a+||c||_a^2)||psi_out||=:epsilon||psi_out||`. Writing `xi` for the outside contraction of `delta` against `phi_out` (`||xi||<=||phi_out|| ||delta||`), the reduced density on `R` is `rho_R=[||psi_out||^2 P_R+|Omega_R><xi|+|xi><Omega_R|+Tr_out|delta><delta|]/(||psi_out||^2(1+epsilon^2))`, hence

`||rho_R-P_R||_1 <= 2 epsilon (1+epsilon)/(1+epsilon^2) <= 2 epsilon + 2 epsilon^2`,

uniformly in the volume, with `epsilon<=2||c||_a+||c||_a^2`. Outside normalization cancels because it multiplies every term. Additional obligations: (i) AM2's cutoff removal covers eigenvalues; the ground *vector* limit (isolated simple eigenvalue, monotone form convergence, strong resolvent convergence of the Riesz projection) must be written; (ii) for a nonzero selected triple `P_R` is the selected-reference product and `Tr(P_R W)` must be evaluated, not assumed zero (AQ2 §5 shows it is zero for the xz face because a `z` link is free, but reference dynamics are not free); (iii) the anchored norm is a maximum over sites, so `sum_{I ni 0}||c_I||<=||c||_a` is the only step where `R` enters.
