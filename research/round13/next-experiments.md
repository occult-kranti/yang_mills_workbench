# Advisor's next experiments and proof obligations

These tasks follow the accepted round13 results. Their purpose is to discriminate hypotheses, including ones the skeptic expects to fail. An experiment that supports an objection updates the roadmap; it is not discarded in a search for agreement.

## 1. Extract a usable stability threshold

**Target:** replace the existential smallness assumption in the fixed-spacing gap application by a conservative, explicit sufficient value for the actual blocked interaction support `S={0,e1,e2,e3}`.

**Forward premises:** onsite Hilbert `L²(SU(2))^⊗3`; free gap `3 alpha/4`; perturbation norm at most `4 lambda_max/alpha` in free-gap units; the reviewed padded finite-box construction; known stability proof.

**Backward obligations:** a numerical `kappa0>0` requires every cluster/combinatorial constant, every convergence inequality and every normalization in an applicable constructive proof. An unspecified `c(S)` is not a value.

**Work:** trace the source theorem to its proved estimates; build an explicit inequality ledger; derive a finite rational admissible region or retain the unresolved constant. Independently recompute combinatorial support bounds and all factor conversions. Test that weakening a required inequality causes rejection. Do not approximate the admissible threshold from a favorable small-box eigenvalue scan. If the published proof provides no extractable constants, report that exact blockage and compare another applicable theorem without altering the model.

**Acceptance:** exact rational certificate for a stated positive threshold and a complete conventional argument that its hypotheses imply the volume-uniform gap. **Failure:** any unresolved existential constant, undeclared finite local dimension assumption, or boundary convention mismatch.

## 2. Connected three-square central observable

**Target:** compare exact-model local dynamics across nested connected regions, using the locality theorem to control spatial truncation and separate bounds for representation and stepping errors.

**Forward:** construct the graph, link incidences and Wilson multiplication from independent complex SU(2) matrices before choosing reduced coordinates. Preserve shared-link kinetic cross terms and physical Haar inner product.

**Backward:** a certified central-observable tolerance requires three independent contributions: omitted boundary interactions, representation truncation and the computed propagator. A small difference between two computations cannot supply any missing term.

**Experiment:** use a bounded central plaquette trace and a common specified initial state embedded in both link Hilbert spaces. Include zero drive, sign-reversed segments, one omitted shared link, nonzero initial representation and an iterator geometry input. Derive the actual chain radius; compare it to the support graph, not a coordinate guess. At least one run should deliberately yield the trivial `2||A||` bound to distinguish inconclusive estimates from a physical instability.

**Acceptance:** source-bound graph identity checks, exact error budget and independently calculated reference on a small fixture. **Failure:** absent shared-link terms, unmatched electric coefficients, or replacing an initial driven state with an interacting ground state.

## 3. Interacting Euclidean loop hierarchy

**Target:** establish a factorized two-square Euclidean baseline, then extend rational moment bounds to an explicitly declared nonfactorized loop measure. This is a separate state from the Hamiltonian vacuum.

**Advisor correction:** for the ordinary pure Wilson action `kappa1 x + kappa2 y` on two adjacent open planar squares, maximal-tree gauge fixing leaves independent Haar holonomies `U,V`; the tilted measure factorizes. The shared link produces a Hamiltonian kinetic cross term, not a Euclidean face-face coupling in this baseline. Consequently `E[xy]=u(kappa1)u(kappa2)` and `Cov(x,y)=0` exactly. Do not present this factorization as an approximation that should fail.

For a nonfactorized finite experiment, either declare the changed bounded action `kappa1 x + kappa2 y + eta z`, where `z=Tr(UV)/2`, or choose a genuinely interacting higher-dimensional plaquette complex and derive its actual action. The parameter `eta` is a mixed-loop deformation of the finite Euclidean action. It is not already present merely because two squares share a link, and it does not automatically return to continuum pure Yang–Mills.

**Forward:** derive shared-link integration-by-parts identities directly from the declared action and Haar measure. Use the exact SU(2) trace identity to reduce trace count while tracking loop geometry. Retain invariants `x,y,z` with `(z-xy)^2 <= (1-x^2)(1-y^2)` and correct central-element limits.

**Backward:** an interval for a loop expectation needs a positive representing measure or necessary localizing constraints, normalization, complete retained identities and a certified optimization error. A full uniqueness theorem additionally needs density of the actual test algebra and a treatment of singular orbit strata.

**Experiment:** zero coupling exact Haar references; the exactly factorized pure two-square baseline at arbitrary finite `kappa1,kappa2`; the explicitly defined `eta=0` limit of the mixed-loop model; nonzero mixed-loop deformation or the selected higher-dimensional action; swapped orientation; a false-factorization point for that genuinely nonfactorized measure; exact rational dual witnesses. Compare independent direct group quadrature only as a numerical diagnostic.

**Acceptance:** narrow contained intervals with replayable rational witnesses, correct exact-factorization baseline and false-closure rejection where the changed action actually destroys factorization. **Failure:** inventing a Euclidean coupling from the shared-link kinetic term, a numerical SDP eigenvalue presented as an exact sign certificate, or a trace identity alone presented as factorization.

## 4. Certified scalar trajectory error

**Target:** upgrade the current floating Riccati trajectory comparison into a total error enclosure on a declared compact positive-coupling interval.

**Available result:** the regular origin series has a rational launch-error bound. The response document also derives a conditional residual propagation inequality for a nonnegative approximate path.

**Missing ingredient:** an enclosure of the residual on every point of each integration interval, including interpolation and arithmetic error. Sampled residuals are insufficient.

**Experiment:** a rational polynomial or interval Taylor path with nonnegativity and continuous residual certificates; propagate its launch error; independently enclose the Bessel ratio at held-out endpoints. Test negative coupling by proved parity, the exact zero branch, and a wrong coefficient whose solution belongs to a different prior.

**Acceptance:** a closed rational error interval, with no floating diagnostic promoted into a proof premise. **Failure:** a sampled error maximum or local tolerance used as a global certificate.

## 5. Continuum bridge and the final target

**Target:** identify a route that remains useful as `a->0`, volume grows and the bare coupling follows a matched scale trajectory, while constructing a nontrivial theory with the required field-theory axioms and positive physical spectral threshold.

The current gap theorem has `lambda/alpha=4/g_H^4<kappa_*`. The standard weak-bare-coupling direction has this ratio growing without bound. The current local propagation estimate likewise loses useful continuum control at fixed physical boundary distance. These are failures of sufficient estimates in that regime, not proofs that the true continuum theory cannot exist.

Search both directions with separate fields for gauge group, spacetime dimension, state, observable algebra, action, lattice spacing, energy units, boundary and quantifier order. A new mass, scalar, deformation, compactification or coupling profile must have either an exact elimination identity or a separately controlled return limit to pure Yang–Mills. A common symbol cannot provide this match. Keep the final target blocked until each required construction and spectral premise has evidence.
