# Independent proof-adapter review

The adapter joins conventional reviewed mathematical implications to a finite Horn-rule search. It is not a formal mathematics kernel. Scientific acceptance remains in its separate ledger; a successful route means that the supplied, explicitly conditional premises imply the target under those admitted rules.

## Admission defect found

The initial `make_library` accepted a previously passed replay object but did not bind it to the current frozen-input bytes. An independently reproduced mutation replaced the premises of existing rule M8 with `compact_measure` after successful evidence replay. The helper then produced a one-rule proof of `hierarchy_unique` without renewed review of that implication. The normal `execute` path did not modify its frozen inputs, so its already recorded routes were not affected; nevertheless, the public helper admitted stale scientific data.

The preserved `proof_adapter_initial_binding_failure.json` and `proof_adapter_before_binding_fix.py` document the actual successful bypass. The repaired adapter binds the complete 19-input fingerprint, revalidates current scientific hashes and reviewed rule IDs, and rejects changed scientific or evidence bytes before construction. The independent before/after controls now pass.

## Independent verification method

`audit_proof_adapter.py` uses isolated copies of all inputs and the actual manifest builder. It performs a real frozen-evidence replay and checks every positive and negative route with a separately written exhaustive Dijkstra/Horn solver. Each positive ordered certificate is replayed with an independent set-of-facts evaluator and its cost is compared with the independently minimal cost.

Additional controls withdraw shared onsite, absolute coefficient-envelope and incidence premises. They require failure when stability smallness, all-order moment constraints or unbounded-order optimizer assumptions are missing. The full four-dimensional target remains unreachable. Target statements cannot be inserted as starting hypotheses or computed gates.

File and directory symlinks, traversal attempts, changed source bytes, rewritten historical core hashes and a symlinked manifest are rejected. Post-replay changes to rules, moment certificates, response reports or the scientific ledger must be rejected even though the earlier replay succeeded.

## Trust boundary and limits

The checked ledger is a trusted reviewed artifact with byte bindings; this software does not authenticate a human reviewer cryptographically. A user who deliberately rewrites the code, evidence and acceptance together has changed that trust base. Likewise, graph-only unit fixtures can intentionally supply abstract premises to test a planner; their output is not scientific acceptance.

Response diagnostics are source-bound provenance in this adapter. Their floating calculations are not rerun as a proof premise for the analytic response identity. Finite moment witnesses and exact locality arithmetic are replayed. Explicit all-order hypotheses and qualitative stability-smallness assumptions remain separate from those finite computations.

Final acceptance: **69 independent gates pass**, both normally and under optimized Python. All 24 route outcomes and positive minimum costs agree with the separate solver. Exact reviewed adapter/builder/test/core hashes are recorded in `proof_adapter_review.json`. Scientific acceptance and its 532-check count are unchanged by this separate software review.
