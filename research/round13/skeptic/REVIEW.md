## Defects found and fixed

1. `bound_from_geometry` traversed a plaquette iterable twice. A generator therefore became empty before the radius calculation, incorrectly returning an exact-zero boundary effect. The fixed entry point freezes all geometry inputs once. Independent generator cases in two and three dimensions now reproduce the same positive bounds as reusable sets. The original source and failed result remain in this folder. Published study fixtures used sets, so their numerical bounds were unaffected.
2. The response solver converted grid entries to floating numbers before rejecting Booleans. `[True]` became a valid sample at 1.0. The repaired path validates every raw entry; Python and NumPy Booleans, mixed lists, strings and complex substitutions are rejected. The original source and reproducer remain. Scientific study grids contained no such entries.
3. The initial logical inventory did not explicitly include normalization in the all-order moment premise. The zero sequence satisfies the homogeneous recurrence and PSD conditions but is not a probability measure. The final premise includes `m0=1` at one finite real κ.
4. The locality and algorithm-convergence routes initially compressed essential limit assumptions. The final claims explicitly require matching coefficients, one integrable uniform envelope, fixed bounded support, nested exhaustive regions, and—on the algorithm branch—successfully certified orders tending to infinity with slack tending to zero. The implemented cutoff at level 6 does not prove that last condition.
5. The first stability supplement could have confused grouped empty-boundary interactions with all individually contained plaquettes. Its explicit free-halo/coefficient-mask construction now shows why the uniform source theorem applies to the latter finite-volume family. A malformed fraction command in the same supplement was repaired before hashing.

## Mathematical critique that remains relevant

A finite rejected candidate must carry its rational quadratic-form witness. The witness slope establishes the excluded half-line; rejection at a point alone does not locate a feasible interval. Singular PSD matrices require zero-pivot handling. The code's exact congruence test agrees with an exhaustive principal-minor implementation on adversarial and deterministic random matrices.

The complete hierarchy proof is stronger than the finite calculation and has different quantifiers. Positivity and the `(1−x²)` localizer make multiplication by x a bounded selfadjoint contraction in the GNS quotient. Its spectral measure is supported on the required interval. The recurrence extends from polynomials to C1 tests by approximating derivatives and integrating. The resulting distribution equation determines the interior density, and its endpoint terms separately exclude atoms. Full finite feasible intervals consequently shrink to the true mean. The particular algorithm inherits convergence only along a successful unbounded-order, vanishing-slack sequence; that is not an observed property of six levels.

The rational point measure at κ=9/8 and mean 1/3 passes the first identity and the first PSD/localizer constraints exactly, yet the next identity has residual 8/9. This is a direct counterexample to accepting a closure from one equation plus positivity. The response equation succeeds because differentiating the same known exponential family gives `u′=Var(x)`, retaining rather than deleting fluctuations. Its arbitrary Bessel-K branch fails regularity at the origin. A coefficient-2 equation belongs to a different reference measure and cannot replace the SU(2) Haar coefficient.

The locality proof must use the commutator differential inequality after unitary resummation. A raw Dyson expansion permits interaction with the whole accumulated union and does not justify consecutive-path counting. The accepted derivation uses the correct recursion. Its estimate concerns one common state and dynamics, not separately chosen ground states. A fixed-coefficient nearest-neighbor spin chain with a unique vacuum and gap tending to zero supplies an additional logical counterexample to locality implying a uniform gap.

The static stability branch requires its own theorem and smallness premise. With outgoing-link cells, the normalized electric gap is one and the perturbation obeys `eta <= (4/3) binom(d,2) lambda_max/alpha`. In three spatial dimensions this is `eta <= 4 lambda_max/alpha`. Choosing an existential regime with `c2 eta <1/2` yields a lower gap `3 alpha/8`. No value of `c1` or `c2` was extracted, so no positive numerical coupling in the simulations is certified to lie inside that regime. Restriction to Gauss-invariant states is valid because the unique vacuum carries a trivial one-dimensional continuous character of the product of SU(2) gauge groups.

The fixed-spacing stability domain is exited by the conventional weak-bare-coupling trajectory `lambda/alpha=4/g_H^4` as `g_H→0`. A volume-uniform lattice result alone therefore gives no continuation to the intended continuum path. The missing reconstruction, nontriviality, physical scale matching and uniform physical spectral control remain explicit.
