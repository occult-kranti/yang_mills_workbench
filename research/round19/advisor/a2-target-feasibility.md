# Conditional A2 target feasibility after A1

Status: conditional target note, **not an A2 contract**. A2 is not frozen until A1 has an accepted canonical producer run and a repaired independent comparison.

If A1 is accepted, the recommended substantive second loop is the direct product-representation construction for the summable exception. This is the best next A2 target because it uses the new A1 boundary-consistent local component result as an input and attempts a real infinite-representation/domain/gap theorem, rather than rerunning finite norm budgets.

## Chosen conditional target

Construct an infinite product reference representation from complete strip ground states and free Haar factors. Define

```text
H_ref = sum_C (H_C - E_C) + sum_free alpha C_e
```

on an explicit finite-excitation core or closed quadratic-form domain, with one positive physical scale `E_star` and `delta/E_star = (alpha/E_star)/8`. Add a spatially summable perturbation

```text
V = - sum_f nu_f x_f,
||V|| <= beta <= alpha |tau|,
```

with termwise zero reference mean proved from actual unused-link Haar factors or another accepted A1 zero-mean mechanism. The target theorem is a unique ground and a positive spectral gap in this product representation under a condition such as `beta < delta`, with the exact min-max/form and essential-spectrum argument written out.

## Scope boundaries

This would be a direct product-representation and summable-perturbation result. It must not be labeled as any of the following unless separately proved:

- convergence of all finite clipped restrictions to the product representation;
- local thermodynamic convergence of finite-volume states, forms or resolvents;
- homogeneous dense nondecaying rotor stability;
- continuum Yang-Mills construction or Clay mass gap.

A finite-restriction convergence loop could be a later target, but it needs boundary stabilization, local-state convergence and spectral convergence as separate premises. A dense homogeneous loop should instead use a Yarotsky-style small per-range perturbation theorem and would still lack explicit constants.

## Feasibility after canonical A1

Feasible if A1 acceptance supplies these inputs:

1. every complete strip/clipped component has a unique full-link ground and local gap at least `alpha/8` in the declared `E_star` scale;
2. complete strip ground states and free Haar factors form a well-defined infinite product reference vector and local algebra;
3. all perturbation terms outside `H_ref` have a summable absolute norm budget, with no cancellation used in `beta`;
4. zero trial mean is proved termwise in the actual product reference;
5. the form-domain proof controls offdiagonal coupling from the reference vector and the essential spectrum.

Not feasible as a dense-goal claim unless an additional source theorem supplies a smallness condition for nondecaying plaquette interactions. Not feasible as a continuum claim under current evidence.

## Immediate post-A1 contract shape

If A1 gate passes, freeze A2 around the product-representation theorem with these acceptance gates:

- exact definition of the infinite component partition and reference product;
- proof of closed/nonnegative `H_ref` and finite-excitation-core gap;
- bounded self-adjoint summable `V` with `||V|| <= beta`;
- codimension-one min-max or equivalent form theorem proving ground isolation and gap;
- controls for homogeneous nondecaying remainder, `E_star=0`, static `kappa` as energy, missing zero mean, finite-only gap promotion and finite-restriction convergence overclaim.
