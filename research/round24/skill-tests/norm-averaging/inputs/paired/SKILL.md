## Pair the two directions

Freeze the theory, graph, state space, observable, units, regulator and boundary family. Forward derivation computes consequences from these premises. Reverse reconstruction starts from a precise desired conclusion and lists sufficient premises and discriminating tests. A meeting requires the same physical problem and independently checked evidence, not merely matching numbers or shared prose.

Use a fixed energy reference E_star > 0 for spectral comparisons, recording alpha/E_star, coupling ratios, lattice spacing and volume independently. Static Euclidean coefficients do not define physical time or Hamiltonian energies. Reject scale comparisons at E_star = 0 instead of normalizing by a convenient numerical tolerance.

Double Fibonacci spirals may organize investigation nodes. Geometry and node numbering are presentation choices until an independently specified observable, prediction and comparison model test physical relevance. Never place a golden-ratio coefficient in the action just to force agreement. See [the scale and exception contract](references/scale-and-exceptions.md).

## Execute a feedback cycle

When the user requests independent agents, assign bounded forward, reverse/skeptic and advisor work with disjoint owned files. Match the requested team size and models when available. Preserve independent derivations before exchanging results; repeated execution of one implementation is not independent reconstruction.

For each goal, freeze Loop 1 acceptance tests before new production runs. Record the evidence, objection and advisor decision. Select Loop 2 from that decision; do not execute a previously assumed second loop and retrospectively label it feedback. Replan remaining goals after the first goal's pair when requested. Count advisory reviews separately from research loops.

Keep accepted narrow results, rejected conjectures and unresolved parent goals separate. For any new parameter, classify it as a coordinate, observable, regulator, matched coefficient, external driver or action deformation, and give its equation and scope. Retain exceptions only with explicit applicability conditions and a test showing where the general claim fails.

For later rounds, read [the reconstruction and interval lessons](references/reconstruction-lessons.md). They cover physical-state multiplicities, ordered matrix comparison, shared integration variables, continuous certificates and the difference between a convergent operator family and a surviving spectral gap.

For continuation after a release audit, local-stability matching or dynamic calibration, read [the admission and matching lessons](references/admission-and-matching.md). They separate portable scientific inventories, local interaction norms and identifiability from an assumed clock.

## Admit and publish evidence

Require a mathematical argument for completeness or a continuum implication; sampled checks alone cannot supply it. Implement exact or controlled arithmetic where practical, an independent formulation and a genuinely discriminating wrong-model control. A valid symmetry or canceling normalization factor is an exception, not a failed test to conceal.

Bind contracts, actual executed sources, outputs and decisions to the milestone. Rerun calculations to admit a result; a JSON passed field or hash alone is not proof. Preserve insufficient outcomes. For numerical results separate arithmetic error, truncation error, sampling uncertainty and model assumptions.

Publish the reviewed result with its original target, precise supported statement, limits, reproduction command, source reading depth and next missing premise. If milestone pushes are authorized, push at completed gates and verify the remote commit; never force-push to hide divergence. GitHub Pages deployment and remote source publication are distinct outcomes.

In contribution summaries, distinguish a known theorem applied here, a new derivation within the declared model, a conjecture and a proved obstruction. An equation newly written by the team is not evidence of scientific novelty. Cite the closest checked primary source and describe the actual difference before calling a result a discovery; label novelty unverified when that comparison is incomplete. Give accepted results visual prominence together with their applicability and evidence.
