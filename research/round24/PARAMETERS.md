# Parameters and missing-premise ledger

The common physical bookkeeping is fixed positive lattice spacing `a`, reference energy `E_star`, ratio `alpha/E_star`, and `hbar`. Every model states its own dimensionless generator and time. A common energy reference does not identify different Hamiltonians. No constant below is adjusted to make a failed physical conclusion true.

| Symbol | Type and units | Role and obligation |
|---|---|---|
| `q in (0,1)`, fixed `eta in (0,1)` | Dimensionless action-profile parameters in the canonical summable model | `tau_q=eta/[8 b(q)]` is fixed by the profile; `q=1` is not an admitted member. |
| `C`, `z=C eta` | Dimensionless window parameters | `T_q=C(hbar/alpha)(1-q)^(-3)` is the specified physical observation time. Changing `C` changes the window; demodulation changes only a displayed phase. |
| `delta` | Dimensionless operator-norm error | V1 transfers an actually established observable approximation through both full dynamics with loss `8 delta+4 delta^2`. Naming `delta` does not construct the approximation. |
| `r`, preparation/instrument errors, `theta=alpha Delta t/hbar` | Dimensionless statistical and apparatus tolerances | V2 gives conditional confidence bounds for explicit error conventions. Required control accuracy is an unsolved implementation cost. |
| `N_shots` | Positive integer resource | A sufficient concentration budget, not an optimal or necessary sample lower bound. |
| `tau`, `M=7|tau|` | Dimensionless coefficients in the distinct homogeneous S model | Kept within S's frozen interval. They are not canonical `tau_q`, T's `lambda`, or measured masses. |
| `Theta` | Dimensionless duration of a mathematical filter | It specifies a proof operation at fixed S generator `G`; it is not a replacement physical clock or added action. |
| `n`, `rho` | Iteration index and a proved dimensionless contraction factor | W2 column contraction follows from the actual participating spectral support. It does not supply a general bounded-operator multiplier norm or later-generator induction. |
| `lambda in [0,1/100]` | Constant dimensionless magnetic coefficient in the finite T graph | `L=H_lambda/alpha=K+lambda V` and `sigma=alpha t/hbar`. T's finite graph gap cannot be imported into S or the canonical family. |
| `R`, `N_degree` | Dimensionless electric-energy and integer spin-degree cutoffs | Different gauge-compatible regulators with different input/time certificates. A finite-rank full-space approximation has error one at time zero. |
| `epsilon`, `epsilon_R` | Dimensionless true and Ritz ground energies | The respective heat operators require their own ground centering. An error in the centering exponent is not uniformly harmless at arbitrarily late time. |
| Residual and projection errors | Calculated dimensionless diagnostics | They must include all omitted physical channels. A small matrix has no authority to declare its subspace invariant. |

## Permitted exceptions and repairs

V uses three spectral outcomes on an infinite-dimensional space: finite outcomes need not mean finite Hilbert dimension. Its coherent readout measures the specified complex functional; sequential outcomes measure a different functional. The distinction repairs an operational claim, without adding a field.

W retains the actual residual and proves a convergent vacuum response at fixed `G`. A separate separated-shift method test proves norm convergence when its *actual* source has a suitable band and uniformly separated participating gaps. That valid exception does not supply those hypotheses for S.

X separates retained-input finite windows from full-input delayed windows and computes the actual small sector's leakage. A time or input restriction is a changed approximation question, explicitly labeled; it is not a modification of the physical action.

No time-dependent coupling is introduced in this round. Such a future proposal would require a changed Hamiltonian, its work/energy budget and new scale-matching obligations. The physical calibration and four-dimensional continuum construction remain open.
