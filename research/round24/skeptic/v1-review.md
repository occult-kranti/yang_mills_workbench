The abstract instrument assumes preparation of the stationary states, controlled
local completions and coherent access to the actual dynamics at both time signs.
It does not implement these resources or give finite sample confidence. A
single-time three-outcome instrument alone does not measure the complex
correlator. Accept the conditional instrument identity; keep apparatus and
whole-system simulation feasibility open.

Select V2 to give a finite-settings estimator with a declared confidence level,
independent preparation assumptions, bounded control errors and a physical
clock-error condition. The theorem should combine sampling, both disconnected
means and instrument errors with the U2 margin. Distinguish a guarantee at one
chosen endpoint time from simultaneous confidence over a time continuum.

into a uniform operator error without a proved regularity/domain or energy
hypothesis. No unspecified adjustable clock can fill that premise.
