# Newton: analysis, synthesis and hidden causes

Use documented practices, not a simulation of Newton's mind. This is a selected, expandable primary-source corpus, not all of Newton's knowledge. Read [the research reference](references/research.md) before the first project application and when historical or esoteric claims affect a choice.

## Working procedure

1. **Name the phenomenon and model.** Write the actual observation or target functional, units, state, domain, support and quantifiers. Separate measured facts, inherited theorems, assumptions and imaginative proposals.
2. **Analyze backward.** Reconstruct a candidate from the observable, symmetry, scaling and invariant structure. State what the observations cannot identify. Never infer a lower bound from an unsuccessful upper bound.
3. **Synthesize forward.** Derive an observable consequence with full hypotheses and compare it with the original target. A derivative is not a finite-time estimate; a geometric example is not an infinite-system theorem.
4. **Keep alternate representations.** Translate between geometry, algebra, derivatives/integrals and computational checks. Mark modern reconstructions as ours. State where series converge, which limits commute, and whether support grows.
5. **Keep a hidden-cause notebook.** Alchemical or theological analogies may suggest transformations, cycles, latent variables or unifying questions. Record the original term, historical context, proposed modern meaning and a discriminating prediction. Without a testable translation, retain interpretation rather than adding a term to the Hamiltonian.
6. **Try to break the synthesis.** Change a boundary condition, remove a claimed necessary mechanism, test a degenerate case, and reconstruct the result from a second formulation. Disclose correlated authorship. Follow an explicit user ban on subagents.
7. **Admit the established scope.** Separate exact identities, conditional theorems, numerical enclosures, historical assertions and conjectures. Save a source-bound report and next missing premise. Update guidance prospectively; preserve frozen instructions.

For finite measurements or transferring a correlation witness, read [observable inference and finite readout](references/observable-inference.md).

## Advisor and skeptic use

As advisor, produce phenomenon → necessary structure → candidate law → predicted observable → acceptance test. As skeptic, challenge inverse inference, sufficiency of causes, hidden fitting, singular limits and unsupported universality. Same-author self-review is not independent review.

In Yang–Mills, require an actual gauge-invariant observable, fixed physical scales and the named model. Demand a complete remainder after a resonant leading term. Use modern operator theory for modern claims: Newton's authority, theological unity and alchemical transformation supply no mass-gap or continuum premise.

## Historical controls

Distinguish Newton's experiment, copied recipe, draft, published proposition and later edition. Do not collapse alchemy, theology, prophecy and astrology into one category or diagnose historical psychology. Retain disagreements and failures. Verify surprising quotations against primary text; never manufacture a quotation.
