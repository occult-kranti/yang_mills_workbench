# Next roadmap after the three-goal review

The original goals remain identifiable. A asks for a complete interval in the declared finite deformed measure. B asks for a larger graph with its actual Wilson action and independent verification. C asks for a numerical volume-uniform physical Hamiltonian stability threshold. Completing six research loops does not automatically complete all three mathematical goals.

## Priority1: turn finite-volume estimates into a genuinely local estimate

Forward: derive a local decomposition for the exact SU(2) electric-plus-Wilson Hamiltonian, including Gauss constraints, interaction range, relative form bounds and boundary terms. Recover the applicable full stability proof through an accessible author or repository version, and propagate its constants without selecting a numerical value for an existential constant.

Backward: begin with a positive threshold independent of the number of plaquettes. Trace every norm/combinatorial inequality back to a source-specific lemma. Hold a common physical energy normalization with `alpha>=alpha_min>0`: even zero magnetic coupling has a vanishing physical free gap if alpha is allowed to tend to zero with volume. The global `sum|lambda_p|` budget deteriorates with volume and cannot supply this goal. A plot of a finite bound is not a replacement for the required volume-independent estimate.

Two proposed loops: first reproduce the applicable local proof with explicit tracked constants or document the first unevaluated lemma; second attack exactly that lemma with a bounded combinatorial or operator estimate. Accept only a threshold for the declared physical generator and boundary assumptions. If access or analysis is incomplete, report the named obstruction. Do not count a finite-graph side theorem as completion.

## Priority2: audit the first graph extension beyond a single closed surface

Forward: specify two adjacent cubes with the shared face included once in the Wilson action; retain actual vertex/edge/face lists and link orientations. The full two-cube complex has12 vertices,20 links and11 plaquettes, with four shared-face edges incident on three plaquettes. Its10-face outer boundary alone remains a sphere; it is a different action. Derive the required Haar intertwiner contractions for the full11-face action. The all11 fundamental-trace product is a proposed zero reference because each shared edge has odd center parity. Verify that reference and a genuinely new admissible coefficient before any full integral.

Backward: the single-surface `d^-4` gluing formula is no longer an automatic oracle when a shared link has higher incidence. Start from explicit Haar matrix integrals and require the forward action to match. Use an open planar patch as a factorized reference, a single cube as the existing exact reference and the two-cube graph as the new target.

Two proposed loops: first validate incidence, gauge covariance and the first genuinely new shared-link contraction; second enclose one selected nonzero-coupling observable, with a declared exact error budget and normalization. A finite Taylor-degree or graph cap reports insufficient when reached. No infinite-volume conclusion follows from this extension alone.

## Priority3: refine a physical spectral inequality without guessing the vacuum

Forward: enlarge the finite variational trial set on the existing cube only when its Haar Gram matrix and Hamiltonian entries can be derived exactly. Include higher representations or multi-loop vectors and account for linear dependence. This provides an upper bound on ground energy, not a lower bound on the full spectral gap by itself.

Backward: pair the variational result with a separately proved lower bound on the second eigenvalue of the full gauge-invariant operator. Check the generator normalization, all trial-state norms and repeated Haar moments. Test the Gibbs-square-root state directly through `H psi/psi`; a shared trace coordinate or coupling symbol does not identify two states.

Two proposed loops: first find the bottleneck between the ground upper bound and excited lower bound; second improve that bottleneck with an explicitly valid complementary-subspace or local estimate. Compare against the existing finite bound at the failed endpoint, retain cases where the new estimate is worse, and expose all volume dependence.

## Continuum obligations retained

A nontrivial four-dimensional pure Yang–Mills continuum construction, reconstruction axioms, physical vacuum and time generator, matched units, a controlled volume/spacing limit and a common positive limiting spectral threshold remain separate obligations. The exact point/interval integrals and physical finite-graph inequalities do not supply those obligations. Added fields, masses or scale functions change the contract unless a derivation establishes their equivalence to the target theory.

These are bounded next research contracts, not background jobs or promises that all possible approaches can be exhausted.
