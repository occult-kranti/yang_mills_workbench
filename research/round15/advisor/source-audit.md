# Source audit: which constant is still missing?

Checked9 September2026. The source search is targeted to the three stated goals. It is not an exhaustive review of every Yang–Mills paper. Original project derivations are separated from imported theorems.

## Original spectral goal retained

Yarotsky's Theorems1–3 cover possibly infinite-dimensional site spaces with a unique onsite vacuum and a uniform onsite gap, perturbed by sufficiently small bounded finite-range interactions. Theorem1 gives a spectral estimate involving range-dependent constants `c1,c2`. Section2 explicitly reuses unspecified constants in its expansion summary and refers to the earlier journal proof. No numerical values for the workbench's admissibility threshold were obtained here. [Primary preprint, statements and Section2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math-ph/0411042).

The original journal record was located again through its DOI and publisher page. The inspected web access failed after a redirect to the publisher's minimal-content host. Targeted title/DOI searches did not provide an accessible author manuscript of that complete proof. This is a retrieval limit, not a theorem that explicit constants cannot be extracted. [Original journal record](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1705718).

A later primary paper states a strong local-perturbation result, including infinite-dimensional onsite spaces, but its displayed constants also remain existential. Its conclusion can allow a locally supported perturbation to close the global gap. Consequently, importing that local stability estimate cannot supply the missing numerical global-gap threshold. The reading covered the setup, Theorem3, boundary note and the cited Yarotsky theorem. [Henheik–Teufel–Wessel, version3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.13780).

**Advisor decision:** the original volume-uniform numerical Hamiltonian threshold remains open. The finite-graph norm/variational work in goalC is an explicitly labelled side result and a quantitative test of the missing uniformity. The source constants are not filled in by choosing convenient positive numbers.

## What A and B add

GoalA transfers point certificates to a complete interval in the same finite probability family. Its derivative bound must hold between samples; neither a source citation nor a positive plot supplies that premise.

GoalB changes the graph, so its Haar integration must be derived again. The cube boundary has a closed surface constraint; independent fundamental-index contractions test the character formula. Open planar factorization remains an exact limiting baseline. Orientation is tested at the level of signed link words and gauge transformations, not by treating whole-face inversion as an error in SU(2).

The special-function and representation formulas inherited from round14 retain their original source ledgers and full project derivations. They are not claims of mathematical novelty. Each new graph/series formula will have its own independent derivation and acceptance record.

## Target status

The official target still includes a nontrivial four-dimensional continuum quantum field theory and a positive physical mass gap. The website continues to list the problem as unsolved. Finite graph, finite coupling and exact arithmetic results do not by themselves construct that theory. [Clay Mathematics Institute](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/yang-mills-the-maths-gap/).
