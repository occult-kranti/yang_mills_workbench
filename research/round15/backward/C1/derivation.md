# C1: finite physical graph, original uniform target retained

Let G be a finite connected simple graph with at least one cycle and girth g. Assign L2(SU(2)) to each unoriented edge and impose SU(2) Gauss invariance at every vertex, with no external charges. On this physical Hilbert space define H0=alpha sum_e C_e with alpha>0, Casimir j(j+1), and the normalized constant Haar vacuum Omega.

The Peter–Weyl spin-network basis diagonalizes H0. A nontrivial representation on exactly one incident edge at a vertex cannot admit an invariant intertwiner. Therefore the active support of any nonvacuum basis element has no degree-one vertex. Every nonempty finite graph of minimum degree at least two contains a cycle. Its active support consequently has at least g edges, each carrying j>=1/2 and Casimir at least3/4. Hence every nonvacuum physical energy is at least deltaG=3 alpha g/4. A fundamental trace around a shortest simple cycle is gauge invariant, orthogonal to constants, nonzero and has exactly that energy. This proves the exact free physical gap, without cutting off representations.

A connected forest has only the constant physical state: remove a leaf and its necessarily trivial incident representation repeatedly. Its physical excitation sector is empty. Do not substitute an infinite girth into the numerical formula or call a fabricated finite number its excitation gap. The simple-graph and no-charge premises also matter; loops, parallel-edge cycles and external charges change the admissible active supports.

Let W=-sum_p lambda_p x_p for declared loops that each use distinct links, with x_p=Tr U_p/2. Each x_p is bounded by1 and has zero product-Haar mean, so ||W||<=L=sum_p |lambda_p| and <Omega,H Omega>=0. On the finite compact configuration manifold the electric Laplacian has compact resolvent, preserved by a bounded potential and restriction to the invariant sector. Order physical eigenvalues E0<=E1<=... with multiplicity. The min-max principle gives E1(H)>=deltaG-L, while the Haar trial gives E0(H)<=0. Consequently

gap(H)=E1-E0 >= deltaG-L.

When this lower bound is positive it also excludes a degenerate ground level. If it is zero or negative, the estimate is insufficient; it does not prove gap closure. For the cube g=4 and six common signed coefficients lambda, the bound is3 alpha-6|lambda|. It is positive below |lambda|/alpha=1/2 and inconclusive at equality.

An open cubic box with n vertices in each direction has3n(n-1)^2 elementary square faces. The same norm bound is3 alpha-3n(n-1)^2 |lambda|, whose sufficient per-face threshold tends to zero with n. This is an explicit volume-dependent estimate, not the requested volume-uniform threshold.
