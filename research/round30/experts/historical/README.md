## Actual historical reading

**Newton: Hermetic source.** Rechecked [Keynes MS.28, ALCH00017](https://newton.dlib.indiana.edu/text/ALCH00017/normalized), the English transcription on ff.2r–2v and manuscript metadata. The normalized text identifies a translation of the Emerald Tablet; Newton did not originate that ancient text. The Latin commentary was visible but not fully translated or independently collated against the manuscript. The above/below analogy may motivate a map; it proves no equivalence between scales or theories. This is substantially duplicative of the installed Newton skill's selected corpus.

**Newton: experimental notebook.** Rechecked [Add.3973, ALCH00109](https://newton.dlib.indiana.edu/text/ALCH00109/normalized), ff.1r–2r (December 1678) and ff.5r–6r (January 1679/80). The latter extends the skill's opening-entry reading: the note distinguishes measured from estimated dry weights and records a cracked vessel and escaped material. The relevant practice is complete quantitative records, including unsuccessful operations. No recipe is proposed for recreation, and no transmutation or modern quantum interpretation is inferred.

**Tesla: patent mechanism.** Rechecked [US787412A](https://patents.google.com/patent/US787412A/en), granted 18 April 1905, description of terrestrial standing waves, three stated resonance requirements and receiving apparatus. The text proposes a diameter/quarter-wave relation and an approximate lowest rate of six per second; it also describes null receiver positions. Those are this patent's statements, not independently certified terrestrial performance or the later Earth–ionosphere shell model. This duplicates the skill's main historical source but adds explicit attention to its receiver-location and nodal control.

**Tesla and Vedanta: reported expectation.** Read the [13 February 1896 letter from Vivekananda to E. T. Sturdy](https://www.ramakrishnavivekananda.info/vivekananda/volume_5/epistles_first_series/057_blessed_and_beloved.htm), including the Tesla paragraph and the surrounding cosmological interpretation. It reports interest and an expected mathematical demonstration; the demonstration is absent. The source is Vivekananda's letter in a later compiled transcription, not Tesla's own mathematical paper. Vedanta is identified as its own philosophical/religious tradition. This source is duplicated from the skill corpus.

**Jung: occult investigation and interpretation.** Read the title/edition, author's second-edition preface and Chapter I's “Automatisms” discussion, especially pp.50–54, in [Collected Papers on Analytical Psychology](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/48225/48225-h/48225-h), edited by Constance E. Long, authorized translation, second edition reprinted 1920. The first essay derives from Jung's early occult study. He discusses cueing and motor responses; his historical explanatory language is not adopted as current clinical science. The preface separately warns against confusing a subject's numerical associations with the author's explanation. These passages motivate control of researcher influence and attribution, not a consciousness variable in Yang–Mills. This is a newly selected passage in this review rather than a completed review of Jung's later synchronicity writings.

**Pauli: archive boundary.** Read the [CERN collection guide](https://scientific-info.cern/archives/Pauli_archive/guide/letters), whose page records its last modification as 19 March 2024. It distinguishes the CERN collection from the Pauli–Jung correspondence held at ETH. Search metadata identifies [Letter 1417, 3 June 1952](https://cds.cern.ch/record/85256) as a Pauli-to-Fierz letter discussing Jung; it is not a letter addressed to Jung. The document page returned an anti-bot interstitial. The letter itself is unread in this pass. A secondary quotation or archive catalogue cannot replace that primary text.
