# Post-A advisory review: bounded next-loop recommendations

This is a planning review of candidate-b.md, candidate-c1.md and candidate-c2.md. No B or C experiment has been executed here.

**What A changed.** The useful improvement was a proved conditional zero expectation in the actual dressed reference, not an inserted adjustable coefficient. All-n support geometry, retained electric links and a common physical scale were essential. The decaying full-support exception leaves homogeneous dense stability open. Boundary-dependent cluster completion also prevents assuming that every finite Hamiltonian is an exact restriction of one infinite operator. The failed limit-overshoot test illustrates why “not established” must not be promoted to “disproved.” These become acceptance conditions for the next loops.

**B1 should establish the full complement and cross operator before B2.** The proposed threshold 9α/2 is mathematically plausible: less energy permits at most five nontrivial links; Gauss law excludes degree-one support, bipartiteness excludes odd cycles, and a support with that few edges must be a four-cycle. A connected component with more than one independent cycle needs a separate exclusion under these edge and girth constraints. Enumerate all actual four-cycles and match all eleven faces; degree-two invariance fixes a common representation and a unique fundamental loop state. A fundamental six-edge rectangle then gives a sharp omitted-state control. The proof concerns the complete spin-network basis and the quadratic form domain, not a finite representation truncation.

For the cross Gram, repeated fourth moments are indispensable. In ordinary character normalization, the needed entries include Eχ_p⁴=2 and Eχ_p²χ_q²=1 for p≠q; the latter needs actual exclusive-link integration. Distinct four-face terms vanish only after excluding every four-face closed parity surface on the actual graph. Subtract the P projection explicitly: the vacuum projection contributes λ_pλ_q/4. These give the proposed diagonal Σλ²/4 and off-diagonal λ_pλ_q/4, with the vacuum row zero. A bare independent-face model and omission of this projection are discriminating controls.

**B2 can use the signed eleven-dimensional coefficient box.** On the codimension-one form subspace Ω-perpendicular, the P component has no vacuum coordinate and its exact Hamiltonian form is 3α times its norm squared. The complete Q threshold and bounded cross Gram then give the proposed scalar two-by-two lower form. This is a valid min–max lower bound on E1 even though Ω is not the interacting ground. Pair it with the separate vacuum E0 upper bound; never subtract two Ritz levels. For |λ_p|≤3α/8 the conservative matrix using c=3/8 and b=3√21/16 is positive, with lower eigenvalue α(27−3√70)/16. The improved endpoint trial upper bound is applicable only when all eleven endpoint magnitudes are present. A common α_min is still needed if describing a common physical family. Retain sign flips, zero couplings, absent complement premises and incorrect mixed-moment controls.

**C1 should first certify admissible coordinates, including singular cases.** A real positive-semidefinite joint Gram of b,a1,…,a4 with rank at most four and unit a_i is realizable in R⁴. The declared b relation must be checked through both its cross inner products and squared norm. Equal complete Grams induce a span isometry, extended to O(4); sphere invariance is sufficient even when that reflection is not an SU(2) group automorphism. This applies to the declared dot-product observable and action, not orientation-sensitive additions or an independently transformed surrounding measure. Exact PSD/rank/type validation must precede caches. Rank one/two, dependent vectors and b=0 with nonzero coefficients are valid cases, not singular inverses to regularize away. The proposed sphere recurrence agrees with the standard pairing denominator; independent coordinate expansion can challenge it.

**C2 should then integrate exactly the two actual link variables and all six variable weights.** The face union and signed word reduction must be established before introducing x,y,w. Their joint moment is not a product of three scalar trace moments: E[xyw]=1/16 is a useful discriminator. The proposed angular representation and character convolution are genuinely different verification routes and avoid division by vanishing angular factors. With all other links fixed, the fourteen constant actions cancel only from this normalized conditional expectation, not from a future surrounding marginal. At κ=0, integrating V annihilates the shared adjoint, while freezing V changes the model and gives 1/27. The proposed quadratic numerator coefficient 5/648 is consistent with keeping both the w² and 4y² terms; discarding the two V-only weights changes that coefficient. Fix the nonzero κ, degree sequence and accepted tail width before evaluating, retain coarse failures and compare the full six-weight action against the missing-weight model. Generic b(V) must use the newly admitted Gram method or an equally complete integration, not the old axis-only routine.
