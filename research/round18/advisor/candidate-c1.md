# Unexecuted C1 candidate

For normalized q on S3, the declared observable family depends only on q·b and q·a_i, i=1..4. The candidate complete coordinate is the joint Gram of b,a1,...,a4. Equal Grams determine an isometry between their spans even with linear dependence; extend it to O(4) and use sphere invariance. This does not transform the surrounding-link measure or establish sufficiency for undeclared orientation-sensitive observables.

Validate a real symmetric positive-semidefinite Gram, rank at most four, unit a_i, and b=Σκ_i a_i through the Gram constraints G_0i=Σκ_jG_ji and G_00=κ^T G_aa κ. Rank deficiency and b=0 with nonzero κ are valid. Do not invert a singular Gram. Negative principal minors, rank five, mismatched norms, and a forged b relation must reject before any cache.

A proposed exact moment algorithm picks i with k_i>0 and uses

    M(k)=sum_j (k_j-delta_ij) G_ij M(k-e_i-e_j)/(4+|k|-2),

for even total degree; M(0)=1, odd moments vanish. The independent method can use explicit sphere-coordinate polynomial expansion with rational fixtures, or complete Wick pairings without sharing this recurrence. Validate nested exact types and keep immutable cache keys and return values. Reuse rank-one, tetrahedral and equal-action/different-Gram counterexamples; add a valid rank-two realization and a common orthogonal reflection. A small complete weighted Taylor enclosure can check the full normalized observable rather than just its partition. No executable acceptance is claimed in this note.
