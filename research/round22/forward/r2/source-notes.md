# R2 targeted form-theorem reading

Read [Teschl, Mathematical Methods in Quantum Mechanics](https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~gerald/ftp/book-schroe/schroe.pdf),
online PDF metadata version 12 February 2009, GSM99: Section2.3,
Theorem2.13 and proof, printed pp.71–72; Section6.5, relative-form definition,
Theorem6.24 (KLMN) and proof, printed pp.149–150.

The representation theorem requires a densely defined closed semibounded
form and characterizes its operator domain by a Hilbert-vector form pairing.
KLMN requires a Hermitian perturbation form with relative bound strictly
below one. R2 verifies those hypotheses using its absolutely convergent
actual-star form and kappa=28|tau|. These theorems identify the form domain;
they do not identify it with the operator domain. R2's response membership
in D(H0) has a separate coefficient estimate.

The following Theorem6.25 discussion was inspected but is not imported;
the sandwiched inverse and its plus sign are derived directly from the
form equation. No source coupling threshold or later-stage theorem is used.
This is targeted text reading, not a full-book or novelty audit. R1's
interacting-inverse source comparison remains inherited; scientific priority
is unverified.
