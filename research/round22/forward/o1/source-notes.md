# O1 targeted primary reading

Read on 2026-09-14, independently of current O1 other-producer solutions.

* [Yarotsky, math-ph/0411042v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math-ph/0411042),
  11 November 2004: definitions and Theorems 1–3, pp. 2–4; Section 2,
  pp. 6–8, especially Lemma 1, equation (8) and its fixed-point explanation.
  Possibly infinite site spaces, unbounded nonnegative onsite operators,
  unit reference gap and bounded finite-range interactions match the inherited
  I1 geometry. The fixed-point estimate is stated with sufficiently small
  interaction and refers to earlier proofs; it does not evaluate the
  range-dependent c1,c2. O1 does not replace these with its remainder constant.

* [Yarotsky, math-ph/0412040v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math-ph/0412040),
  December 2004: Theorems 1–2 and the relative/bounded split, pp. 3–4;
  proof of Theorem 1, pp. 6–10, including Lemmas 1–4 and the polymer
  counting step. The proof treats local perturbations via analytic semigroup
  bounds and connected configurations; the spatial counting constant remains
  unspecified. The purely relative part annihilates the reference vacuum.
  O1's R fails that condition, while its generated interaction is no longer
  of the original fixed finite range. No numerical application is claimed.

* [Block-diagonalization of infinite-volume lattice Hamiltonians with
  unbounded interactions, arXiv:2108.13907](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.13907),
  retrieved 54-page PDF: targeted reading of Lemma 2.8 and its complete
  proof, equations (2.45)–(2.49), pp. 10–11; Theorem 2.10, p. 11;
  Theorem 3.3 and its final proof, p. 35; and the assumptions of Lemma 4.1.
  The domain argument separates the support from commuting exterior energy
  and relies on a prior weighted-generator estimate. The spectral conclusion
  depends on the intervening induction and inverse-gap hypotheses, with an
  unevaluated t_d. That full induction was not read or reproduced here.
  O1 proves its stronger operator-domain preservation for the special bounded
  finite-rank local generators directly; no source numerical threshold is used.
