# Round20 team and protocol

The user authorized paired forward/reverse research, advisor feedback, skill updates and milestone publication. The work used a lead skeptic/advisor, two research agents with disjoint scientific directories, a separate final mathematical audit agent, and a separate presentation agent. The root agent owns integration, skills and GitHub publication. These are AI-agent reviews and exact computational checks, not independent human peer review or formal proof-assistant certification.

| Role | Owned work | Independence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Forward researcher | `forward/<loop>/` | Derives consequences from the frozen contract; freezes its own report, code and outputs before result comparison |
| Reverse researcher | `reverse/<loop>/` | Reconstructs sufficient premises from the target; uses separate ledgers, graph construction, Haar/Lie calculations or spectral argument |
| Lead skeptic/advisor | `contracts/`, `advisor/`, scientific site data | Selects the next loop from admitted evidence, compares outputs, reviews proof premises and binds source bytes |
| Final mathematical auditor | `external-audit/` | Reads completed proof routes and writes a separate exact checker without importing producer/advisor implementations |
| Presentation agent | `build_site.py`, route tests and site assets | Displays gate-verified claims and limitations; presentation edits do not rewrite admitted scientific sources |
| Root integrator | Publication, shared skill, global project pages | Verifies deliverables and publishes/merges reviewed milestones |

The two directions share the mathematical contract, inherited accepted premises and clearly attributed advisor objections. They are separate formulations, not a claim of statistically independent discoveries. In H2 the external auditor proposed a variance route, then a two-free-link identity; both directions independently reconstructed the geometry, Haar integration and spectral consequences. The proposal's common origin is recorded in the H2 decision rather than hidden behind matching output.

The first three goals D, E and F were selected from actual Round19 missing premises. G and H remained deferred until the six D1–F2 gates existed; `advisor/roadmap-after-first-three.md` and `execution.json` record that decision and its F2 gate hash. Every Loop2 contract depends on the preceding Loop1's actual gate. Ordinary and optimized runs of the same source count as one implementation; two implementations plus comparison count as one research loop. Closing inherited C2 is separate from the ten Round20 loops.

Each gate hashes the contract, actual producer sources, reports, outputs and comparison/decision files. The replay driver verifies these bytes and regenerates scientific results. Hashes show identity of reviewed evidence, not correctness of a theorem. Written proofs handle general statements; exact finite fixtures expose implementation or premise failures but do not replace a completeness argument. Later presentation updates remain outside accepted scientific gate inventories.

The organizing picture is two opposed Fibonacci/golden spirals. Their expansion nodes pair forward derivation and reverse reconstruction at a common declared physical scale. The geometry contributes no action coefficient, physical constant, observed law or independent experimental evidence.

Method snapshots for future continuation are `paired-physics-research.md` and `reconstruction-lessons.md` in this directory. The inherited history remains in `research/round19/methods/`. The final skeptic roadmap states the next missing premises; none of its prospective loops are counted as completed research.
