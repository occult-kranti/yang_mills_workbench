## Continuum work remains a separate construction problem

Before a continuum loop, freeze a genuine lattice-spacing family a, gauge group, spacetime dimension, state, observable renormalization, boundary limit and positive physical reference energy. The original convention alpha(a)=g_H(a)^2/(2a) and lambda(a)=2/[g_H(a)^2a] gives lambda/alpha=4/g_H^4. The weak-bare-coupling direction leaves the known fixed small-ratio stability regime; that is failure of the current estimate, not a proof that the continuum theory fails.

A useful first task is an explicit map from a controlled lattice observable family to a candidate nontrivial continuum observable with a uniform error budget. Its second loop must come from that result: test reconstruction/positivity and physical spectral control, or retain the exact missing premise. Neither q nor c nor the Fibonacci display coordinate may replace a. A mass scale or extra field needs a controlled elimination or return limit before it contributes to pure Yang–Mills.

The forward researcher's additional observation that V_q may converge strongly on the dense local-excitation core could be checked as a short H2 corollary later. It is not part of the ten-loop admission, and it would not by itself prove norm-resolvent convergence or fill the continuum gap.
