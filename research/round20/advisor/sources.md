# Sources and reading depth

- Gerald Teschl, [Mathematical Methods in Quantum Mechanics](https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~gerald/ftp/book-schroe/schroe.pdf), online 2009 edition, sections 6.1 and 6.6: targeted section reading for bounded perturbations, same-space resolvents and spectral projection convergence. These are established tools, not research novelty. Round20 supplies its own model-specific tail, support and error arguments.
- Arthur Jaffe and Edward Witten, [Quantum Yang–Mills Theory](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/yangmills.pdf): official problem statement, targeted scope reading. A lattice product-sector bound is short of construction of a nontrivial continuum theory on R^4 with the required axiomatic properties.
- Gerald Teschl, Yifei Wang, Bing Xie and Zhe Zhou, [On Generalized Strong and Norm Resolvent Convergence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10476), 2026: abstract inspected only; current related context, not used as an unreviewed theorem premise.

Accessed 2026-09-14. No literature-priority claim is made for our model-specific applications or symbolic formulas.
