The current finite target is one explicitly deformed `SU(2)^2` Euclidean measure. ## Common contract carried through every experiment

Each proposed bridge must identify gauge group, geometry, action, measure or state, observable algebra, time generator, units, regulators and limiting order. A shared symbol is insufficient. A numerical degree `N` bounds a polynomial remainder; it is not lattice spacing. The extra trace `z` is an existing coordinate; the coefficient `eta` changes the finite action. If extra fields are introduced later, their elimination or removal must be an explicit theorem.

The advisor–skeptic owns the contract and rejection gates. The forward role derives from the accepted measure or operator. The backward role lists sufficient premises for the selected target and independently checks the forward output. Keep exactly these three roles when continuing this protocol; do not count an author's rerun as an independent review.

## Next loop A: a complete same-action parameter interval

**Hypothesis:** the covariance is positive throughout a specified compact positive-deformation interval at fixed `k1=k2=1`, for example `[1/8,1/4]`.

**Forward steps:** derive a parameter-interval version of the Taylor polynomial and remainder; retain coefficient dependence instead of sampling. Use rational interval coefficients, or combine exact point certificates with a proved bound on `|C'|`. A conservative universal bound follows from the centered third cumulant: `C'=E[delta x delta y delta z]`, so `|C'|<=2` by bounded `delta z` and Cauchy–Schwarz. Every covered cell needs a positive lower bound after its full Lipschitz transport error.

**Backward steps:** demand complete coverage of the declared closed interval with exact rational cell endpoints, no gaps, a valid same-family derivative bound and positive margin in every cell. Independent verification reconstructs the entire cover and all margins. Pointwise samples alone fail the gate.

**Acceptance:** a finite exact cover whose union equals the requested interval and whose lower covariance bound stays positive. **Rejection:** negative lower margin, uncovered boundary, parameter/state mismatch, dropped normalization error or missing derivative proof. **Outcome if blocked:** report the failing cell and tighten the target or method explicitly; do not call a narrower interval the original result.

This is a bounded next calculation, not a promise of global sign for all real triples. It does not establish long-distance decay.
