# Source audit and the outer spectral route

The finite-loop calculation and the continuum spectral target require different evidence. This audit checks whether available rigorous lattice results supply a missing premise; it does not use a source's prestige or the number of citations as an acceptance criterion.

## Current target

The official Clay page continues to classify the four-dimensional Yang–Mills existence and mass-gap problem as unsolved. The target includes constructing the required quantum field theory, not merely finding a positive number in a finite matrix. [Clay Mathematics Institute](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/yang-mills-the-maths-gap/).

## The unevaluated Hamiltonian threshold

Yarotsky's Theorems 1–3 provide the qualitative perturbative result used in round13. The theorem states range-dependent constants; the following proof overview deliberately reuses symbols for unspecified constants and sends details to the earlier journal paper. This inspection does not yield an evaluated threshold for the workbench. The original publisher record was located, but its redirected minimal page did not expose the theorem text in this access path. That is an access limitation, not evidence that constants can never be extracted. [Primary preprint, Theorems 1–3 and Section 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math-ph/0411042); [original journal record](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1705718).

The related relatively bounded perturbation theorem allows a bounded remainder controlled by a power of the relative-bound margin. Its prefactor is again existential in the inspected statement, and the action, form bounds and periodic-volume convention must be matched separately. Introducing that prefactor as a fitted variable would not make it a proved admissibility constant. [Primary related theorem, Theorems 1–2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math-ph/0412040).

**Decision:** retain explicit constant extraction as an open, high-priority spectral task. The two requested executable loops target the finite integral's total error instead. They supply independently testable mathematics without pretending that an unknown stability constant has been evaluated.

## An explicit threshold for a different generator

Shen–Zhu–Zhu give explicit strong-coupling conditions and functional inequalities for lattice Yang–Mills measures and their Langevin dynamics. Their paper separately treats spatial correlation decay. These are useful rigorous reference results. The displayed Poincaré constant is tied to the Langevin Dirichlet form; it is not automatically the spectral gap of the workbench's electric-plus-magnetic Hamiltonian. The state, generator, normalization and continuum limits require their own matching argument. [Primary preprint, Assumption 1.1 and Theorems 1.2–1.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2204.12737).

**Decision:** use these results as a distinct Euclidean and stochastic branch in the graph. Do not substitute their numerical parameter threshold for Yarotsky's unevaluated Hamiltonian threshold. The mixed-loop term in this round is also an explicit finite-action change; its inclusion is not covered just by quoting a theorem for a different Wilson action.
