## Relevant contemporary comparisons

- [Chevyrev–Shen, gauge-covariant renormalisation in stochastic 3D Yang–Mills–Higgs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.03060): the Penrose report inspects the current version's theorem and explicit open construction/measure limits. Three-dimensional stochastic-flow results do not construct the target four-dimensional pure theory.
- [Bonanno et al., SU(N) scale setting, topology and volume independence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07355): Tesla inspects the flow-scale convention and separate finite-spacing, topology and volume systematics. These numerical scale conventions cannot calibrate the workbench's canonical alpha without a mathematical map.
- [Feynman's qualitative 2+1-dimensional Yang-Mills study](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/t10r3-s2322): the dimension and qualitative scope are explicit. The historical author's authority does not close the 3+1-dimensional quantum problem.
- [Nachtergaele–Sims–Young, quasi-locality bounds, Part I](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.02428): advisor checked abstract and bibliographic scope as a methodological comparison, not all proofs or a new theorem premise. It explicitly treats infinite onsite Hilbert spaces/unbounded onsite contributions and distinctions between strong and norm topologies. The workbench must still prove its own declared support-cardinality estimates.

Recent preprints can contain errors or overbroad claims. The individual reports retain screened caveats rather than silently importing questionable assertions. No claimed solution located in a search is accepted as a resolution of the Clay problem.

## Official target

The advisor and skeptic checked the [official Clay status](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/yang-mills-the-maths-gap/) and [Jaffe–Witten problem statement](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/yangmills.pdf), especially sections 3–5. The target includes a nontrivial theory on four-dimensional space, the required axiomatic properties and a strictly positive physical mass gap for compact simple gauge groups. It remains unresolved. The proof obligations are dependent, not equal-weight points in a percentage score.

Historical and occult sources contribute questions and methodological warnings in this round. They supply no new force, measured frequency, Hamiltonian term or mass-gap premise. Scientific priority of the workbench's model-specific contributions remains unverified.
