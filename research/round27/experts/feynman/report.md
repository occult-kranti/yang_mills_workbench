# Feynman-method source review and recommendation

Date: 2026-09-22. Role: historical-method research lens and separately authored reverse worker, not Richard Feynman, a professional endorsement, or external peer review. The source ledger records exactly what was inspected. No new physics was executed before the AI1 contract freeze.

## What the historical sources actually support

Feynman's 1974 **Cargo Cult Science** discusses his own investigation of mysticism, isolation-tank experiences and purported psychic demonstrations. The same primary address asks researchers to disclose alternative explanations and contrary evidence, replicate controls, and seek predictions beyond observations used to formulate a theory. These passages support exploring an unusual claim with controls; they do not establish paranormal mechanisms or a bridge to Yang–Mills. The modern inference used here is to require an actual countermodel for an attractive equation. [F1]

His 1948 path-integral paper formulates nonrelativistic quantum mechanics through complex amplitudes and action phases, with equivalence to the Schrödinger formulation. For this workbench the relevant distinction is between an observable scalar and the complete underlying dynamics. The repository's endpoint correlations cannot be promoted to a global Hamiltonian equivalence just because two scalar formulas coincide. That application is our methodological inference, not a claim in the 1948 paper. Only the primary abstract was successfully retrieved; the attached PDF fetch failed. [F2]

The 1982 quantum-simulation article's Caltech record and publisher metadata were verified. Full text was not available through those endpoints in this search. No specific technical argument from unread full text is claimed here. [F3]

The directly relevant 1981 **The Qualitative Behavior of Yang-Mills Theory in 2+1 Dimensions** is unusually useful for keeping scope honest: its primary abstract explicitly describes qualitative/suggestive SU(2) arguments in two spatial dimensions and excludes the 3+1-dimensional case. Its record also acknowledges substantial revision after errors were identified in an earlier manuscript. Thus citing Feynman does not close this workbench's four-dimensional continuum obligations. The full paper was not retrieved; this is an abstract-level comparison. [F4]

## Current primary research that changes the decision

Kane, Hariprakash and Bauer (2025) propose the Statistically-Bounded Time Evolution protocol: keep errors from approximate evolution below the working statistical uncertainty while performing renormalization. This is a direct reason to preserve separate numerical, physical-model and continuum error budgets. It does not prove the workbench's continuum limit. [F5]

Negro and collaborators (June 2026) demonstrate Hamiltonian step scaling for 2+1-dimensional U(1), using a force-based renormalized coupling and keeping the reference-distance/lattice-extent ratio fixed along extrapolation. This supplies a concrete comparison target for a future fixed-physical-scale plan. It is a different gauge group and dimension, not evidence for a four-dimensional SU(2) theorem. [F6]

The September 2026 digitization preprint compares U(1) with finite Z(N) gauge groups in the Hamiltonian limit and finds persistent finite-N differences. Its discussion distinguishes dimensionless ground-state observables from dimensionful scale matching. This directly motivates demanding an independent clock/energy calibration before transferring a scalar endpoint between models. No numerical discrepancy from that paper is transplanted to the SU(2) workbench. [F7]

These are recent, relevant primary comparisons located by a targeted search, not an exhaustive review of all 2026 work. None was used as an unproved premise in AI1.

## Advisor recommendation and executed narrow result

Select AI identifiability before a speculative new mass-gap claim. The inherited AD2 multi-frequency formula is already strong enough to expose missing information. Freeze alpha, eta, q, a physical time label and the actual observables. In a demodulated physical-time surrogate the observable sees only kappa=alpha eta(1-q)^3/hbar. A compensating eta-q family gives identical complete curves. A carrier can recover alpha conditionally, but the pair eta,q remains unresolved. The current AI1 reverse result proves that exact classification, gives rational counterexamples and retains carrier aliases.

The counterexample remains meaningful even if it is disappointing: adding a free parameter cannot repair an inverse problem unless an additional observation separates it. The most useful next candidate is a finite-q ratio of independently specified coherent Wilson sources reaching face weights q^4 and q^5. Its full-system error and source-support obligations must be solved under a new contract. It cannot be declared measured from the q->1 endpoint.

This lens changes strategy by replacing visual resonance or a matching curve with a discriminating prediction and a failed-model control. The novelty is narrow and repository-specific: the classification for these particular endpoint observables. Structural identifiability and countermodels are established general methods; priority for any broader discovery is unverified.

## Completion estimate

There is no defensible scalar percentage of a mathematical proof completed. A finite list of successful subproblems is not a linear measure of distance to the Yang–Mills theorem. Under the concrete question “has this work established the required four-dimensional continuum theory with a positive physical mass gap?”, the answer is no. The repository still lacks its scale-matched continuum construction and its volume/cutoff-uniform physical gap argument. Reporting “0% of the final theorem established” is at most a binary theorem-status convention, not a claim that the research has zero value or an estimate of remaining effort. Prefer a list of unresolved proof obligations and separate counts of completed local studies.
