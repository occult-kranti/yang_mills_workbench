## Decision

The three loops improve the workbench's logical and computational position. They do not determine a percentage of the four-dimensional Yang–Mills problem solved. The most useful next unexecuted priority is a complete accounting and bound for the actual additional homogeneous remainder E, followed by the changed-diagonal and weight-margin conditions needed for a next correction. Improving a canonical readout's enormous observation clock is an interesting secondary research direction, but it does not supply that missing homogeneous premise.

AI2 follows the complete-device principle at the mathematical level. It uses actual Wilson multipliers of norm `2 sqrt(2)`, a newly reconstructed ten-link common seed, complete reference-factor collars, and the inherited component-wide exterior-loading proof. It does not replace the multiplier by a normalized rank surrogate or delete the second evolution column. The successful cell uses `u=10^-18`, collar depth five, 993 complete reference factors, 1,857 links and 1,171 retained omitted faces. The canonical comparison is at the same physical time and same carrier for both hypotheses.

Its qualified success should be preserved exactly: at `u=10^-18`, the q5 probe's disjoint-disk margin exceeds `4.4050e-26`; an added equal absolute scalar error below `2.2e-26` per hypothesis is sufficient to retain separation. The common time is `2e48 hbar/alpha`. This scalar allowance cannot simply be called a timing tolerance, phase tolerance, hardware fidelity or shot count. Those conversions need an instrument, calibration and a full-model sensitivity bound. No measured alpha is supplied. All simple-ratio comparisons remain insufficient, including the successful scalar-disk cell. That failure is valuable evidence about conservative inversion costs, not evidence that the exact q-ratio algebra is false.

The simple first-moment ratio was my recommended next diagnostic after AI1. AI2 appropriately kept its failed finite-error ratios and found the stronger admitted result through direct pairwise scalar comparison. The new evidence therefore changes my assessment from “retained profile information exists” to “one pair of full canonical predictions is certified distinct.” It does not justify “q is now continuously reconstructed from observations.” Increasing the clock further to the tested `u=10^-24` does not help at the frozen collar depths: those cells remain insufficient. Smaller profile defects alone do not guarantee a better full readout certificate.

AG1 addresses the more central homogeneous stability obstruction. All three derivations keep the actual input-source norm in the denominator, rather than dividing by a loose cubic upper bound. The three independent estimates give different valid sufficient constants; those constants should be reported with their own decompositions and assumptions, not averaged as independent measurements. The reported contraction concerns selected mixing, while combined scalar/diagonal/source bookkeeping has additional costs.

## Percentage estimate

**Yang–Mills solved: not estimable as a defensible percentage.** There is no established denominator of equally difficult proof steps, calibrated probability model, or quantitative distance from these fixed-model statements to a nontrivial continuum construction with a positive physical gap. Assigning an arbitrary figure such as 20% or 80% would add unsupported precision. Counting proven lemmas is also unsuitable: a single missing uniform estimate can contain more difficulty than many completed finite calculations.

The defensible execution metric is **3 of 3 requested investigations executed (100% of this continuation's execution plan)**. It is not a mathematical completion percentage. The final Yang–Mills existence-and-gap theorem remains unproved here. Preserved milestones include exact finite-model calculations and the scoped readout and correction results. Open obligations include the complete homogeneous remainder, repeatable changed-diagonal induction, volume/cutoff-uniform matching at a common scale, a nontrivial continuum limit, reconstruction/positivity requirements, and a positive limiting gap.
