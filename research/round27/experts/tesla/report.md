## Current research comparisons

1. **Scale setting:** Bonanno et al., [arXiv:2511.07355v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.07355v1), sections II–III and V, distinguishes the gradient-flow definition, lattice artifacts, topology and volume systematics. Its flow window separates lattice spacing, smoothing radius and physical volume. The transferable lesson is to declare a reference observable and matching convention before converting units. Its SU(N) numerical calibration cannot be copied to our canonical summable alpha without a map.

2. **Measured gauge dynamics:** Froland and Grabowska, [arXiv:2606.14842v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.14842v1), sections II, III.2, V and VI, studies a digitized two-plaquette SU(2) system and reports IBM-device measurements with an explicit estimator and error mitigation. Observable accuracy, digitization and state complexity have different resource demands. This is a relevant comparison for specifying our apparatus and noise budget; it is not an implementation of AD2 or a continuum proof. Its named U.S. government support was traced to the official [DOE Quantum Horizons program](https://science.osti.gov/np/Research/Quantum-Information-Science), whose remit includes quantum simulation and nuclear physics. Program existence is verified; sponsorship is not mathematical validation.

3. **Observable-designed subspaces:** Zhang, [arXiv:2608.25696v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.25696v1), sections IV–V, constructs Wilson-loop subspaces using both Hamiltonian and overlap matrices in a 2+1-dimensional Z2 model. Its finite basis becomes expensive if the required loop size grows extensively. This supports complete Gram and omitted-channel accounting as a promising method. Abelian demonstration and proposed non-Abelian transfer must remain distinct.

4. **Resonance identification:** Koloskov et al., [18 September 2026](https://angeo.copernicus.org/articles/44/949/2026/), introduction and calibration sections, separates driven spectral maxima from cavity eigenvalues and uses multiple field/mode observations to infer source geometry. This is a direct counterexample to assigning every spectral peak to an intrinsic frequency. Its Earth-ionosphere model is not Tesla's patent model and neither is a Yang–Mills mass gap. The displayed web equation (1) defines X and Y identically despite describing different axes; no numerical calibration coefficient from that passage is imported here.

5. **A screened caution:** Bradshaw, [arXiv:2603.26819v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.26819v1), proposes gauge cooling and explicitly limits exact correction when singlet multiplicities are nontrivial. However, the introduction says neighboring vertex constraints do not commute. Standard full-link gauge transformations at distinct vertices commute because shared links carry commuting left and right actions. That sentence is not adopted. The paper is a research lead needing a separate audit, not an accepted premise of this continuation.

These are selected current papers, not an exhaustive state-of-the-art survey or priority search. No external simulation result has been reproduced in this source pass.
