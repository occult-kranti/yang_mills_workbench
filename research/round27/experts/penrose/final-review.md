## AG1 geometry and support audit

The AG1 reference is the homogeneous complete-24-link block model. Its four-factor coarse stars must not be confused with AI2's physical cube graph, the 293-coordinate finite-graph heat approximation, or the evidence-network drawing. The contraction does not move between those models.

The following steps in the inspected forward/reverse derivations address the previously missing one-step support estimate:

1. The complete star difference set has thirteen possible anchors, including the source itself. The twelve interior crossings remain; the origin's three crossings and the no-crossing boundary are separate cases. Each first crossing carries the complete connected seven-factor union.
2. Repeated ordered words and attachments to already generated factors are counted. The forward rooted recurrence retains both possible root locations; the reverse relative-word construction retains the root translations. The union size 4+3n alone would not establish a norm bound, but the reports also supply all-order coefficient sums and convergence ranges.
3. The auxiliary support weight rho=9/4 is explicitly an estimate parameter. Its recurrence ratios are 6561/8000 in the forward majorant and 28431/32000 in the reverse majorant at M=1/1000; both are below one. These differing valid counts need not produce identical upper bounds.
4. Every nested commutator pays a portion of the rho-to-2 weight loss. The support first-moment estimate and the n! bound control the entire BCH remainder. The Pauli-string example correctly shows why arbitrary weight-2 interactions cannot satisfy a support-independent same-weight bilinear commutator estimate.
5. The denominator is the actual indexed input norm 16 c_Lambda r, with 1<=c_Lambda<=4 and r the unevaluated but positive actual local norm. The cubic formula is used only as an upper bound. Small and boundary-deleted cuboids therefore remain covered without substituting 64 times a loose source budget into the denominator. At zero coupling or empty support, no ratio is taken.

This is a uniform statement about the declared interaction decompositions in containing finite volumes. It does not construct a bounded extensive generator S on the infinite-volume Hilbert space, or a new continuum limit.

## Exact update, independent arithmetic and scope

Both producers correctly retain

\[
[S,G]=-A+R,\qquad
e^S(G+A)e^{-S}=G+R+N,
\quad
N=\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{\operatorname{ad}_S^n(nA+R)}{(n+1)!}.
\]

Thus the first nonlinear term is [S,A+R]/2. They establish graph-domain preservation before integrating the unbounded G conjugation and place only bounded commutators inside the subsequent series. Finite matrix checks diagnose this algebra; the physical completeness claims depend on the inherited infinite-dimensional source/domain proofs and the new all-order support estimates.

As a review check, I independently recomputed the displayed forward and reverse endpoint formulas using exact rational arithmetic. The rounded results are:

| Certificate | M=1/1000 | M=1/10000 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Forward BCH convergence ratio upper bound | 5.591999053e-7 | 4.206967988e-12 |
| Forward entire nonlinear remainder / actual input norm | 7.785040508e-5 | 1.109425910e-11 |
| Forward selected total correction ratio majorant | 12.56357360 | 0.639241567 |
| Reverse selected total correction ratio majorant | 20.32406930 | 0.923602618 |

The narrow bounds are below 2/3 and 0.93 respectively. The main-interval total majorants exceed one, despite a small nonlinear error. This means those positive certificates do not prove contraction on the larger interval; it does not mean the actual correction expands. The narrower interval was predeclared in the frozen contract, so its successful refinement is part of AG1, not an extra loop. My arithmetic check is also a review, not another physics loop.

For each generated support, the scalar/mixing/reference-diagonal split is exact. Their separate norm costs are at most one, one and two times the term norm. The new mixing source inherits the narrow contraction, but the combined bookkeeping budget can cost four times as much. The scalar can be extensive and is a reference-vacuum expectation, not the exact interacting ground energy. The reference diagonal distinguishes the vacuum from its complement; it is not diagonal in every excited spectral sector.

Most importantly, A is one selected genuine repeated-anchor cubic source family. If the full transformed Hamiltonian is G+A+E, its output still includes e^S E e^-S. The supplied transport estimate is conditional on a complete stronger-norm bound for E; this loop does not supply that original remainder inventory. No equation in AG1 licenses setting E to zero for the original problem.

The changed diagonal and larger supports also change the next inverse/filter hypotheses. The consumed rho-to-2 margin cannot simply be restored at every iteration. Consequently even the newly proved selected-source contraction is one-step progress, not the previously missing all-stage stability theorem.
