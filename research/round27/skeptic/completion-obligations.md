# What can be said about completion of the Yang–Mills problem?

**A defensible numerical percentage is not identifiable.** The repository establishes useful bounded results and counterexamples, but it has no proved decomposition of the continuum problem into weighted tasks whose completed fraction measures distance to a proof. Inventing weights would manufacture a number. An unknown percentage does not mean the work has no value, and is different from claiming that a known amount of work remains.

The current [Clay status page](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/yang-mills-the-maths-gap/) was read on 22 September 2026 and is marked Unsolved. The [official Jaffe–Witten statement](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/yangmills.pdf), especially section 4, requires a nontrivial four-dimensional quantum Yang–Mills theory with the specified axiomatic strength and a positive mass gap for every compact simple gauge group. It specifies local fields and short-distance agreement with asymptotic freedom. These are mathematical obligations, not benchmark scores.

The following ledger is a workbench assessment against that target, rather than an official decomposition or additive checklist:

| Obligation or necessary bridge | Existing workbench evidence | Missing result | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Original homogeneous dynamics and scale matching | One controlled selected-source nonlinear update and narrow-interval mixing contraction; canonical-family and finite-graph results with declared scales | Convergent changed-diagonal iteration and a proved map on states, domains, observables and generators | Open |
| Bounds that survive growing graph volume and removal of regularization | Exact fixed-graph finite-channel and error controls; selected local uniform bounds | Complete estimates uniform along a matched limiting trajectory, in the required topology | Open |
| Nontrivial four-dimensional limiting theory | Finite or explicitly different summable models | Construction and nontriviality of the requested continuum theory | Open |
| Required axiomatic properties and field identification | Gauge-invariant observables in declared lattice models | Limiting local fields, covariance, positivity/reconstruction and ultraviolet behavior at required strength | Open |
| Positive finite gap in fixed physical units for the constructed theory | Finite-model gaps and conditional endpoint information | Gap lower bound for the actual continuum theory, with matching and limits justified | Open |
| General compact simple gauge group | Workbench emphasis on SU(2) | A proof with the required gauge-group coverage | Open |

Neither proof length nor novelty counts estimate the missing work. Numerical simulation can reject an incorrect finite model or support a bounded estimate without deciding the existence of the required limit. The final three-loop report should separately give the exact number of requested loops executed and their accepted scopes; that operational completion rate is not a percentage of solving the Millennium problem.

This is a separately authored model-agent skeptical assessment, not an estimate elicited from the historical people, external mathematical peer review, or a proof of impossibility. The source hashes and access record are in `inherited-source-review.json`.
