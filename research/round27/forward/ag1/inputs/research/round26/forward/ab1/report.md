## Consequences and exceptions

For either nonzero sign of tau, M bounds the complete crossing sum and (AB1.2) holds without assuming that G(tau) and G(-tau) have the same spectrum. If there are no retained crossings (for example Lambda={0,1}^3), E=0 and every actual equal-energy block vanishes exactly. Indeed K is then the bounded exact full inverse; disjoint retained terms commute with K. This is a boundary geometry exception, not the general containing-volume answer.
