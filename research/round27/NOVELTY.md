The entries below are contributions to this workbench. Scientific priority is
unverified: the selected source survey is not an exhaustive theorem comparison,
and independently authored agent checks are not external mathematical peer review.
The elementary identities and standard tools used in these arguments are not
claimed as new mathematics.

AI1's algebra of identifiable parameter combinations is standard inverse-problem
reasoning applied to this particular model. A uniform bound on function values
does not by itself control a derivative; the explicit counterexample prevents an
invalid initial-slope interpretation.

AI2 extends the actual multiplication observables and the reached reducing
component used in AD2/AA2. That component is complete for this preparation and
retained dynamics, not for the whole energy shell. Its full scalar disk retains
state preparation, averaging, connected-tail, finite-volume and polynomial errors.
The mathematical result distinguishes two fixed hypotheses with the same limiting
slow rate. It neither proves stable inversion over a continuum of parameters nor
matches the canonical summable profile to the homogeneous model.

AG1 uses standard bounded-commutator integration, spectral calculus and
support-weight loss, with explicit complete counts for this source family. It
includes conjugation of both G and A: the nonlinear series contains
`ad_S^n(n A + R)/(n+1)!`. Its contraction denominator is the actual indexed input
norm, not the upper cubic budget. The forward and reverse authors independently
obtained different valid positive bounds; the skeptic checked the stronger
forward recurrence and supplied a separately attributed integrated-tail bound.
The public statement uses the conservative factor `2/3`. Neither one correction
nor a smaller selected source establishes an all-stage stability theorem.

The source survey is summarized in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md). It compares selected
primary historical texts and recent work in stochastic gauge dynamics, lattice
measures, scale setting and quantum simulation. Dimensions, actions, gauge groups,
limits, observables and clock conventions are kept explicit. Patents, mystical
interpretations and conversation threads do not become physical premises by being
included in the survey.

The target remains the nontrivial four-dimensional continuum quantum theory and
its positive physical mass gap. No percentage of that theorem can be inferred
from these rows, the network size, code checks or pages of manuscript.
