## Exact nonlinear target

Assume the finite-volume generator S is bounded skew-adjoint, preserves D(G), and has the actual bounded commutator

\[
[S,G]=-A+R.
\]

The earlier priority note displayed a G-only integral identity. The target requested here is G+A, which has the exact update

\[
e^S(G+A)e^{-S}=G+R+N,
\]

\[
N=\int_0^1 e^{s\operatorname{ad}_S}
\big(s[S,A]+(1-s)[S,R]\big)\,ds
=\sum_{k\ge1}\frac{\operatorname{ad}_S^k(kA+R)}{(k+1)!}.
\]

Derivation: expand G only after replacing its first commutator by the bounded -A+R, and expand the bounded A separately. Their zero-order A cancels the -A from [S,G]. At order k>=1 the combined coefficient is A/k!+(-A+R)/(k+1)!=(kA+R)/(k+1)!. Equivalently combine the first-order integral remainder for A with the second-order integral remainder for G. The first nonlinear term is [S,A+R]/2. Losing the [S,A] contribution would check the wrong update.

For an additional E,

\[
e^S(G+A+E)e^{-S}=G+R+N+e^SEe^{-S}.
\]

Transporting E does not establish E is small, fully inventoried, or harmless to the gap. An unknown E must stay an unresolved conditional term.
