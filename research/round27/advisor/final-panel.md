# Final panel assessment

**Three requested investigations are complete and source-complete within their
declared scopes. A defensible percentage of the Yang–Mills existence-and-gap
proof cannot be estimated from this work.**

The panel consists of contemporary model agents using selected documented
methods associated with Newton, Tesla, Jung/Pauli, Penrose and Feynman, plus an
advisor and separate skeptic. It is not participation by those people, external
human peer review, independent physical observations or formal proof checking.
The individual final reviews were written after the three executions; some
explicitly predate the last administrative source gate. The final AG1 gate now
records source-complete admission.

## What the three loops added

| Loop | Accepted result | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| AI1 | Exact fibers of three declared limiting/surrogate readout maps, conditional carrier identification, and controls that block an invalid finite-profile interpretation | General finite-profile inversion, noise-certified calibration or model matching |
| AI2 | Actual coherent Wilson probes with a complete ten-link seed/error analysis distinguish two frozen equal-rate hypotheses at one of nine settings | A continuous inverse, successful simple-ratio identification, or a practical experiment at `2e48 hbar/alpha` |
| AG1 | Full one-step nonlinear correction of the selected cubic source; contraction below `2/3` in the original interaction norm for `M<=1e-4` | Complete original remainder control, a second changed-diagonal step, all-stage stability, a homogeneous numerical gap or continuum construction |

AG1 has a full nonlinear estimate on the larger interval `M<=1e-3`, but its
contraction uses the narrower interval. The scalar, reference-diagonal and
mixing pieces remain explicit. Differing positive upper bounds from the two
producers and skeptic are compatible estimates, not different measurements.
The tighter forward recurrence and skeptic's separately attributed `<0.605`
integrated-tail refinement were checked after independent freezes. The public
factor `2/3` retains a conservative producer-supported statement.

claim of 100% of the theorem, and “not estimable” does not mean the research has
zero value. Nor is a guessed small percentage justified by caution alone.

obligations depend on one another and have no canonical equal weights.

## Combined next decision

The panel recommends **AG2: inventory and bound the complete original remainder
E before attempting another correction**. Its scalar, reference-diagonal and
mixing pieces may require different estimates. Do not assume E is a fourth-order
term, replace it with a free budget, or apply a whole-E estimate when a more
specific decomposition is needed. Only then establish the changed reference's
domain, gap/inverse hypotheses and remaining support-weight schedule.

AI3's cancellation-aware ratio and AH's complete graph scaling remain planned
alternatives. The [roadmap](roadmap.json) records those targets; no fourth loop
or future contract was executed. Paired derivation remained useful in this
cycle, but forward/backward language itself supplies no mathematical premise.

The [source survey](../SOURCES.md) covers 55 expert records, with exact reading
depth, including historical and occult writings, patent proposals, government
custody records, discussions and contemporary technical papers. No mystical or
rumor source supplied a verified new force, physical coefficient or mass-gap
premise. Scientific priority of the model-specific additions remains unverified.
