# Scale matching and exceptions

For a Hamiltonian H = alpha sum_e C_e - sum_p lambda_p x_p, alpha and lambda_p carry energy units, while x_p and C_e are dimensionless in the chosen convention. Record a single physical E_star and alpha/E_star. A dimensionless finite gap delta_N/alpha_N bounded away from zero does not imply a positive physical gap when alpha_N tends to zero.

Literal finite-volume restrictions require the same local coefficients on overlapping terms. A lower bound alpha_N >= alpha_min by itself does not make different finite Hamiltonians restrictions of one infinite Hamiltonian. Local coefficient consistency, limiting states, limiting dynamics and spectral passage have separate obligations.

On SU(2) graphs, distinguish the full link Hilbert space from gauge-invariant states and finite truncations. Specify the actual graph, including internal faces; the two-cube complex is not its outer boundary. Prove low-energy channel completeness including nonplanar cycles and intertwiner multiplicity before using a complement threshold.

When integrating another link, rebuild the affected-face union and signed words. A normalized inner expectation must be averaged with its conditional partition weight. A factor independent of the chosen observable may cancel from a normalized ratio; use partition evidence or another observable only after proving it discriminates. Do not force mismatch for a measure-preserving variable substitution.

For an exception equation, record: original claim, counterexample or missing premise, modified equation, its domain, recovery of the original case, and the next rejecting test. A spatially summable inhomogeneous family can be a useful result while the homogeneous dense target remains open.

Scientific comparisons between paired spiral nodes use the declared observable at the same model parameters. Fibonacci index is never a physical energy, time or volume without a separately defined map and empirical or mathematical justification. Test physical spiral hypotheses against alternative schedules using observables held out from construction; no such evidence is supplied by a visual resemblance.
