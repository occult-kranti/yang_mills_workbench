# Projected memory and boundary response

Use these lessons for a projected semigroup, Schur complement, retained-diagonal inverse or a support-locality claim. They arise from the independently reviewed Round22 Q/R investigations. Preserve the exact source sector, physical graph, units and topology before choosing a norm or importing an operator theorem.

## A compression is not a closed evolution

For an isometry J and P=JJ*, first calculate B=QHJ on the actual physical graph. If B is nonzero, J*exp(-tH/hbar)J need not equal exp(-tJ*HJ/hbar). Derive the exact block equation, including its domains, rather than infer an autonomous model from the instantaneous compression. A useful leading term does not make the retained channel set invariant.

In a Volterra elimination, preserve the full projected return factor. A symmetric double-Duhamel comparison and a one-sided memory equation have different middle factors; do not reject the valid one by applying the other's formula. For positive-energy resolvents derive the minus-self-energy Schur sign and the closed domain of the actual infinite-rank block. A finite-rank theorem, or one assuming a bounded reduced map, is not automatically applicable to an unbounded infinite-rank compression.

Compute spectra on the full mapped physical observables, not their values on a section. Q2's actual channels have equal instantaneous diagonal weights19/4, but different first energy moments57/4 and285/8. A scalar or one-exponential kernel therefore does not describe both. Exact low-channel spectral matrices are coefficients of the full operator, not an autonomous finite system.

State whether an error is absolute or relative and whether uniformity concerns delay, coupling, complementary resolvent energy or the full resolvent. A complement gap can give uniform absolute memory errors while relative late-time error or the full zero-energy resolvent remains uncontrolled. Resolvent order under positive perturbation does not imply operator order of heat semigroups.

## Identify the topology before reading a limit

Strong small-time convergence and convergence of scalar operator norms do not establish operator-norm convergence of the normalized operators. Q1's normalized positive-time differences are compact, while their strong limit is a noncompact multiplication operator bounded below. That is a direct obstruction to the stronger topology. Keep such a skeptical post-freeze refinement distinct from the producers' original claims.

## An interior inverse leaves crossing terms

Derive the actual initial retained-diagonal form bound; do not borrow a gap from another model or a later iteration. Prove finite-volume operator and form domains separately. A form-energy estimate need not be an operator graph estimate. Establish domain preservation before exponentiating a generator with an unbounded reference Hamiltonian.

When extending a local generator by identity, keep every crossing interaction and its declared union support, including incoming anchors and repeated indexed unions. R1's actual SU2 character probe has squared boundary defect7tau^2/432. All21 retained faces contribute even though only two physically touch its selected free link. A whole-factor source and projected interaction cannot be analyzed by physical probe-edge incidence alone.

An admissible diagnostic source is not automatically a residual generated by the original iteration. A valid family bound with one weight loss does not prove repeated contraction. Attribute a one-direction refinement accurately even when all reviewers later verify it.

## Vacuum response, operator locality and support weights

A global vacuum reduced inverse cancels a global rank-two source. For a local v_Y this source is A_Y tensor P_ext, whereas the identity-extended source is A_Y tensor I. An actual exterior excitation detects their difference; never silently exchange them. Specify whether the target is a vacuum response or a full operator homological equation, including its excited-sector solvability conditions.

For an infinite initial form, prove absolute form summability and its relative bound before applying KLMN. A sandwiched Neumann inverse may yield local-vector coefficients with geometric norm and energy tails. Derive a response graph bound from its actual local recurrence if available; do not infer global operator-domain equality from form equivalence.

A spatial collar bound is not a positive volume-weight interaction estimate. In three dimensions an origin collar has volume(n+1)^3; a geometric coefficient majorant multiplied by exp(mu(n+1)^3) fails the specified summability test for fixed positive mu and nonzero coupling. This is failure of an upper certificate, not divergence of actual coefficients or impossibility of every connected-support regrouping. Preserve the initial-H0+D scope; the omitted residual, later diagonal gaps and continuum construction remain separate obligations.

## Concrete regression cases

Before admitting a method update, check it against the bound prior failures: Q1 strong-versus-norm topology; Q2 wrong scalar memory, return factor and Schur sign; R1 dropped crossing faces; R2 exterior-excitation source mismatch and the specified collar-weight majorant. Execute the wrong control when feasible and retain a nondiscriminating attempt with its valid replacement. A retrospective regression validates these lessons on known evidence; it is not a new blind forward-research study and adds no research loops.
