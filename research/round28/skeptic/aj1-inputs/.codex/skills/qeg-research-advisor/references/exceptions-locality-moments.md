# Exceptions, locality and moment hierarchy contract

Apply this contract when extending the Yang–Mills workbench with new variables, exceptional regimes, compact moment hierarchies, growing-graph dynamics or product-vacuum stability arguments. It is a project-owned guide, not an installation of a new personal skill.

## Match both proof directions

For every route record gauge group, spacetime dimension, action, state, observable algebra, boundaries, spacing, energy units and quantifier order. Forward consequences and backward requirements may meet only under a proved match of these fields. Distinguish a Euclidean tilt, Hamiltonian magnetic/electric ratio, regulator, physical time and dynamical field. A shared symbol does not establish equality.

Classify exceptions: exact algebraic identity; genuine special regime of the same model; coordinate reparametrization; auxiliary representation with exact elimination; or changed action/dimension/geometry requiring a return theorem. Do not dismiss a valid restricted theorem because it misses the continuum. Do not transfer it beyond its domain because a missing coefficient is convenient to choose.

## Compact moment certification

Use the actual integration-by-parts recurrence, normalized support and localizing matrices. Finite constraints give necessary bounds; they do not supply the infinitely many premises of uniqueness. Every rejected halfline must retain its exact rational quadratic witness, affine inequality and intersection direction. Check feasible inner points and bound optimizer slack relative to the full finite feasible interval. A numerical eigenvalue or high-precision decimal is not an exact sign certificate.

The full hierarchy needs one normalized moment sequence at one fixed finite coupling, all positivity and recurrence constraints, compact measure existence, extension to the required test functions and endpoint-atom treatment. Algorithmic convergence additionally requires successfully certified orders tending to infinity and vanishing optimization slack. A finite implementation limit does not execute either asymptotic assumption.

For the exponential-Haar family, `u'=variance` is a coupling derivative. The Riccati origin is singular after division; use exact Haar moments and a regular series. An alternative integration constant can violate boundedness or positivity. Coefficient 2 corresponds to a different uniform prior, so its success changes the measure. Separate an exact launch-error bound from floating ODE error; continuous residual enclosures are required for a total trajectory certificate.

Two adjacent open planar Wilson squares are an exact Euclidean factorization baseline after maximal-tree gauge fixing. Their Hamiltonian shared-link kinetic term does not create a face-face coupling in that Euclidean measure. A nonfactorized experiment requires an explicitly changed mixed-loop action or a suitable higher-dimensional complex. Retain this baseline as a test before invoking false-factorization objections.

## Locality and unbounded electric operators

Keep electric coefficients identical in compared volumes, bounded plaquette interactions, a common integrable absolute envelope, finite observable support and actual graph incidence. Freeze arbitrary iterables once before repeated traversal. A consumed generator must never turn a connected graph into a zero boundary bound.

Use support-preserving electric interaction pictures and a unitary-resummed commutator recursion. Raw Dyson histories do not obey the simple overlapping-chain count. Compute radius as interaction-chain distance to removed terms, with disconnected and zero-action branches handled explicitly. A fixed-spacing local norm limit between volumes is not norm continuity in time on all bounded operators, nor a vacuum or gap theorem.

## Product-vacuum stability

Read the exact source theorem and verify whether infinite-dimensional onsite spaces and unbounded onsite operators are admitted. Block links into sites explicitly, retain free-gap units, perturbation norms and finite interaction support. Match source boundary conventions: finite-box padding with free cells may transfer a uniform bound without identifying every thermodynamic boundary limit.

An existential threshold cannot certify a particular positive numerical coupling. If extracting constants, trace every combinatorial and analytic inequality. Keep the physical Gauss restriction explicit; prove the unique vacuum belongs to it rather than assuming all full-Hilbert excitations are physical. A fixed-spacing gap in the small magnetic/electric regime is useful even though the usual weak-bare-coupling continuum trajectory exits it.

## Frozen evaluator inputs

Bind the complete frozen rule and source bytes to the successful evidence replay. Revalidate the fingerprint when constructing each library, not just the rule IDs. An exposed adapter can otherwise accept a mutated rule after replay. Retain an independently implemented mutation that attempts this change. Keep mathematical review, arithmetic replay, numerical diagnostics and interface testing separate, and do not sum normal/optimized repeats as new gates.
