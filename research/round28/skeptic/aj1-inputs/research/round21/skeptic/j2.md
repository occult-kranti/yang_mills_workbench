## Averaging and the reverse inclusion

perturbed vacuum. G2 full-algebra cyclicity supplies local `A_n` with

Both reports correctly use a weak operator Haar integral, equivalently strong
vector integrals, for the endpoint average. Strongly continuous unitaries imply
continuity of matrix elements and of their action on fixed vectors; they do
not imply operator-norm continuity of conjugation on every bounded operator.
The average is a bounded contraction. Conjugation preserves the original link
support even when endpoint transformations also act on neighboring outside
links. The local factor algebra is strongly/weakly closed, so the average
remains local. Haar invariance at all endpoints gives invariance under every
finite-support vertex transformation.

Since both Psi and xi are invariant, vector integration gives

`||E_n(A_n)Psi-xi|| <= ||A_n Psi-xi|| -> 0`.

No uniform bound on `||A_n||` is necessary: this argument controls the vector
error before averaging. Therefore `H_cyc=H_inv` is established here. It depends
on access to **all** bounded local invariant averages and on full-algebra
cyclicity. It would not follow for an arbitrary selected list of Wilson
operators or a scalar-only algebra.

than the full Hilbert space, while its nonzero centered Wilson fluctuation
