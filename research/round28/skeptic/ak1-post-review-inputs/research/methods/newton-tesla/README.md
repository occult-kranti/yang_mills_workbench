## Mathematical bridge, without a historical shortcut

The Newton-inspired inverse question in U1 was: what local observable could detect an endpoint effect? One loop state's diagonal perturbation vanishes by parity. Two equal-energy loop states differing by a plaquette produce an actual nonzero matrix element. The Tesla-inspired mechanism question was: does that coupling persist when the rest of the system is included? U1 could not yet answer that and received a limited verdict.

U2 then counted every allowed connected interaction word. Starting with eight complete factors, each next face can add at most three; at most forty faces meet a factor. This yielded an all-order remainder in the dimensionless variable s=alpha tau t/hbar. The exact resonant term exceeded that remainder for a stated small interval, while the ground-state error vanished. This supplies a lower bound on an actual correlation difference. No historical claim enters the calculation.

## What the occult material contributes

Newton's laboratory records connect speculative hidden processes with concrete weights, residues and failed mixtures. His prophetic language uses a different symbolic dictionary. The skill keeps both contexts visible. It encourages searching for transformations and hidden structure, then requires a declared map and a discriminating mathematical or experimental test.

For Tesla, the contemporary Vivekananda letter reports an anticipated demonstration rather than supplying one. The attributed 1930 essay has weaker web provenance in this review. These materials motivate questions about unified descriptions, cycles and transformations. They do not identify prana with a measured energy, ether with a quantum field, or imagination with a verified physical effect. Those would be new hypotheses requiring their own evidence.
