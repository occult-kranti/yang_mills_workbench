## AI4 recommendation: shorter-duration uniform family

I recommend the advisor's **shorter-duration uniform-family candidate** as the
next bounded question, with an explicitly acceptable insufficient outcome.
The duration change is Feynman's proposal; its combination with the dyadic
collar schedule is the advisor's proposal. The candidate is `z=10^-7`,
`u0=10^-24`, and `k(j)=6+j` on

```
u0*2^(-(j+1)) < u <= u0*2^(-j),   j=0,1,... .
```

These are proposed parameters, not selected or certified in this review.
The fixed-cell alternative `u=10^-24,k=6,z=10^-7` is the economical fallback:
it tests the duration tradeoff with an existing exact collar. Its limitation
is that it mainly adds one parameter setting. The proposed uniform family
asks for a more useful statement about the resource schedule and shrinking
candidate separation. Its larger proof burden must be part of the frozen
contract, rather than silently replaced by a few successful sampled bands.

The inherited formulas explain why the tradeoff is worth testing. Reducing z
reduces the spatial tail more rapidly than the leading signal, while the state
error is independent of this observation duration and becomes larger relative
to that signal. AI3 already identified a cell where spatial error dominates
and state-only intervals separate. This motivates the proposal; it does not
prove a positive base margin after shortening the duration.

The contracted target should retain the following obligations:

* Prove the exact base inequality and continuous bounds within each dyadic band,
  then an induction for every integer j. A finite list of bands is a control,
  not the uniform proof. Any such list and new collar enumeration must be
  declared before production.
* Derive an all-depth coordinate/owner/face envelope from the actual complete-
  factor rule. The depth-six enumeration checks the base only. Whole strips,
  positive-orthant boundaries, ordered words, repeated faces and return paths
  remain charged.
* Keep the full state and spatial costs, both averaging columns, multiplier
  norm and strictly positive denominator. Separate retained center bias,
  all-order combination arithmetic and physical errors. Seek a stated positive
  multiple of u as a margin only if proved; retain failure if the frozen
  schedule does not meet it.
* Treat the family as predeclared candidate-pair tests at their common original
  time `t=2zu^-3 hbar/alpha`. Do not feed the true unknown u into an instrument
  schedule and then call it inferred. Alpha, hbar, alpha/E_star and spacing
  remain fixed; q and eta are not lattice spacing or a fitted clock.
* State the u-dependent scalar allowance a proved margin would permit. A
  vanishing allowance is not robustness to fixed absolute instrument noise.
  Demodulation still needs the common independently fixed carrier. No unknown
  preparation, readout, phase or timestamp uncertainty can be canceled or
  presumed covered by a ratio of retained centers.

The advisor's precision correction is necessary and correct. The current
integer routine rounds the **inner** `sqrt(b(q^2)/96)`, then multiplies the
rounding error by tau and the declared remaining factors. Since tau decreases
as u^3 on these hypotheses, a fixed absolute inner bracket does not create a
fixed scalar floor. It must still be propagated and charged. A fixed absolute
error appended to the final scalar is a different matter.

At fixed z, an individual fixed-degree retained Taylor remainder is a constant
upper bound in u. It may remain harmless in a denominator with a uniform
positive leading value, but cannot be used uncritically as a numerator error
when the intended separation shrinks with u. The proved combination remainder
has the required `1-q` factor. Relative analytic envelopes or band-dependent
precision are possible tools, not automatic requirements for every inner
square-root operation.

A successful AI4 family would still be a conditional mathematical readout
theorem in the canonical summable model. It would not construct a continuous
inverse, calibrate alpha, realize the enormous physical clock, establish a
homogeneous matching map, or solve continuum Yang–Mills. Selection remains
with the advisor after AI3 admission.
