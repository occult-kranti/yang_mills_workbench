**Advice only; no AJ2 selection, derivation, checker or fixture has been executed.** The final AJ1 gate remains pending its approved admission specification. This advice is based on the actual reviewed AJ1 result, not on a positive spectral threshold being mistaken for an excited vector.

Prefer a bounded target: exhibit one specified, nonconstant, bounded gauge-invariant local Wilson multiplication operator in the actual homogeneous I1 state, prove that its centered GNS vector is nonzero, and state exactly what this adds to the imaginary-time spectral measure. Keep the same complete-factor model, whole-star orthant prescription, actual centered physical generator and symbolic `|tau|<tau_*`. Do not inherit the dyadic J1/J2 variance constant or its numeric coupling range.

The first candidate route to assess under a frozen contract is qualitative and uses AJ1's new local normality. For a specified elementary Wilson function, examine its local Haar multiplication spectral projections and the measure of its level sets. Ask whether local normality rules out a density being supported entirely on one such level set. This route might establish nonzero variance throughout the inherited symbolic regime without an additional perturbative continuity radius. It requires a real proof of the chosen function's level-set property on the complete local configuration space; nonconstancy by itself is insufficient for an arbitrary measurable function. A constant observable and a bounded step function with positive-measure level sets would be useful falsifiers of any overbroad claim. An SU(2) trace is real; an imaginary-time correlator should not be described as the imaginary part of that trace.

A smaller alternative is a source-qualified local-continuity argument near the product state, with a strictly positive product-state variance and an explicitly symbolic extra radius. It could be easier if the level-set route encounters an obstruction, but it narrows the parameter claim and must identify the exact usable source estimate. Neither approach should manufacture a numerical nonzero interval from unevaluated stability constants.

If a nonzero centered vector is established, the contract should distinguish positivity of its finite-time imaginary-time correlator and a gap-based upper decay ceiling from a lower decay estimate, an attained lowest eigenvalue, or real-time magnitude decay. A finite gap exclusion and a nonzero vector may determine spectral support without proving an atom at the threshold. Any desired energy moment or differentiability statement must separately check the vector's form or operator domain; bounded locality alone does not provide it.

They would not directly remove AJ1's currently explicit nonzero-physical-sector omission. No Goal5 choice should be inferred from this preference before the AJ pair has been reviewed.
