# Newton research reference

## Coverage

Consulted 21 September 2026. Selected primary texts were read in the passages identified below. Catalogues are discovery routes, not claims that every manuscript was read. Some algebra disappears from web extraction: consult manuscript images before relying on ambiguous symbols. Modern formulas below are our reconstructions, not quotations or claims that Newton used modern notation.

## Mathematics: motion and inverse operations

**N1 — October 1666 tract on fluxions**, Add.3958.3, ff.48v–63v, [diplomatic transcription](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/diplomatic/NATP00100). Read ff.48v–49r and the substitutions/quadratures discussion. Newton introduces auxiliary quantities to express rates of change, then substitutes them back. Visible revisions reveal work concealed by a polished formula. Equations missing from extracted text are not verified transcriptions here.

Modern reconstruction: if F(x,y)=0 and F_y≠0, then y′=−F_x/F_y. The condition F_y≠0 cannot disappear in reverse reconstruction. Integration also requires initial data; distinct functions share a derivative. Likewise, one matrix element or finitely many moments need not identify an operator. Record the kernel of every inverse map.

**N2 — De motu corporum in gyrum**, [NATP00089](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/normalized/NATP00089). Read Theorems 2–3 and corollaries. The passage relates circular centripetal force to squared speed divided by radius and reconstructs force laws from orbital geometry through a limiting construction.

Modern reconstruction: uniform circular motion gives a=4π²r/T². If T²=kr³, then a=(4π²/k)r⁻². This uses circular kinematics and a common k; it does not alone establish the general many-body law. For f(x)=Σa_nx^n, termwise differentiation requires convergence and domain conditions. In this project the analogue is counting all connected words before summing a perturbation series. First coefficients do not control a tail.

## Physics: experiments and explanatory scope

**N3 — Opticks, Book III, Query 31 (1718)**, [NATP00051](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/normalized/NATP00051). Read closing analysis/synthesis discussion and opening diffraction observations. Analysis proceeds from experiments and effects toward causes; synthesis derives consequences from established principles. Queries remain invitations to further examination. The shadow experiments vary distances and media. Adopt that workflow while using current mathematics to evaluate results.

**N4 — Principia, Book III, Rules of Reasoning**, [1729 translation](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Mathematical_Principles_of_Natural_Philosophy_(1729)/Rules_of_Reasoning_in_Philosophy). Read Rules I–IV: sufficient causes, comparison of like effects and revisable induction. These are historical commitments, not exemptions from counterexamples. Prefer mechanisms with explicit assumptions and test beyond the motivating fixture.

**N5 — Letter to Oldenburg, 11 June 1672**, [NATP00394](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/normalized/NATP00394). Consulted as the primary record of Newton's response to objections to his optical account. Correspondence helps identify what an experiment was supposed to discriminate. A measured refrangibility account and a speculative microscopic mechanism are different claims.

## Alchemy and hidden processes

**N6 — Experiments, Add.3973**, [ALCH00109](https://newton.dlib.indiana.edu/text/ALCH00109/normalized). Read December 1678 opening entries: weights, residues, colors, heating and mixtures that did not combine as intended. These are laboratory notes within chymistry, not proof of transmutation. Translate the useful practice into dated operation logs, quantities, negative outcomes and complete error budgets.

**N7 — Of Natures obvious laws & processes in vegetation**, Dibner MS.1031B, [ALCH00081](https://newton.dlib.indiana.edu/text/ALCH00081/normalized). Read opening propositions and proposed correspondence between mineral and living processes. This is a historical search for organizing principles through vegetation and hidden agencies. A symbol may motivate a search for conserved structure; only a derived map preserving measure, domain and observables licenses it in our model.

Distinguish operations from interpretations, and Newton's work from copied authorities. Early modern “spirit,” “vegetation” and “mercury” are context-dependent, not automatic names for quantum fields, energy or modern substances. Neither dismiss every alchemical experiment nor import alchemical claims as physics. No recreation of hazardous recipes is part of this skill.

## Theology and symbolic interpretation

**N8 — Observations upon the Prophecies of Daniel (1733), I.II**, [THEM00196](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/normalized/THEM00196). Read the mapping between natural and political worlds. This posthumously published theological interpretation is not a mechanics experiment. Its methodological relevance is an explicit interpretive dictionary. Expose the dictionary between a metaphor and a mathematical question and disclose where it fails.

Theology is not interchangeable with ceremonial occultism. This selection does not establish Newton's participation in every tradition attributed to him. Study chronology and prophecy historically without deriving physical constants from scripture. Ancient-wisdom motifs can motivate reconstruction of lost assumptions; age and secrecy do not validate equations.

## Project application and expansion

For local-to-global physics ask whether a small mechanism survives omitted factors. Retain numerical units, input states, truncation and error sources. In UI distinguish prediction, enclosure and simulated trajectory. Select the next loop from the most damaging unresolved objection.

Example: a nonzero resonant matrix element predicts a first-order term. Synthesis must control all bulk-connected higher terms and state replacement. Until then, endpoint nonconvergence is unproved. This is our modern application, not Newton's theorem.

Use the [Newton Project introduction](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/texts/introduction) and [Chymistry catalogue](https://newton.dlib.indiana.edu/) for expansion. Reading leads: complete Principia definitions/laws/limit arguments; De analysi and De quadratura; optical correspondence; Index Chemicus; General Scholium, chronology and theological manuscripts. These remain leads unless a dated note identifies inspected passages. Search niche articles for manuscript locators, then return to primary sources. Never claim exhaustive coverage of every book or private thought.
