# Hidden causes and reachable spectra

Selected reading updated 2026-09-21. This is an expandable primary-source and modern-method map, not complete knowledge of Newton's fields or a priority review.

## Historical provenance

- Read Newton's [Keynes MS.28, ALCH00017](https://webapp1.dlib.indiana.edu/newton/mss/norm/ALCH00017), ff.2r–2v, as a translation/transcription of the Hermetic Emerald Tablet. Its above/below and separation/recombination language may suggest a correspondence question. It is not a Newtonian theorem that two scales or models are equivalent. English passage and manuscript metadata inspected; Latin commentary not fully translated.
- Read [ALCH00109](https://newton.dlib.indiana.edu/text/ALCH00109/normalized), December 1678 entries, for measured residues and unsuccessful mixtures. Preserve failures and quantitative balances without importing alchemical terminology as modern substances or forces.
- Read [General Scholium, NATP00056](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/normalized/NATP00056), final paragraphs: theology and speculation coexist with an explicit shortage of experiments for proposed hidden mechanisms. Keep that evidential boundary.
- Distinguish Newton's assertions from ancient authorities copied in [TRAN00013](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/normalized/TRAN00013), a modern translation of his mythology notes.

## Translate an analogy into a test

Record historical term → candidate modern variable/map → units → observable → discriminating prediction → rejection condition. For a cross-scale correspondence require an actual map, domains, state/measure relation, generator relation and a bounded defect. A metaphor cannot supply those hypotheses. For a latent parameter compute the forward Jacobian and its null space before fitting. Frequencies proportional to chi/R identify that ratio, not chi and R separately. Additional modes with the same dependence do not remove the ambiguity.

## Exact reachable components as a valid exception

Whole degenerate energy blocks must be retained unless a smaller component is proved reducing for the averaged operator. An observable can reach a much smaller component than the whole eigenspace. Prove its physical basis complete, every internal matrix entry, and exclusion of every external equal-energy channel. Use spectral support, not merely a positive expectation. In tensor reference models, distinct factor ownership and symmetry charge can force exterior excitations above the initial energy. Check every boundary geometry and fusion branch.

If (I-P_E) A P_L has no participating frequencies within delta of E and the averaged orbit stays in L, the primitive on L obeys 2 epsilon ||A||/delta even if other unused energies approach E. Carry it through integration by parts; do not relabel this source-specific bound as global operator-norm averaging. Keep stationary-state replacement, spatial tails, physical clock and arithmetic errors separate. Use stable Re(F)-1 or exact rational centers near unit correlations.

The Yang–Mills workbench Round25 AA1/AA2 is a same-author application, not independent validation or established literature. Its sixteen-state cube component does not transfer to another observable or a homogeneous model without a new proof.
