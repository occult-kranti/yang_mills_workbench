## Five new/deeper source items

1. **Tesla's retrospective account of mystical experience:** [*My Inventions*, section VI](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/My_Inventions), the discussion of a psychic society, his mother's death, and his later interpretation. The text describes strong initial supernatural impressions and a subsequent search for ordinary external causes. It also includes his own unverified generalizations about coincidences and bodily responses; he should not be recast as a flawless modern skeptic. This is autobiographical testimony through a transcription, not a controlled experiment or a contemporaneous lab note. The operational lesson is to distinguish an arresting coincidence from a preregistered discriminator.

2. **Patent's complete energy path:** [US685957A](https://patents.google.com/patent/US685957A/en), especially the descriptions of figures 1, 3 and 4 and claims. Its radiant source charges a condenser; figure 3 separately powers the local output circuit using source B. Figure 4 requires excitation of the radiation tube, and the text predicts receiver changes when radiation is interrupted. A detector controlling a powered load is not evidence that the detector supplies all output energy. The patent establishes a proposed arrangement, not unrestricted performance or a missing Yang–Mills term.

3. **Rumor traced back to that patent:** [r/NikolaTesla discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/NikolaTesla/comments/14ehl7r/nikola_teslas_invention_for_collecting_energy/) links the patent to claims of instantaneous transmission, unlimited loading and a radiation type without ordinary magnetic behavior. Those claims remain discussion content. The actual patent explicitly contains incident radiation, storage, a circuit controller and optional separate power. Its text provides no tested result supporting those broad extrapolations. This is a new claim-to-primary-record trace beyond Round27's custody rumor, not a survey of Reddit users or a validation from patent status.

4. **Observable cancellation with surviving bias:** Eddins et al., [arXiv:2606.20441v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.20441v1), sections I, III–IV and selected appendices, propagates inverse modeled noise into a modified observable. The authors separate exact model identities, truncated approximations, sampling costs and residual noise-model error. Their low sampling cost can retain unbounded truncation bias. The relevant inference for our work is methodological: an exact cancellation inside a model does not cancel uncertainty in the model, omitted operators or the apparatus. The qubit circuits are not our canonical full-link theory, and their results were not reproduced here.

5. **Normalization and hidden correlated faults:** Fischer et al., [arXiv:2609.13108v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.13108v1), main setup and appendix A, combines error detection with modeled cancellation. Faults can evade post-selection jointly even if each would be detected individually. The correct ensemble averages unnormalized contributions before dividing by the common success probability; separately normalizing branches generally changes it. Its hardware and perturbative claims remain external research. The transferable lesson is to retain normalization, omitted joint events and the cost of rejected samples when a ratio appears to remove an error.

These five items add a specific mystical/autobiographical passage, a patent/rumor provenance pair and two 2026 primary research comparisons. They are selected readings, not complete coverage of Tesla's writings or current quantum research. No new government-custody inference is made; Round27's official custody and program records retain their stated scope.

## AI3/AI4: cancellation that must earn its error reduction

The roadmap's proposed identity `m3(B5)-q*m3(B4)=2q^13(1-q^2)` concerns exact retained moments. Audit its sign in the sine series, all higher odd orders needed for the chosen time window, and the full remainder before claiming a useful ratio bound. A polynomial factor `(1-q)` in a center is not automatically a factor in its uncertainty.

For a proposed combination of scalar values, write `y_r=h_r+e_r` with the already certified full error radii. In the absence of a proved joint error structure, the conservative bound is

\[
|e_5-q e_4|\le R_5+|q|R_4.
\]

Add preparation, measurement, phase and clock errors through their own valid sensitivity maps. Correlated statistical errors can be treated more sharply only after specifying and checking their joint measurement distribution; shared code, a shared symbolic term or identical upper bounds does not establish physical correlation. If q is unknown, evaluating `y5-q*y4` with the true q is circular: use candidate-q residual intervals and return the admissible parameter set. The denominator interval must stay strictly away from zero.

Recommended frozen controls: both exact signs of the imaginary Taylor terms; `q=1` as a formal retained symmetry/null control without declaring a global endpoint generator; a deliberately misweighted q5 edge; independent versus adversarially aligned scalar errors; denominator-crossing cases; phase mismatch; and a complete ten-link support rejection test. Changing the measured combination may change its operator norm and apparatus. Preserve complete-factor collars and both source and vacuum loading columns.

Choose AI4 only after AI3 review. If only the retained Taylor floor improves, target the dominant still-uncancelled physical term or a precisely specified instrument sensitivity. If a full parameter interval is certified, use a held-out interval/time and an explicit nuisance-parameter control. Report failed certificates as insufficient bounds, not actual indistinguishability. Keep physical time and the reference alpha beside every numerical gain.
