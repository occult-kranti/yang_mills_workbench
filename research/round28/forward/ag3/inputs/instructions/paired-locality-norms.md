# Locality norms and computed heat

Use these lessons for filtered inverses, translated interactions and certified semigroup evaluation. They are modern mathematical workflow lessons from the paired Yang–Mills Round26 (2026-09-22), not external peer review or established priority. Preserve older instruction snapshots.

## Prove the topology actually needed

Remove unbounded onsite generators in a support-preserving interaction picture before using bounded interaction recurrences. Require strong/weak calculus on the actual separable Hilbert spaces. An all-time Lieb–Robinson path estimate can be integrated over an entire Gaussian when its exponential-in-time majorant is integrable. The older connected-word majorant may have only a finite time radius and cannot be integrated beyond it. A norm limit as spatial volume grows does not imply norm continuity in time on the full local bounded-operator algebra.

Operator-norm approximation J_R to J alone does not control [J-J_R,G] when G is unbounded. Prove the regional identity and sum every crossing commutator separately before asserting graph-norm approximation. State whether the conclusion is for one source, a translated interaction family or an extensive generator.

A collar with radius R can contain order R^d sites. Exponential radius decay may support diameter weights while the same upper budget fails after multiplying by exp(mu R^d). This proves failure of that upper certificate, not actual divergence or impossibility of another decomposition. Audit actual connected words if the target norm weights support cardinality. Verify any finite-qudit theorem before applying it to infinite-dimensional local factors.

## Keep source geometry and filter tradeoffs explicit

Boundary counts at the origin may differ from counts for translated interior sources. Enumerate the relative-anchor difference set and retain all overlaps, including repeated interaction words and root-containing translations. Do not export a three-crossing coefficient to a twelve-crossing family.

A compact-time filter can give both per-source operator contraction and finite cardinality-weighted norms on a narrower coupling interval. Disclose that interval and keep physical time distinct from proof-filter duration. Finite weighted summability is not weighted contraction. Test the duration inequalities analytically on the continuous interval. If no duration meets two sufficient certificates at the old cap, report that limited incompatibility rather than failure of the actual dynamics. Newly generated sources and changed diagonals need fresh estimates.

## Execute the claimed numerical object

Distinguish exact matrix action, a bound on an exact semigroup, and an executed heat vector. In a nonorthonormal basis use the actual Gram metric for self-adjointness, residuals, projector error, independent-vector comparison and export rounding. Isolate the minimum eigenvalue before turning a small trial residual into a ground-center interval. Compare independent exported vectors within their numerical enclosures; different physical envelopes can yield different valid total errors.

Separate physical omitted channels, true-versus-retained ground centering, uncertain numerical retained center, exponential approximation and coordinate export. A fixed center error produces a secular heat error and cannot be extrapolated to infinite time. A piecewise evaluator may use a bounded-time polynomial branch and a late ground-projector approximation, with separate projector and excited-tail bounds. Prove branch coverage on the entire promised domain; sampled exports do not supply uniformity. Preserve the true full-output denominator and normalized input class.

## Preserve executed provenance

Flatten every supported producer source inventory into admission checks, including different field names used by independent implementations. Retain immutable old gates and record an explicit additive inventory repair. Bind optional controls to their actual execution receipts; never say the default replay ran a control it did not execute. Keep reviewer, replay and interface counts separate from physics loops.
