## Proposed bounded AH2 target

**Recommend delayed-loading physical error refinement on the same 561-state
compression, with the same coupling interval, preparation class and clock.**
AH1's early bound charges every initially prepared excited component with the
whole `29lambda` outside envelope. It does not exploit that all initial P0
columns vanish. The exact matrix now supplies the missing route to propagate
that cancellation at positive times.

Let `N=P+-P0` and `F=P0-|Omega><Omega|`. The concrete inherited premises are:

- `BP0=0` and `||B||<=29lambda`, including every new-input column.
- The complete new-to-new magnetic block vanishes by the checked center parity.
- `N(A+-mu+)N=K_N+(29lambda-mu+)N>= (9/2)N`.
- `NA+P0=-lambda NSF` and `NS Omega=0`.
- AH1's complete original residual Gram already fixes `||NSF||=sqrt(57)/2`.
- The full and retained ground estimates, true denominator and common-domain
  Duhamel relation remain available with their exact scopes.

The proposed new work is to derive and certify the inhomogeneous retained
loading equation with `Nu(0)=0`, retain its full feedback, then integrate the
result against the full outside envelope. It should give a continuous-coupling
early bound and join it with a separately valid full spectral tail at a
predeclared time. The advisor should freeze that join and acceptance comparison
before execution. A proof that the refined certificate improves on the admitted
AH1 bound is a clear target; an insufficient bound must remain an honest limited
outcome. No new numerical threshold is computed or preclaimed in this review.

This task uses the existing sparse matrix and small exact Gram data. Its main
cost is proof and rational certification, rather than a larger basis. Its risks
are incorrectly extending initial cancellation to autonomous dynamics, losing
feedback or center error, and claiming preparation-uniform control beyond P0.
The frozen contract should explicitly require controls for each.

If the advisor instead prioritizes executable heat vectors, a bounded numerical
alternative is the same graph at fixed lambda=1/100: a certified early matrix
polynomial and late approximate ground projector, with a new trial residual,
spectral isolation and complete excited tail. Every sparse matvec uses 2,124
entries; a degree-d polynomial uses O(2,124d) coefficient operations per vector
and time, with big-integer/rational bit cost charged separately. The physical
omission allowance, numerical center, approximation and export errors must stay
separate against the same true denominator. AF2's old polynomial degree, trial
ground, numerical tolerance and accuracy do not transfer automatically. This
alternative yields usable vectors but does not itself reduce AH1's dominant
physical bound. I therefore favor the delayed-loading target for this second
loop, leaving any additional evaluator requirement to an explicit advisor
decision before freeze.
