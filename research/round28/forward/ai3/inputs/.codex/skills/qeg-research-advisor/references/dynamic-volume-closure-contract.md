# Dynamic, volume and hierarchy bridges

Use after the two-plaquette contract. Maintain the current roadmap separately from immutable historical experiment plans. Every status change needs a source/data path, verdict and scope; completion of an old proposed experiment must not be inferred from a favorable summary alone.

## Driven representation error

State the physical Haar norm, self-adjoint domain, nested orthogonal polynomial spaces and common initial state. For the seven-link model, K preserves every degree flag and bounded trace multiplication changes degree by at most one. For fixed α,ρ>0 and real integrable λᵢ, define b(t)=|λ₁(t)|+|λ₂(t)| and A(t)=∫b.

For a normalized initial state of degree at most d₀ and cutoff D≥d₀, the reviewed high-degree block/Duhamel argument gives exact-state error at most min(2,A^(D−d₀+1)/(D−d₀+1)!). This compares exact full-space and exact Galerkin propagators. It is not an automatic error certificate for a computed vector.

Account separately for initial mismatch, representation error, coefficient construction and numerical time integration. A sampled boundary residual integral needs a rigorous interpolation/quadrature enclosure before it can certify anything. A bounded-observable norm bound does not control an unbounded electric-energy expectation. The cap2 presumes normalized exact states; do not apply it to arbitrary approximate vectors. Treat a bound of2 as valid but inconclusive accuracy evidence.

Changing the drive history at fixed A tests the bound, not equality of the physical dynamics. Never replace the absolute action by a signed integral or endpoint value. Track electric phase αT and fixed/shared-link weights separately.

## Volume and continuum

The open-box free physical gap proof uses Gauss law at every vertex, no external charges, a simple graph whose shortest cycle has length4, and an existing square. It does not automatically apply to short periodic identifications or parallel edges.

A heat-kernel ground-state comparison can deteriorate exponentially with link count even when the true gap does not. Use independent tensor copies as a counterbenchmark for that inference, clearly labeling their disconnected geometry. A finite positive bound on each graph is not a volume-uniform theorem.

Keep a lattice Gibbs measure, its stochastic-time generator, Euclidean covariance decay and a physical Hamiltonian distinct. Functional inequalities for one do not transfer to another by naming both a mass gap. Test any proposed common ground-state density by applying the Hamiltonian and checking whether Hφ/φ is constant.

In the declared3+1 convention α=g₀²/(2a), λ=2/(g₀²a), λ/α=4/g₀⁴. A planar finite graph remains a toy graph after coefficient substitution. List scale matching, observable construction, volume, continuum, nontriviality, reconstruction and physical spectral support as separate proof obligations.

## Hierarchy closure and source critique

Derive compact Haar identities before changing variables. Distinguish a new coordinate, an existing fluctuation observable and a new physical field. A variance variable records a missing moment; it does not determine the rest of a hierarchy.

Audit article equations against the stated generator, ordered-plaquette convention and source derivative. Use explicit matrices or independent link perturbations. State the exact article version and equation affected. A local typo or inconsistent coefficient is not a refutation of every result in the article.

Before a color-Lorentz ansatz, check dimensions and rank: ηᵀgη=I_(N²−1) requires N²−1≤D. A restricted-color choice changes a full-color claim. Separate point-concentration closure from Gaussian higher-cumulant closure; a counterexample to one does not refute the other.

## Acceptance and search

The advisor proposes; an independently implemented skeptic challenges. A successful objection improves or rejects the hypothesis rather than becoming a target to defeat. Freeze the actual source bytes before replay. A proof planner searches reviewed implications; it cannot manufacture a missing continuum theorem. Include withheld bandedness, incorrect initial degree, source/action mismatch and unavailable numerical enclosure among the negative controls.
