# Centered heat comparison and single-loop closeout

Use for ground-centered projected evolution or a request to finish one active research loop and stop. These lessons come from Round23 T2; its derivation and review used one agent at the user's request. They are not independent-agent validation.

## Centering requires actual spectral objects

Prove compact-resolvent inheritance on the actual restricted Hilbert space and a positive separation between the lowest two eigenvalues. Construct each ground projection separately. A vacuum trial vector supplies an upper energy bound, not an interacting ground identification. Preserve the common unbounded domain under bounded perturbations.

For a complementary perturbation, interpolate L_s=L_0+s lambda D with D=QDQ. If the complementary centered block is at least d>0 and the offdiagonal norm is b, ground leakage is at most ell=b/d. Differentiate the isolated eigenvalue: epsilon'_s=lambda<psi_s,D psi_s>. This term cancels the ground-ground contribution in the centered Duhamel identity.

Write F_s(t)=exp[-t(L_s-epsilon_s)]-G_s. Retain QF_s(0)J=-QG_sJ; it generally does not vanish. Combine its leakage bound with its excitation decay, rather than multiplying an unshifted heat bound by an uncontrolled centering exponential. Treat absolute, relative, ground-centered and real-time conclusions separately. Rational finite matrices test algebra and error enclosures, not full physical-space completeness.

## Close only the authorized loop

Recover the remote branch and source-bound progress ledger. A PR description or conversation report can lag the committed evidence. Name the active loop, its frozen contract and the explicit stop boundary before producing new results. Preserve previous contracts and gates. Publish a partial-cycle milestone honestly when the user requests a stop before the remaining loops.

If the user prohibits subagents, use one agent and mark both formulations and the critical review as correlated same-author work. Record an execution amendment without changing the mathematical contract. Never satisfy an independence field with a fictional reviewer. Do not weaken a required repository approval or access control; workflow attribution and server-enforced approval requirements are separate.

Make default reproduction select completed reviewed loops, not the entire future roadmap. Bind any solo amendment and required controls into admission. Execute negative controls for missing control names, false independence claims and coherently rebound manifests. Preserve inherited gates and rerun their producers.

Update the handoff with completed count, exact result scope, unresolved parent goals, next contract status and a clear no-execution boundary. Verify the final committed tree in a detached checkout, then confirm the published branch/merge tree and deployment separately. A repaired PR or successful build does not settle open physics.
