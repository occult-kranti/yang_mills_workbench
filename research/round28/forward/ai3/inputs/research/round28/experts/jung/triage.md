I inspected its setup and relative-fit validity theorems. Its coverage target under misspecification is a specified projection into a model class. Conditional validity relies on the stated testing assumptions; an approximate result concerns a near-projection set. Thus even a valid uncertainty statement does not certify that the selected class is the actual physical mechanism. ### AI3 and the subsequently selected AI4

The inherited candidate cancellation is
`m3,5-q*m3,4=2*q^13*(1-q^2)`.
This exact retained-model identity motivates analysis; it does not by itself bound the actual combined readout. Freeze the allowed q interval, measurement pair, nuisance class, physical clock, and entire error expression before comparing certificates.

For a combination `Y5-q*Y4`, separate errors permit only a conservative bound `epsilon5+abs(q)*epsilon4` until a joint cancellation is proved. Uncertainty in q adds a coefficient-error term. If q is itself unknown, a readout weighted by its true value is not automatically an implementable statistic: use declared candidate-indexed tests or a uniformly controlled estimate.

Controls should include q=1 as an algebraic limiting check, a near-endpoint regime, both signs of the odd contribution, a denominator approaching zero, and independent preparation/clock errors that destroy an assumed correlation. Cancellation of the cubic coefficient must not silently erase higher odd terms. If a ratio denominator is not bounded away from zero, return an inconclusive ratio certificate or use a frozen absolute observable.

Judge improvement using a complete actual-model error budget on the declared parameter set, with the original nine-setting outcome retained as context. A certificate that separates two candidates is not a continuous inverse. ## Guardrails against desired interpretations

Maintain a decision ledger recording the options available before each result, the selected statistic, and why the next loop changed direction. Preserve unsuccessful settings. Distinguish an algebraic identity, an upper certificate, a model-specific counterexample, and an actual measured effect. Independent implementations can expose mistakes but are not independent physical observations.

Do not add significance levels to deterministic arithmetic, infer probability from a visually striking integer, or hide a new model inside a rescaled clock. Any added parameter needs a role, units, range, and identification rule. Exploratory patterns can become proposals for later contracts; they cannot retroactively become predictions.
