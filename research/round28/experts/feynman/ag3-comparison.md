After the advisor opened exchange, I read the frozen forward report, skeptic's
independent derivation and additive passive comparator. Their mathematics agrees
with the reverse result. I found no blocking objection in these inspected
arguments. This is an additive review, not a new research loop or a modification
of any frozen AG3 evidence.

All three use the same complete actual AG2 indexed mixing B and preserve its
denominator `r=||B||_2`. They prove the bare local inverse and exterior identity,
sum the infinite indexed family in each finite cuboid's graph norm, and retain
the full `[X,D_next]` term. The exact remainder coefficients agree. Both root
placements, nonempty overlap, arbitrary generated support sizes and the complete
nested support loss are accounted for. The source-independent logarithm and
overlap refinement was shared by the advisor before production; agreement on
its constant is independent validation of a shared proposal, not separate
discovery of that proposal.

The exact coefficients are `3060/3623` on the main interval and `5949/96812`
on the narrow interval, multiplying the actual input r. Scalar and centered
diagonal costs agree, as do the reference-only lower bounds approximately
`.992789305211` and `.999589550670`. Forward states slightly stronger rounded
reference thresholds than the reverse headline; the underlying rational bounds
are the same. Reverse additionally bounds the retained diagonal's lower-weight
norm by `(3/4)^4 d`; forward and skeptic use the looser valid bound d. This
does not affect the shared relative-form result.

The passive comparator is essential to interpretation. Doing nothing already
gives `||B||_(3/2)<=81/256*r`. The main active coefficient is weaker than that
upper ceiling, so it alone certifies no improvement over passive lowering of
the weight. The narrow active coefficient beats that universal upper ceiling.
It still does not prove a comparison to the actual old lower-weight norm:
arbitrary support size m permits a passive ratio `(3/4)^m`, with no positive
uniform lower bound supplied here. Both the skeptic's class witnesses and the
reverse additive note make this distinction. The forward report includes the
qualification before its freeze; reverse and skeptic record its later arrival
without rewriting their earlier scientific bytes.

The final supported AG scope is therefore a complete actual-source map with
different input and output support weights, including all scalar and diagonal
updates. There is no proved actual improvement at a common norm, no unspent
reset of either weight margin, and no all-stage or original-Hamiltonian gap
theorem. The generated reference remains a separately controlled operator while
its mixing term is retained. These qualifications should accompany the public
numerical factor.
