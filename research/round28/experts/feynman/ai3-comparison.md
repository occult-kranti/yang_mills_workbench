## Proposed adaptive AI4 question, not selected or executed

Initially I proposed a smaller target: keep u=10^-24 and the admitted k=6
geometry, predeclare a shorter dimensionless duration such as z=10^-7, and
recompute every physical error before deciding whether the pairwise ratio
separates. This has a direct physical-budget rationale. In the inherited
bounds, shortening duration reduces the spatial contribution relative to the
leading signal while increasing the relative stationary-state cost. AI3
identifies room to investigate that tradeoff; it does not prove the new cell.

The advisor's written candidate instead proposes an infinite dyadic family
at z=10^-6 with k=7+j. After reading that proposal and the advisor's subsequent
message, I favor the combined prospective target:

    z=10^-7, u0=10^-24,
    u0 2^(-(j+1)) < u <= u0 2^(-j), j=0,1,...,
    k(j)=6+j,
    H1=(1-u,1/2), H2=(1-2u,1/16),
    common t=2z u^-3 hbar/alpha for each candidate pair.

This is a recommendation for the advisor's contract, not a new result or
parameter evaluation. It reuses admitted base-collar geometry and would ask
for a uniform family of pairwise discriminators rather than another isolated
successful fixture. It also gives the all-order retained numerator factor a
substantive purpose: an ordinary fixed absolute numerator Taylor budget cannot
certify separation proportional to arbitrarily small u. Keeping the ordinary
Taylor error in a uniformly positive denominator is a different issue and is
not intrinsically fatal.

Compared with one fixed cell, the uniform target adds four real obligations:

1. Prove a coordinate envelope for every complete-factor collar, including
   whole strip completion and positive-orthant boundaries, then derive a
   polynomial upper bound on every N_k. Counts through six do not prove it.
2. Bound the exact rational centers, denominator, signed retained deviation
   from candidate q, and state budget continuously throughout each band.
   No finite sampling can replace the continuous inequalities.
3. Prove the complete spatial and averaging bounds for all band indices by a
   genuine induction or closed envelope, retaining all ordered words and
   source/vacuum columns. Growing N_k must be charged in averaging.
4. Prove one strictly positive uniform separation coefficient proportional
   to u, or report the exact failed inequality of the frozen schedule. Any
   finite small/large-band controls or new direct collar enumerations must be
   declared in the contract before execution.

This added analytic work is bounded and more informative than a lone cell,
while a new joint physical-state cancellation theorem would be a substantially
different and less controlled project. No such cancellation should be assumed
to make this schedule pass. If the uniform target proves too ambitious, a
fixed-cell fallback must be explicitly frozen beforehand and reported as a
restricted outcome, not chosen after seeing results.

There is a precision distinction to preserve in the contract. Reverse AI3
rounds sqrt[b(q^2)/96] upward before multiplying by tau. A fixed absolute
rounding error at this inner square root contributes a physical state error
proportional to tau and therefore does not create a fixed physical floor as
u tends to zero. Rounding the final state radius with a fixed absolute
tolerance would create such a floor. Analytic rational state envelopes are a
clean option for the uniform proof; the placement and propagation of any
rounding must be explicit.
The advisor has preserved the earlier planning document and added a separate
precision correction, attributing this point to both this researcher and the
skeptic. That correction has been read and agrees with this assessment.

Success would remain a family of candidate comparisons at candidate-prescribed
common times, not an operational continuous inverse for unknown parameters,
a practical clock, physical phase calibration, homogeneous-model matching,
or a four-dimensional Yang-Mills result. Neither the smaller target nor any
band, new collar or later goal has been computed in this review.
