2021, internal date 30 August, 54 PDF pages. The
[ETH institutional record](https://www.research-collection.ethz.ch/items/142ea4e3-1538-4f05-8a97-5200a860d558)

1 January 2023, DOI [10.1016/j.jfa.2022.109734](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfa.2022.109734).
Publication metadata was checked; the final journal text was not compared with
v1. This is not a revalidation of the complete paper.

The relevant source statement permits separable, infinite-dimensional onsite
spaces and unbounded operators. Its input has a nonnegative onsite reference,
unique product vacuum, normalized gap at least one, fixed finite interaction
range and local relative-form bounds. Theorem 3.3 gives unique finite-volume
ground states and gap at least one half for a sufficiently small, volume-independent
coupling. Section 2's nearest-neighbor presentation is explicitly expository;
the initial statement allows fixed finite rectangular range. The norm in
(1.11) sandwiches a potential between local energy weights. The evolving
reference in (2.29) retains earlier diagonal interactions and its scalar
energy (2.32). Lemma 4.1 requires an existing local gap; Theorem 3.3 invokes
the intervening induction. No numerical workbench threshold was extracted.

Targeted passages read: §1.1–1.2 and Remark 1.1; §2 opening; (2.29)–(2.32);
