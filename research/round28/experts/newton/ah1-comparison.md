## Bounded AH2 recommendation; no selection or execution

I recommend making **delayed loading on the same continuous coupling and
preparation class** the primary AH2 target. AH1 now supplies the precise
matrix information that its deliberately coarse early bound does not use.
The target should keep this exact graph, all 561 retained functions, the
complete 29lambda outside envelope, both actual ground centers and the true
denominator. The acceptance question is whether a complete loaded-channel
estimate improves the admitted AH1 upper certificate. No old numerical
threshold should be stipulated as an inherited result.

The prospective block structure to verify in that contract is concrete.
Let N=P+−P0 and F=P0−|Omega><Omega|. Since every new state is even,
`N A+ N=K_N+29lambda N`, with K_N≥9/2. The exact coupling
`C=N A+ P0=−lambda N S F` kills Omega. The complete old-to-new Gram already
exported by forward and skeptic is `7 I+(1/4)J`, with bright eigenvalue
57/4. Thus the intended all-column coupling norm is
`||C||=sqrt(57)lambda/2`, to be independently reconstructed from the admitted
matrix. These are source premises for the future task, not a newly executed
AH2 result.

For `u(sigma)=exp[−sigma(A+−mu+)]x`, the exact new-channel equation starts
with Nu(0)=0 and has damping at least 9/2, since mu+≤29lambda. A useful
estimate must bound its driving face component through the full retained
evolution, including feedback. It must then integrate the complete BNu
contribution and add the distinct center defect. A separately proved late
spectral bound and a prospectively fixed join provide all-time coverage.
The historical AC2 forward spectral-loading and reverse face-forcing
arguments are two valid methodological leads, not constants to transfer.

Required risks/controls are clear: do not assume old inputs remain in P0;
do not drop either shared branch; do not substitute a single bright input
for the full face coupling; do not omit feedback or the scalar difference;
and do not infer a continuous/all-time result from sampled inputs. Any
stronger result still applies to this finite graph and declared initial

A **certified numerical heat evaluator at fixed lambda=1/100** is a useful
alternative if executable state vectors are the advisor's higher priority.

budget must remain separate and the numerical budget must be divided by the

In particular, exponentiating an uncertain center forever is inadmissible.
The AF2 late-projector method is a relevant source because it avoids that
