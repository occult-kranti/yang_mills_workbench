# Advice after the AJ2 final skeptical review — plans only

I favor the actual homogeneous Wilson quantitative-signal question proposed by
the Newton-method review. AJ2 closes qualitative nonvacuity for the actual
state; its strongest remaining local objection is that the proven variance
could be arbitrarily small without a quantitative property beyond normality.
The full model, original observable, selected-strip coefficients, physical
scales and boundary prescription already match AJ1. This makes a bounded
quantitative continuation more directly informative for that strand than a
return to a different model's readout problem.

The proposed first investigation should seek an explicit lower certificate
for the actual variance using AJ1's local reference-energy bound, with the
same complete two-factor region and all original endpoints. Declare an
omitted-coupling cap that remains inside the unevaluated stability interval.
A numerical auxiliary cap alone must never become a certified numerical
stability radius. The actual interacting mean must be retained throughout;
neither symmetry of product Haar nor its variance can be assigned to the
interacting density without proof.

I would keep two candidate proof routes open until the contract is frozen:
a controlled complete local energy cutoff, or an analytic concentration/energy
inequality in the original link variables. In the cutoff route, positivity in
a compressed matrix must pay for the omitted tail, cross terms and unknown
mean before it becomes an actual-state lower bound. An eigenvalue computed
in one convenient sector does not supply a bound on all mixed local densities
admitted by the premises. In the analytic route, the domain, measure and
normalizations of the proposed inequality must be proved. Either route can
honestly return a limited or insufficient certificate. A compactness proof
of an unspecified positive minimum would be an existential result, distinct
from a constructive numerical margin. No cutoff or constant has been computed
in this planning note.

Only after the first investigation is reviewed should the second be selected.
If it supplies a useful quantitative variance floor, I support asking for a
finite physical first energy moment of this same centered Wilson vector and
a bounded energy interval carrying a certified amount of its spectral mass.
The new work would need a form-domain argument for the actual smooth original
Wilson multiplier, the full finite-volume kinetic and interaction expression,
ground centering, and a justified passage to the source-defined infinite-volume
spectral measure. Boundedness of the observable is insufficient. Tested local
resolvent convergence must not be relabeled as a common-representation strong
limit. A moment upper bound may survive a limit even when equality requires
additional uniform integrability; the claimed direction must be checked.
An interval with overlap would give neither a threshold eigenatom nor an
identified lowest mass. An operator-domain or second-moment result would
require a separate obligation.

If the first investigation remains existential or its explicit certificate
fails, the second should resolve the particular cutoff or concentration
bottleneck exposed by review. It should not divide by an unproved variance
floor merely to keep the originally suggested sequence. This is the adaptive
choice the second loop needs to make.

The main alternative remains the Tesla-method fixed coarse-band canonical
readout proposal. It has a clear operational question: choose the clock and
complete collar from independently supplied prior information rather than
the exact unknown parameter. I agree that this would improve on AI4's
parameter-indexed settings. However, separation of paired candidates with the
same parameter still would not resolve competing hypotheses with independent
unknown parameters. A later composite decision rule must charge the complete
model error, timing, phase and source loading, and permit an unresolved set.
The canonical summable state supplies no direct quantitative bound on AJ2's
homogeneous Wilson vector. This makes the readout pair a useful later goal,
but my second choice for the two remaining authorized investigations.
