# Generated supports and complete iteration certificates

Use these lessons for overlapping local rotations, arbitrary-support homological maps and quantitative stability attempts. They were demonstrated in Yang–Mills Workbench Round22 O1/O2. They are proof and review rules, not a new general spectral-gap theorem.

## Preserve the operator domain before expanding

For an unbounded reference H0 and bounded local skew generators, prove domain preservation and the first bounded commutator on D(H0). Then integrate that commutator and expand only bounded operators. A finite physical volume can contain infinitely many indexed interaction words: prove absolute convergence in the graph estimates or group convergent terms by their finitely many support sets. Finite volume alone does not make the word expansion finite.

Preservation of D(H0) does not mean regularization of arbitrary exterior vectors. An infinite-volume local interaction estimate does not construct a bounded global perturbation or a global unitary in another representation.

## Declare the decomposition used by an interaction norm

State the root sum, exponential support weight, indexed multiplicity and each declared support. Keep all ordered nested words, repeat anchors and outer generators that meet only previously generated support. Count translations containing the root separately from relative words.

The same operator can have different interaction-norm budgets under different support declarations. Regroup on a smaller actual support only after proving that operator identity, and state the changed decomposition. A control that reports the regrouped value cannot be presented as the value for earlier pair-union declarations. In O2, independent chain-union enumeration exposed precisely this distinction; the qualitative inverse-cancellation conclusion survived, but its displayed constant required an explicit interpretation.

## Keep scalars, mixing and retained diagonal terms

For a bounded self-adjoint local residual R and rank-one vacuum projector P, write Q=I-P and c=<Omega,R Omega>. The exact split is

    R = c I + (Q R P + P R Q) + Q(R-c I)Q.

Adding c I and Q R Q would double-count c Q. Scalar centering alone does not remove vacuum mixing. An extensive scalar energy shift need not damage a gap, and a bounded local interaction norm is not a volume-independent global operator norm.

State whether the homological inverse uses bare H0 or an interacting diagonal reference. A bare inverse does not silently acquire the interacting gap or cancel commutators with retained D. Conversely, an interacting inverse needs its own local-gap, domain and relative-energy assumptions.

## Test every stage of the numerical claim

Keep the retained-D commutator in the complete recurrence. Generic bounds may produce a term linear in the residual, multiplied by a diagonal budget and divided by the available support-weight loss. If the chosen diagonal upper budget stays positive while summable losses tend to zero, a positive scalar majorant can shrink initially and still lose its series radius later.

Prove the all-step statement; do not extrapolate from an early decreasing prefix. Distinguish the exact logarithmic schedule from a conservative rational loss and its directed rounding enclosure. A numerical failure index belongs to the specified recurrence and rounding scheme.

Failure of a positive upper-budget recurrence is not a lower bound on the actual residual, failure of the algorithm, failure of every alternative estimate, or physical gap failure. A successful gap implication needs every radius condition, summable corrections, positive limiting weight, controlled relative diagonal growth and convergence of unitary products and inverses in the relevant graph norm. State the finite-volume conclusion separately from representation and continuum limits.

## Challenge the right operator class

An equal-weight generic commutator counterexample may exploit support growth that an excitation-energy inverse cancels. Check the actual homological map in the proposed example. O2 also supplied a different counterexample using onsite bare-homological generators and arbitrary many-site vacuum-diagonal interactions; even that stronger class statement does not show that those interactions occur along the actual SU2 trajectory.

Compare a source's exact inverse, norm, boundary, domain and induction premises before importing its threshold. Unevaluated constants or one visible proof step do not establish the full numerical iteration.

When review clarifies a frozen fixture, preserve the original submission and identify the post-freeze interpretation, independent check and affected claim. Do not attribute the clarification retroactively to two independent producers. Such reviews, controls and skill updates add zero research loops.
