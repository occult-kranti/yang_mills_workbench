# Lenses and evidence

Use selected primary materials rather than exhaustive-reading claims. These are discovery routes; record the actual passages read on each application.

| Lens | Useful operation | Required countercheck |
|---|---|---|
| Newton | Analyze effects into candidate causes, then synthesize predictions; record quantities and failures in laboratory notes. | An inverse may have a kernel. Theological or chymical terms need a declared dictionary and do not establish a modern field. |
| Tesla | Specify phases, circuit mechanism, source, load and receiver; compare full device to reduced mode. | Account for input energy, loss, omitted channels, physical clock and actual measurement resources. |
| Jung/Pauli | Record symbolic interpretation and selection of striking patterns; compare competing explanatory maps. | Freeze observations and selection rules, retain null outcomes and distinguish meaningful narrative from statistical or causal evidence. |
| Penrose | Translate geometry into algebra, inspect spin-network/representation structure and model dimension. | Check norm/Gram degeneracy and completeness; do not import 3D or self-dual results into full 4D quantum theory. |
| Feynman | Compute an observable in a specified model, build a simulator and seek a damaging counterexample. | Include everything that could invalidate the conclusion; simulations of a regulated system do not construct the continuum. |

Primary routes: [Newton Project](https://www.newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/), [Chymistry of Isaac Newton](https://newton.dlib.indiana.edu/), [Tesla Museum](https://tesla-museum.org/en/), [ETH Pauli archive](https://pauli-archive.ethz.ch/), [Feynman Caltech publications](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/), original Penrose papers and authenticated Jung editions. Catalog access alone is not reading.

Use the [Clay official problem statement](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/yangmills.pdf) when assessing Yang–Mills obligations. Distinguish fixed graph, thermodynamic limit at fixed spacing, matched continuum limit, required axioms, nontriviality and positive physical mass gap. Establish their relevant dependencies rather than assigning equal weights.

A government report containing a claim shows the claim entered that record; it does not show government experimental confirmation. A patent grant is not an efficiency test. An occult essay may suggest hidden-variable questions but must yield a specific competing observable map before it contributes to a physical model. Do not treat the absence of a missing document as evidence that it contained a breakthrough.
