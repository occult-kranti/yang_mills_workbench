# AI4 candidate for panel review: a uniform collar schedule

Planning only, after reading both frozen AI3 reports and the independent skeptic
derivation. AI3 admission is still under review. This document does not select
AI4, execute another collar, or establish any new numerical certificate.

AI3 identifies two different sufficient-bound obstructions. At u=10^-18,
the state bound alone overlaps; at u=10^-24,k=6, the spatial bound is too
large while the state-only interval separates. Merely refining the retained
arithmetic again would not address either physical bound.

One candidate is a uniform family rather than one more isolated grid point.
Keep z=10^-6, the same H1/H2 relation and the same physical clock within each
pair. Consider u0=10^-24 and dyadic bands

    u0 2^(-(j+1)) < u <= u0 2^(-j), j=0,1,...,
    k(j)=7+j.

These are proposed parameters, not accepted constants. The panel should compare
this target with a more economical fixed-parameter certificate or a justified
state-bound improvement before the advisor freezes anything.

The prospective route is to prove a geometric coordinate envelope for *every*
complete-factor collar, hence a polynomial upper bound on N_k. Retain all
ordered spatial words and both averaging columns. A dyadic induction could
then compare the spatial decay with the shrinking candidate separation, while
u^(3/2) state cost and u^3 times polynomial-collar averaging cost decrease
faster. The base inequality and every ratio in that induction need exact proof.
The historical k<=6 counts alone cannot establish the geometric envelope.

Use the cancellation-centered ratio enclosure with its full physical errors.
The AI3 all-order combination remainder is proportional to 1-q; an ordinary
fixed-degree individual arithmetic radius is not a suitable vanishing
numerator allowance uniformly as u tends to zero. Likewise a fixed absolute
square-root rounding tolerance would create an artificial floor. Analytic
relative envelopes or precision that scales with the band are required.

Prove a positive denominator, separate the known retained deviation from q,
and seek an explicit ratio separation proportional to u throughout the whole
family. Prove the continuous parameter bounds and unbounded band induction;
finite examples can test the implementation but cannot substitute for either.
Selected small and very large band indices would be useful controls only after
the contract freezes their list. Any new direct collar enumeration should also
be predeclared and bounded.

This target would still be a family of pairwise mathematical discriminators at
candidate-specified times. It would not construct a continuous inverse, infer
an unknown parameter using the true q as an instrument setting, demonstrate a
practical clock or apparatus, cancel unprovided measurement error, or match the
canonical model to homogeneous or continuum Yang–Mills. If the proposed
schedule fails a complete bound, report the exact obstruction rather than
altering the frozen schedule after seeing the result.
