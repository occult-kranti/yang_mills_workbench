## All-depth complete-factor geometry

A free reference factor is a singleton link. A strip based at (4a,2b,c)
contains six x links at x offsets 0,1,2 and y offsets 0,1, and four y links
at x offsets 0,1,2,3. Its vertices have x offsets 0..3 and y offsets 0..1.
Every link's full owner is determined by this periodic rule; z links and
the separator x/y links are free. Completing an owner from any of its links
can extend endpoint coordinates by at most (3,1,0). The checker verifies all
24 axis/residue classes; this is a periodic rule check, not a new collar.

If a face touches a current link, every vertex of that face lies within
one coordinate step of the current endpoint box. Completing all its owners
therefore extends that box by at most (4,2,1). The common seed has endpoints
in [0,4] x [0,2] x [0,1]. Induction from the actual collar growth rule proves

    endpoints(E(F_k)) subset [0,4+4k] x [0,2+2k] x [0,1+k].       (G1)

The lower boundary is clipped to zero: only nonnegative-anchor physical
faces occur, and strip bases are nonnegative. Clipping removes possibilities;
it cannot create links beyond the displayed upper envelope. This proves the
claim for all k, including all newly completed links. It does not extrapolate
the finite counts.

A retained face has all its complete owners in F_k, so all its vertices and
its anchor lie in this box. Counting three orientations at every possible
integer anchor, even when an orientation would leave the box, gives the
valid polynomial overcount

    N_k <= 3(4k+5)(2k+3)(k+2).                                  (G2)

The checker verifies (G1), complete strip closure and (G2) against every
admitted owner/link/face list at depths 0..6. No new collar beyond six is
enumerated. At k=6 the envelope is 10440 faces, whereas admitted N6=1808.
With k=6+j, bounding each positive affine factor separately gives

    N_(6+j) <= 10440(j+1)^3,   M_(6+j)=N_(6+j)/24 <= 435(j+1)^3. (G3)

The deliberately generous face envelope affects averaging and is fully
charged. It is never described as the actual face count at a new depth.

## Continuous parameter and stationary-state bounds

Write a=1-q, p(q)=2+5q+5q^2+6q^3+3q^4 and A(q)=(1+q)^2(1+q^2). The admitted

    b(q)=p(q)/[24a^3 A(q)], tau=eta/[8b(q)],
    v=z tau/[96 eta a^3]=z A(q)/[32p(q)].

For every contracted u and either candidate q>=99/100. Positive coefficients
give 21q^4<=p(q)<=21 and A(q)<=8. Evaluation of these rational bounds at
99/100 proves, continuously rather than by sampling,

    q^4 >= 9/10,  tau <= (3/2)eta a^3,
    z/100 <= v <= V:=z/64.                                     (P1)

For q>=99/100, all of 1+q,1+q^2,1+q^4 are >=19/10. Since

    a^3 b(q^2)/96
      =p(q^2)/[2304(1+q)^3(1+q^2)^2(1+q^4)]
      <=(21/2304)(10/19)^6 <=1/4096,

the positive square root satisfies sqrt[b(q^2)/96]<=a^(-3/2)/64. The complete
stationary-state cost is 48dhat=384 tau sqrt[b(q^2)/96]/(1-eta), including
the connected means. Hence

    state_H1 <=9u^(3/2),
    state_H2 <=(9/5)u^(3/2),                                  (P2)

where the latter uses 2sqrt(2)<=3 and eta/(1-eta)=1/15. These are analytic
upper envelopes of the original state term, not a replacement of the state
by a regional vacuum. Set s0=sqrt(u0)=10^-12; dividing (P2) by u gives bounds
9s0 and (9/5)s0 for the entire family. The code checks the exact squared
physical state expression against its analytic envelope at each fixture.

No numerical square root is required in this proof. A fixed absolute inner
rounding delta at sqrt[b(q^2)/96], if used, would add at most
384 tau delta/(1-eta). Since tau<=3u^3/4 on both candidates, that allowance
decays cubically. A fixed absolute addition to the final scalar radius is a

the two. No q=1 Hamiltonian or subtraction rounded to q=1 is used.
