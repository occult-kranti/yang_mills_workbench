# Averaged limits and useful relative-error repairs

## Transfer a local scalar limit without inventing a global generator

Keep the original physical observation time. A slow variable such as s=alpha tau t/hbar is a proof coordinate, and removing a known reference phase changes only the displayed scalar. Derive the actual propagator order, adjoints and phase sign before taking limits. Compare stationary states inside a bounded connected correlation, retaining both means; a small ground-energy error multiplied by a diverging time need not be small.

For finitely many full Haar links, prove compact resolvent from the electric Peter-Weyl spectrum and bounded reference potentials. Preserve the operator domain, gauge reduction and whole energy eigenspaces. Finite spatial support does not impose a spin cutoff. Compact resolvent of every finite region does not imply compact resolvent of the infinite reference.

For pure-point reference K and bounded self-adjoint A, define Abar as the strong direct sum of P_E A P_E. Prove its boundedness, domain preservation and gauge properties. Analyze the primitive of exp(isK/tau) A exp(-isK/tau)-Abar on fixed vectors. Use orthogonal energy components, a uniform bound and density; integration by parts should apply the small primitive to compact averaged orbits. Prove adjoint convergence too. Keep a fixed-energy-column estimate separate from a global norm estimate. A positive ground gap does not separate all excited Bohr frequencies. Conversely, inspect the actual source: a special source can have a uniformly small norm primitive even when a generic theorem supplies only strong convergence.

Retain the entire degenerate energy block. A known pair of resonant states does not prove closure. Distinguish an actual unaveraged leakage calculation, an abstract counterexample and a calculation of the actual averaged block.

If regional scalar limits f_k exist and |F_q-F_(q,k)|<=tail_k+state_error_(q,k), prove that tail_k tends to zero uniformly in q and each state error tends to zero at fixed k. Compare two regional limits through the same F_q, prove their Cauchy property, and choose k before taking q to its endpoint. Do not substitute pointwise spatial convergence or claim a joint regulator rate. Separate an actual scalar-limit theorem and a certified disk from numerical block assembly, a trajectory or a global limiting Hamiltonian.

## Repair a relative bound with an actual input class

Define the denominator explicitly for the same input. For own-ground-centered heat operators E and E_R, retain the generator defect QLP+(mu-epsilon)P. Vacuum cancellation QLP Omega=0 does not cancel the centering term or later generated leakage.

Compute the whole retained-to-omitted map when extending a ground-residual certificate to preparation errors. Prove channel completeness and its Gram matrix, including shared-link components. A small residual on one vector does not bound the map on every retained vector.

Derive a genuine early-time Duhamel bound using the actual evolving retained input and full exterior propagator. Independently derive a late-time spectral estimate and a true-output denominator lower bound. If the early majorant increases and the late one decreases, a fixed joining time gives an all-time bound through their maximum at the join. Prove coverage over the continuous coupling interval; a grid of times or couplings is not that proof.

Label vector-norm preparation balls, normalization, phase alignment and Ritz-overlap floors separately. Charge uncertainty in the computed Ritz vector, inner product and preparation before accepting a floor. Comparing both evolutions on the same prepared input differs from comparing with an ideal input, which needs an additional preparation-bias term. A failed sufficient floor is not proof the actual denominator vanishes. Preserve exact time-zero and zero-coupling identities and existing excluded-input obstructions.

Keep the ground coefficient exactly one in a numerical spectral representation. Bound excited-gap and projector errors uniformly in time and handle the unbounded exponential tail. Separate physical truncation, representation, scalar evaluation and physical-input uncertainty. None of these diagnostics is an added action parameter or an arbitrary denominator regularizer.
