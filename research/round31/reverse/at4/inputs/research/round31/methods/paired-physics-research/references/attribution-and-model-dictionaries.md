# Attribution and complete model dictionaries

When an author requests personal names for project results, introduce stable
aliases linked to legacy IDs. Keep known theorem names, external authors,
mathematical symbols and frozen source evidence intact. Give the human author
the requested byline, disclose AI assistance, and preserve the distinction
between model-agent review and external human peer review. Catalog entries,
equations and parameter aliases have different grains: do not add them into a
discovery count or rank them as percentages of an open problem solved.

Compare a proposed new result to the closest checked primary source before
writing a novelty headline. A similar mechanism in another gauge group or
reference model neither proves copying nor establishes priority. Describe the
specific difference, reading depth and unverified priority. Rewrite current
presentation through aliases; never silently rewrite an admitted historical
gate to change its author, theorem name or hypotheses.

For a lattice-to-physical map enumerate every electric link, selected and
omitted magnetic class, additive scalar, actual ground subtraction, boundary
rule and physical clock. An interior interaction dictionary does not identify
finite Hamiltonians with different boundary restrictions or their limiting
states. A common positive energy multiplier preserves dimensionless coupling
ratios; at a fixed energy reference and hbar it changes physical gaps and the
clock. Independent suppression of magnetic coefficients is an action change
unless a separate equivalence theorem proves otherwise. Failure of a sufficient
gap certificate is not a no-gap theorem.

Before transferring an explicit stability constant, compare the reference
vacuum, on-site excitation operator, support cardinality, gauge group/Casimir,
trace convention, indexed interaction norm and all induction/resolvent bounds.
Keep active fine links, fine outgoing-link blocks and coarse factors distinct.
A loop occupying one coarse factor can still contain four active fine links;
this alone does not refute an energy lower bound. In the I1 grouping each
four-site star has norm 7|tau| and an interior site belongs to four anchors,
so the indexed site budget is 28|tau|. Changing to individual faces changes the
decomposition and requires its own complete norm calculation.

When rederiving a source majorant, freeze its proposed constants before
production, prove the combinatorics and complete fixed-point and excited-sector
arguments, retain all scalar/diagonal terms, and justify untruncated domains.
An exact numerical substitution into an unproved recurrence proves no radius.
An alternative finite-volume certificate does not automatically evaluate an
older theorem's named constants or identify an infinite-volume state.

These lessons record the reviewed Round29 AL1/AL2/AM1 scope. They supply no
unexecuted-loop result or continuum premise.
