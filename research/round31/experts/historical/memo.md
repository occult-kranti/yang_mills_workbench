## Modern technical triage

**HTW is relevant but is not yet the desired numerical certificate.** Henheik–Teufel–Wessel, arXiv:2106.13780v3, Theorem 3 permits possibly infinite-dimensional on-site spaces and supplies exponential local stability under an existential smallness threshold. Theorem 3's constants `c,c1,c2` and its boundary perturbation assumptions must be matched explicitly. The AQ numerical gap threshold does not by itself evaluate those constants. The useful AR contract is to produce a verified inequality with explicit constants for the actual whole-star model, or isolate this unavailable threshold. The theorem's established boundary-independence conclusion must retain its own hypotheses and attribution.

**Contemporary truncation estimates suggest a route, not an AQ enclosure.** Ciavarella et al., arXiv:2508.00061v2, Sections 2.1 and 4, analyze leakage through the electric-basis cutoff and obtain leading perturbative suppression. The inspected derivation starts from a single-mode nearest-step structure; its applications and numerical agreement do not furnish an off-the-shelf rigorous remainder for this project's three-dimensional SU(2) state. A proposed adaptation needs all coupled representation branches, complete-factor support and state replacement. Successive-cutoff agreement is a diagnostic, not a proved error bound.

The useful controls are: `tau=0` with the **actual selected strip vacuum**, the entirely free corner only as a separately labeled normalization fixture, `tau` of both signs, an incorrect uncentered sample, missing whole stars, a changed physical clock, and a truncation with nonzero omitted coupling.
