Test a physical vacuum-plus-character superposition: free energy can be quadratic while the perturbation is linear. ## Finite spectral improvement

## Conditional common variables

A rank-three invariant projector requires the inverse Gram matrix, including offdiagonal coefficients. Rank and scalar positivity do not replace the full tensor.

For x_i=q·a_i the action variable b=Σκ_i a_i is useful, but a joint observable retains the relative a_i or equivalent tensor data. Rotate the observable with the action when aligning b; at b=0 do not normalize b. Test two actual boundary assignments with equal b and partition function but different joint insertions. A cache keyed only by b is not a valid observable cache. At nonzero coupling use weighted moments; the Haar projector alone is the zero-coupling contraction. Boundary-independent terms cancel in a normalized central conditional expectation but still affect the surrounding marginal and full bulk integral.
