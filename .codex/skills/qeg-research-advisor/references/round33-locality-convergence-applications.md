# Round33 lessons: locality, convergence of named constructions, certificates for the limit, applications

Round33 (BA1-BD2, eight investigations, all accepted within scope) turned the Round32 subsequential statements into statements about one limit of two named box constructions and applied the admitted equations to related problems. These notes are method lessons for the next cycle; the gates in `research/round33/advisor/` are the only authority for any number.

## Chain that worked

1. Coefficient locality first (BA1): bound the change of the creation coefficients by a boundary change at a rate q^(N-1), by two genuinely different routes (a weighted anchored norm; analyticity on a complex coupling disc plus Cauchy's estimate). Coefficient decay is not state decay.
2. Dynamics comparison on a compact window (BA2): Duhamel over the boundary source with a committed Lieb-Robinson excerpt; the rate in N is only O(1/N) with a polynomial decay function.
3. Marginal locality of reduced densities (BB1): an every-site form of the coefficient input is needed (the gate may admit only u in R); charge normalization of intermediate regions explicitly (vacuum probabilities, or orthogonality at the changed support); per-site charging of support sizes, never products of growing shells.
4. Whole-sequence convergence (BB2): run it in parallel with its input by freezing the input's targets as explicit monotone hypotheses and discharging them item by item against the input gate (comparison x form x cutoff regime x sign) in the review; direct comparison of two boxes gives factor 1, telescoping gives 1/(1-q).
5. Certificates for the limit (BC1): identification with every subsequential limit comes before any inheritance; a restatement copies gate rationals exactly; a finite-box statement that the record proves only for limits stays an obligation.

## Traps met and how they were caught

- A rate claim without its range: the correlation bound was O(1/N) only on 5<=N<=14000 and vacuous from N=14419 because a frozen region factor e^{|Y|/10^8} with |Y| ~ N^3 eventually wins; the reverse producer caught it. Every rate claim carries its N-range in the same clause.
- An external theorem cited without a committed excerpt (Kotecky-Preiss): repair by committing the excerpt and a post-hoc comparison record; next time declare the excerpt as a premise at freeze.
- Obligation wording: "uniqueness of the limit" closed as a whole would have implied uniqueness of ground states; split into "all subsequential limits of the named constructions coincide" (closed) and "uniqueness of any ground state" (open). The phrase scanner's vocabulary did not contain the phrase, and a "heading: list" pattern severs exclusion cues from list items: reviewed wording, not the scanner, was the guard.
- Silent route-A constants in a route-B packet: the admitted checkers hard-code the other model's numbers; the skeptic's source-edit substitutions (22, all aborting) are the defence.
- Single-route loops confirm arithmetic and code when the skeptic follows the same frozen formula chain; say so, never call it a second method.
- Transfer across gauge groups needs criteria, not analogy: a central element acting as -1 gives the link flip, a vanishing third Haar moment gives first-order parity; the two columns differ (SU(5) has parity but not the flip; its obstruction is first visible at fourth order). SU(2) constants and dictionaries are never transferred.
