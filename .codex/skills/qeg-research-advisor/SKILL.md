# Quantum fields and gravity research advisor

For driven truncation, growing-volume or hierarchy-closure work, read [the dynamic/volume/closure contract](references/dynamic-volume-closure-contract.md). It records the distinction between exact Galerkin error and computed-state error, common-action variables, volume-deterioration counterbenchmarks and source-specific closure tests.

For the coupled Yang–Mills branch, read [the two-plaquette contract](references/two-plaquette-contract.md) after the existing Yang–Mills contract. It records shared-link derivatives, quotient measure/domain, exact degree-tail control, common/difference coefficients and the finite-graph proof boundary.

Read [the advisor protocol](references/advisor-pipeline.md) when planning a new research cycle or changing the panel. Read the current experiment contract and saved evidence before accepting a result; historical prose is not authoritative over the source/data it describes.

## Establish the physical contract

Specify action, units, metric signature, charge convention, quantum state and preparation, supporting sources, approximation order, regulator, boundaries and observable. Distinguish mathematical consistency, implementation correctness and physical applicability. The four original research fronts use different geometries; a homogeneous response does not close a charged horizon or a transverse mixing calculation.

When a contract changes, name the change and rerun the affected physical checks. Do not silently rename a prescribed-field calculation as backreaction, a tangent sensitivity as quantum noise, a local effective action as a causal current, or a finite grid as the continuum.

## Assign bounded work

Use proposer/theorist, implementer and independent verifier roles when they can do useful independent work. Give each its inputs, owned output files, forbidden shared edits, acceptance tests and stop condition. A site integration remains owned by its parent agent under the Sites workflow. Stop assigning work to a temporary reviewer after its handoff has been integrated. Completed workers need no artificial ongoing status.

An implementer cannot certify its own evaluator by agreement. The verifier should use an independent formulation or analytic reference, and at least one discriminating wrong-model control when a subtle physical inference is at stake. Do not hide failed runs or repeatedly scan unrelated parameters after the concrete acceptance question is answered.

## Review evidence

Check file hashes and actual code against every claimed fix. The previous report claimed per-mode current subtraction, but its delivered source still added separate totals. Keep that historical evidence and label a new source revision explicitly.

Demand computed, finite diagnostics rather than allocated zeros. Verify each reported residual is populated from the state or an independently integrated quantity. A successful process exit is not an accuracy gate.

Separate ODE tolerance, momentum nodes, momentum window, Landau cutoff, temporal boundaries and state preparation. Do not estimate response gain by dividing through a vanishing field or numerical residual; use a declared global physical scale and return undefined where that scale is zero.

## Decide and communicate

Accept only the narrowest claim supported by the contract and evidence. Record failed, superseded, unresolved and passed gates separately. Source reading, numerical replication and independent physical validation are distinct statuses. A paper, government project, patent or agent ranking is not authority to skip its assumptions.

Provide auditable derivations, methods, decisions and results. Do not request or manufacture private hidden reasoning. The full Einstein–QED problem remains open until the common covariant current/stress closure, fluctuation validity and geometry-matched evolution are actually established.

## Lessons from the skeptical regulator review

Maintain three separate questions: what is proved for exact finite coefficients, what the numerical implementation verifies on its tested domain, and what is required to pass to a physical continuum theory. A coefficient bound uniform in Landau cutoff at fixed momentum window does not establish current, state, energy or stress convergence. State whether a limit is fixed-window, iterated or genuinely cofinal. Prove any interchange or cofinal claim; a plot cannot supply it.

Treat a successful counterexample as a reason to change the hypothesis. Do not frame the panel's objective as defeating the skeptic. The finite-window integral maximum does not transfer to arbitrary positive quadrature; retain the peak-aligned two-node falsifier. The fixed-matching joint-cutoff obstruction rejects uniform positivity of the separated coefficient, not existence of a consistently combined renormalized current.

Do not overcorrect a claim while making it modest: the finite source tangent is a projection of the finite-model retarded current response with contact terms. It does not reconstruct a full spacetime kernel or symmetrized quantum noise. A stationary-vacuum second-variation bound controls its specified transformed variables, not every raw tangent variable or a pumped trajectory.

Each published claim needs its exact assumptions, complete argument, strongest reviewed objection, affected source/data versions, and next missing premise. Match examples to actually executed fixtures. Distinguish project derivations from established literature novelty. Review the website copy against the frozen scientific verdicts before promotion.

## Variable and closure extensions

Use the [round7 variable contract](references/round7-variable-contract.md) for the scalar branches. Classify a new quantity before using it: regulator label, RG scale, matched coefficient, prescribed driver, physical field or calculated observable. A physical scalar requires a specified action, force, energy and stress. A time-dependent external coefficient requires support work.

Do not reject valid reference-subtraction algebra because physical matching remains incomplete. Its regulator-dependent coefficient changes the fixed-coefficient premise. Conversely, a bounded scalar on a common compact domain cannot cancel the stated cofinal coefficient divergence. Distinguish this separated-coefficient claim from the combined renormalized current.

A finite fixed-B hybrid action and a classical scalar-gravity action remain separate models. Unit conversion does not identify their different gauge functions or supply common quantum stress. Require current, energy and both directional pressures from one state and one causal renormalization prescription before merging. Do not reconstruct a pressure from the conservation identity being tested.

If an agent review ends before completion, preserve delivered notes and distinguish completed equation review from unfinished code review. Root validation may finish the task; it must not be attributed to the interrupted agent.

## Spectral and Millennium connections

For exceptions, compact hierarchy closure, local dynamics and restricted uniform-gap arguments, apply [the round13 exception contract](references/exceptions-locality-moments.md). It records exact witness bounds, scalar coupling response, iterator geometry, source-theorem applicability and frozen evaluator inputs.

For nonabelian work, read [the Yang–Mills contract](references/yang-mills-contract.md). It records the finite-Haar hierarchy, transfer-operator normalization, operator-norm and physical-time obligations. A finite selected set of identities cannot be substituted for the complete smooth-test-function family.

Read [the spectral connection contract](references/spectral-connection.md) when connecting the finite QED work to a mass-gap claim. Resolve the exact gauge group, dimension, physical spectrum and regulator limits before accepting a bridge. The Yang–Mills prize is a separate pure nonabelian theory, not a renamed Einstein–QED completion.

A fitted effective-mass plateau cannot certify a spectral lower bound. For a positive zero-temperature spectral measure the exact effective mass bounds the lowest overlapping energy from above; a missing channel or tiny overlap can hide lighter states. Require fixed physical units, full reconstruction assumptions and a common decay rate on a dense physical-state family before admitting a Hamiltonian gap implication.
